4. Every editable Settings change is for the current Project. Do not expose Global/Project/Goal/Host editing scopes or inheritance controls.

7. Existing concept pages are frozen. Shared code may be changed only additively and with proof that the original concepts did not regress.

Everything else in the included base and correction packets remains in force unless this packet explicitly supersedes it.

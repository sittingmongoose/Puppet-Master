L00000540	#### Persistence and recovery guarantees
L00000541	Terminal persistence uses three guarantee tiers:
L00000542	
L00000543	| Tier | What belongs here | Rules |
L00000544	|---|---|---|
L00000545	| `guaranteed_durable` | section/tab/pane layout, titles, pin state, selected tab or pane, terminal-session and dev-session bindings, restore banners, durable settings refs | MUST restore deterministically after restart or project reopen |
L00000546	| `best_effort_durable` | bounded transcript snapshots, command-block metadata, shell-integration hints, cwd snapshots, exit metadata, derived Output or Ports linkage | MAY be pruned or absent after crashes or storage pressure, but absence must be disclosed |

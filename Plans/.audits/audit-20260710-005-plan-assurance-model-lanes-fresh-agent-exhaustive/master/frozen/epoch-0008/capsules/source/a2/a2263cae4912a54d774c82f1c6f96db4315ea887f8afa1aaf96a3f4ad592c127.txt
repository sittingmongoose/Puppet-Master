L00003263	
L00003264	**For `merged_result` target (default):**
L00003265	
L00003266	| Strategy | Steps |
L00003267	|----------|-------|
L00003268	| **Squash** | Fetch → checkout target → `git merge --squash {branch}` → **run tests** (staged, uncommitted) → pass: `git commit` / fail: `git reset --hard HEAD` |

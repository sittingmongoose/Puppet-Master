## Additive Correction v4 — Wonderer Convergence Boundary (2026-09-03)

This section applies `PM_Assistant_v2_Additive_Correction_v4` (`WONV-001..004`, `WONV-006..007`)
to this owner. Wonderer's status as a built-in Puppet Master Persona, selectable in the Persona
picker alongside Product Manager, Architect, Implementer, Reviewer, and Teacher, is unchanged.

### WONV-001 — A Persona plus a Skill, not profile infrastructure

Wonderer is a built-in Puppet Master **Persona** paired with the Wonderer methodology **Skill**
owned by `Plans/Skills_System.md`. It is not Hermes profile infrastructure: no profile home, no
profile config switcher, and no profile runtime is implemented or imported.

### WONV-002 — Leads, not conclusions

Wonderer explores tethered adjacent possibilities and produces **leads**. A lead explains its
connection back to the seed, so lateral exploration stays tethered and does not drift. A lead is
not a final answer and is not an evidence claim; Wonderer does not conclude.

### WONV-003..004 — Never votes, delivers passes

An active Wonderer never votes in a BrainStorm: it abstains and is excluded from the quorum and
majority denominators. A surface may show `Abstained` without depressing the support percentage,
and abstention is never counted as opposition.

Wonderer takes no debate turn and never argues for or against a proposal (DL-218). It delivers
its passes and answers questions put to it about its leads, and its passes stay part of synthesis
evidence; it is not removed from the discussion because it cannot vote.

### WONV-006..007 — Role changes and additivity

If the user reconfigures a slot from Wonderer to an ordinary voting role, only the new role's
current attempt may vote. The earlier Wonderer attempt remains abstaining history and is never
retroactively converted into a vote.

Wonderer stays additive in BrainStorm, Crew, Chat Room, PRD Builder, and Planning Wizard and
never replaces a required core participant. Where concurrency is lower than the logical roster,
the roster runs in waves rather than dropping a core role to fit a cap.

# Combined Workflow v8 source contracts (all_writers.v8)

EP-125 to EP-127, GRS-086 to GRS-089, CV-354, SP-321, SP-322, ATS-059, ATS-060 and BRS-031 own this complete bounded source for `pm.executor.workflow_source.all_writers.v8` and its producer component `pm.goal_run_certified.producer_source.v3`. The files under `Plans/workflow_combined_source_contracts/` and `Plans/workflow_standard_source_contracts/native-v8/` are normative together. The source is not installed: native capability authentication, installation, execution, codec, transaction, durability and recovery evidence and all schema instances remain NOT_RUN. No WorkNode or NodeSeed is created.

## Placement

This directory holds the combined profile: the root and Stop protocols (`protocol.md`, `stop-protocol.md`), the combined roots in `schemas/`, the root maps, the binding companions and the descriptors. Component subdirectories hold the certified coordinator v2 successors (`coordinator/`), the producer v3 component (`producer/`) and the Replan source (`replan/`). The native successor maps, protocol and nine native successor schemas are in `Plans/workflow_standard_source_contracts/native-v8/`, beside `native-v7/`, whose scope sentence they do not widen. The v7 contract directories stay untouched.

The 59 source files were copied from the external combined source edition and edited only by relocation, by the identifier rename of `rename-map.json`, and by the listed repairs (P-01 stored release token, P-04 slot-origin header, P-16 superseded Stop text, the D-1 helper inlining, the U3-Q13 consumer reference, the six unreferenced legacy wrappers deleted, the stale-string repairs (the route rule and codec strings, stale identity names in operative prose, and the package-only label for the condition that puts a run into `blocked`), the stale-status repair of the placed physical map, the O-01 static profile domain, the P-06 status mark and four follow-on pins). `canonical-inverse.json` records every delta pointer by pointer with its class; applying it to a placed file gives back its source member byte for byte. `superseded-lineage.json` lists the superseded Stop text S1-S15 with their dispositions and the zero-selection census, and records R10's precedence sentence and the Stop-successor prefaces of the five placed protocols (S16-S20) as lineage prose that is not a precedence rule.

Package-relative mentions kept as authored in placed text resolve only through `composition.json` `component_path_scopes`: exact mention-to-target aliases, the routes of `original-reference-routing.json`, or the external source member. Never resolve a mention against the shared checkout, guess a missing prefix or substitute the newest same-ID resource.

## What stays open

The four D06 statements hold: the D06 argument grammar is carried; its issuer `owner.executor.native.record_cancellation.v1` is an unbound dependency; nothing here activates it by implication; the Stop route rests on `owner.executor.native.revoke_run_execution.v1`. The seven positive-route obligations are source obligations with instances and native execution NOT_RUN; TRUTHFUL-UNAVAILABLE is compiled as a stated rule only. The released-idle readbacks presuppose a later release to idle whose transition from `revoked` the source does not state; they remain source obligations, NOT_RUN. The consumers, projector and checkpoint are A2's; the `goal_run.replanned` Event contract, registry row and payload successor are A3's. Replan release stays unavailable until A2 supplies the projection admission. The inherited `required_child_goal_run_ids` reading under Goal V2 is an open owner question.

At an idle slot, `owner.executor.workflow_source.record_stop.v1` is the ordinary original Stop route (stop-protocol.md R02, first row). Its `StopResult` needs an original native cancel and `SchedulerStop` that this edition does not supply, so it returns `unavailable`. No slot revision, limited revocation or R04 invalidation is published at idle, and a current Goal Stop denies every method through `RunRevocationCurrent` `goal_execution_revoked` (R06). The source names no caller of `record_stop` after the Goal Stop latch (GRS-078 step 3); that remains an Executor and Goal owner question. A Stop that revokes a certified slot at `appended_held` leaves the slot revoked with a durable held `goal_run.certified` Event: `commit_native` admits only `certified:appended_held`, recovery issues no second append or native certification and cannot release a partly verified operation, and release admits only `certified:completed`. Under the stated transitions the slot does not return to idle, and how readers treat that held Event is open for A2 and A3.

Replan `apply_native` advances the checked u32 run generation via the original graph-lock owner (`replan/protocol.md`) and consumes a genuinely issued graph-lock capture and origin. The only graph-lock issuer, `owner.executor.native.apply_graph_lock.v1`, remains a complete dependency contract only (EP-117), and no v8 method map binds it. This edition activates nothing. Unless an owner unit states that the `apply_native` transaction itself performs the lock as that owner, genuine Replan native application is unavailable until the graph-lock owner's participation is separately established.

The source's replanned payload envelope requires `expected_goal_revision`, allows `parent_goal_id`, and puts `expected_goal_revision` in its v3 idempotency key (`replan/protocol.md`). The goal_run v3 envelope that `Plans/Goal_Runtime_System.md` records for started, cancelled and certified requires `expected_goal_run_revision`, `goal_run_revision` and `idempotency_key` and has no `expected_goal_revision` or `parent_goal_id`. This edition does not reconcile them; A3 decides the `goal_run.replanned` v3 envelope.

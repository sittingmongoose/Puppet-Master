L005001: - Recommended meanings:
L005002:   - `recover`
L005003:     - attempt to restore or reconcile a suspect/orphaned/conflicted worktree into a safe known state
L005004:   - `archive`
L005005:     - retire from active use while preserving enough identity/metadata/lineage for historical browsing
L005006:   - `prune`
L005007:     - cleanup-oriented action for items already policy-eligible; narrower than generic remove and typically scoped to obviously stale/orphaned/live-no-longer-needed worktrees
L005008:   - `remove`
L005009:     - explicit destructive removal of live worktree backing after confirmation and eligibility checks
L005010: - The historical lane/worktree record must survive `archive`, `prune`, and `remove`.
L005011:
L005012: ### Eligibility / gating direction
L005013: - Cleanup must be gated by more than age.
L005014: - Recommended checks before archive/prune/remove:
L005015:   - no active-run ownership
L005016:   - no unresolved blocked recovery requiring that exact worktree
L005017:   - no required safe-point restore targeting that worktree/baseline
L005018:   - no unresolved conflict workflow the user still needs to inspect
L005019:   - no newer lineage operation depending on that lane/worktree still being live
L005020: - If any of those hold, the object should remain `retained`, `suspect`, or `restoring`, not `cleanup_eligible`.
L005021:
L005022: ### Trigger direction
L005023: - Good candidate transitions into `cleanup_eligible`:
L005024:   - lane/package complete and no retention reason remains
L005025:   - superseded by graph patch and no unresolved recovery depends on it
L005026:   - revoked/reopened flow leaves an old lane/worktree no longer needed live
L005027:   - recovery completed and old broken backing is no longer needed
L005028: - Good candidate transitions into `retained` instead of immediate cleanup:
L005029:   - recent completion pending review/promotion
L005030:   - weak integration still under investigation
L005031:   - unresolved concern or corroboration tied to outputs from that lane
L005032:   - manual operator retention
L005033:
L005034: ### Source Control vs Orchestrator boundary
L005035: - This seam reinforces the earlier boundary:
L005036:   - Orchestrator owns lane-pool truth and cleanup posture in execution context
L005037:   - Source Control owns concrete worktree actions and compact inventory display
L005038: - Because Source Control is narrow, it should default to compact current/live worktree rows with filters/toggles for:
L005039:   - retained
L005040:   - cleanup-eligible
L005041:   - archived/removed history
L005042: - Orchestrator can carry the richer historical lineage and policy explanations.
L005043:
L005044: ### Historical-lineage rule
L005045: - Strong recommendation:
L005046:   - live cleanup must never erase run/lane/worktree lineage from `History`, `Ledger`, or graph-linked inspection
L005047: - After live removal:
L005048:   - Orchestrator still shows the historical lane/worktree identity
L005049:   - Source Control may show it only in filtered historical mode or via lineage/detail, not necessarily in the default narrow current list
L005050:
L005051: ### Confirmation / undo implication
L005052: - This seam connects directly to the earlier confirmation policy:
L005053:   - archive may be `light` or `strong` depending on state
L005054:   - prune/remove should usually be `strong`
L005055:   - remove on a worktree with blocked/recovery lineage may approach `hard_gate`
L005056: - Most of these are not true undo operations.
L005057: - The clean recovery path is usually compensating action or restore via preserved metadata, not “Undo remove.”
L005058:
L005059: ### Contradictions / gaps surfaced
L005060: - Current docs expose worktree actions and blocked classifications, but they do not yet define the full lane/worktree cleanup lifecycle.
L005061: - `historical`, `archived`, `removed`, `retained`, and `cleanup_eligible` still need sharper cross-surface meanings.
L005062: - Without a split model, the UI risks either:
L005063:   - deleting useful historical truth
L005064:   - or keeping too much live clutter forever
L005065:
L005066: ### Candidate fixes to carry forward
L005067: - Define distinct lane and worktree lifecycle states, with explicit mapping but no forced identity collapse.
L005068: - Make cleanup eligibility depend on runtime/recovery/lineage checks, not only simple completion.
L005069: - Preserve historical lane/worktree records after archive/prune/remove.
L005070: - Keep Source Control worktree-first and compact, with historical/retained material behind filters or lineage views.
L005071:
L005072: ### Do-not-forget details
L005073: - active-run ownership must be visible before destructive worktree actions
L005074: - safe-point and blocked recovery lineage can keep an old worktree relevant even after supersession
L005075: - cleanup should reduce live clutter without erasing the historical object model

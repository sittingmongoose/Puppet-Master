FINDINGS

**FIDELITY-01**
- **severity:** blocker
- **where:** `Plans/Tools.md#3.5D Web operation family runtime contract`
- **ledger obligation that did not fully survive:** Parts R-S carried implementation-ready per-tool contracts for `webfetch`, `webextract`, `webresearch`, `webcrawl`, and `webmap`, including concrete request fields, locked defaults, output fields, validation behavior, and tool-specific error/application rules.

- **what kind of recovery is needed:** packetize fuller section content

The detailed provider-specific canon remains ledger-only.

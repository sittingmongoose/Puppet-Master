## Design Context

### Users
This guidance is scoped to `Concepts/ConceptHub` and concept-review tooling. The primary user is Jared, often reviewing many model-generated Puppet Master concepts side by side on a Mac and occasionally from another device on the local network. The job is to compare visual directions, stress responsive spacing, preserve each prototype's interactive controls, and identify the strongest design without managing many servers or tabs.

### Brand Personality
Precise, calm, and creatively technical. The interface should feel like a purpose-built Puppet Master review room: capable and information-rich, but never noisy or generic.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use PMConcept7 as the visual baseline and the Qwen Usage gallery as the interaction reference. Default to Friendly Dark, support all eight PM themes, and use compact native-feeling chrome, clear model ownership, tinted surfaces, restrained depth, and crisp SVG iconography. Avoid generic admin-dashboard styling, decorative gradients, emoji, oversized cards, and effects that compete with the concepts being reviewed.

### Design Principles
1. The concepts are the visual focus; Hub chrome should organize rather than compete.
2. Preserve live prototype state while resizing, filtering, sorting, and focusing.
3. Make model ownership unmistakable at every level.
4. Treat responsive width, reduced motion, and all eight themes as first-class review tools.
5. Prefer direct, compact controls and honest capability labels over simulated support.

## Scope and fixed profile

1. The restricted lane is Puppet Master-initiated automation. Ordinary direct Assistant Chat and Chat-originated delegation are excluded. A controller-issued origin determines the lane; unknown, unregistered, ambiguous, wildcarded, or caller-self-asserted origin fails closed. Sources: `records/corrections.jsonl:7,9`; `records/decisions.jsonl:14,18,30,31`; `records/design_atoms.jsonl:39-40`.
2. One unchanged profile is designed for relative-low routes and applies to every subject: one objective, one acceptance group, bounded evidence, stable requirement IDs, explicit output fields, and no broad/global completion judgment. It has no model-specific, task-specific, dynamic, or user-adjustable profile and no required execution forecasts. Sources: `records/corrections.jsonl:5-6`; `records/design_atoms.jsonl:4,13,16`; `records/decisions.jsonl:4-6,12-13`.
3. The empirical matrix remains exactly eight relative-low slots and two relative-high slots. Those labels are route strata, not capability certification. The user will supply the exact models before testing. Sources: `records/corrections.jsonl:13`; `records/decisions.jsonl:46`; `records/design_atoms.jsonl:55`; current testing correction `charter/correction-0001-user-model-selection-hold.md`.

## Compilation, splitting, and evidence custody

4. One shared Agent Packet Compiler owns typed work-intent intake, deterministic construction/splitting, final rendering, coverage manifests, and receipt production. Feature-local bypass prompts are invalid. Sources: `records/design_atoms.jsonl:30`; `records/decisions.jsonl:21`.
5. Controllers and durable stores retain the complete source universe. Agents receive only bounded assignments. Plan work uses stable PlanUnit and acceptance/source refs; generated shards are not assumed-safe packets. Missing context is requested through typed, smallest-sufficient, newly admitted follow-ups. Sources: `records/decisions.jsonl:16,19,22`; `records/design_atoms.jsonl:24,28,31`.
6. New or changed unstructured sources without an authoritative obligation census are deterministically segmented into immutable overlapping windows, redundantly extracted, span-covered, and hierarchically reconciled. Every obligation retains its exact source span and classification; unresolved or conflicting meaning remains mandatory and blocks execution. Sources: `records/design_atoms.jsonl:33-35`; `records/decisions.jsonl:24-26`.
7. Split results carry per-requirement dispositions, evidence refs, uncertainty, missing-context states, and disagreements. Hierarchical reducers consume bounded typed manifests, never raw-transcript fan-in. Missing IDs, partial reducers, contradictions, or undisposed disagreement block completion. Sources: `records/design_atoms.jsonl:32`; `records/decisions.jsonl:23`.

## Authority, admission, safety, and dispatch identity

8. Controller policy, objective, response contract, and allowed tool directives are explicit authorities. Source, retrieval, memory, plugin, and tool text are untrusted evidence and cannot change objective, permissions, write scope, response schema, or override authority. Unresolved authority conflicts fail closed. Sources: `records/design_atoms.jsonl:45`; `records/decisions.jsonl:36`.
9. Packet Admission Gate and FileSafe are independent deterministic programs over the same final provider-visible payload, structured attachments, and route binding. Packet Admission proves structural/obligation coverage; FileSafe proves safety and authorization. Neither substitutes for the other, and infrastructure gates add zero hidden mandatory model calls. Sources: `records/corrections.jsonl:11`; `records/design_atoms.jsonl:48,54`; `records/decisions.jsonl:39,45`.
10. FileSafe receipts bind exact safety-current inputs, including payload/attachment digests, provenance, instruction/runtime/run/route identities, permission snapshot, canonical roots/scopes, baselines, overrides, and mutation revision. Semantic adjudication cannot grant FileSafe authority. Sources: `records/design_atoms.jsonl:47,49-50`; `records/decisions.jsonl:38,40-41`.
11. The adapter sends one immutable dispatch-intent snapshot binding exact provider bytes, attachments, both gate receipts, route/model/account/transform identity, and run/node/attempt identity. Reconstruction, late transformation, or any changed byte/binding forces both gates again. Sources: `records/design_atoms.jsonl:52`; `records/decisions.jsonl:43`.
12. Receipt dependencies are content-addressed and currentness-sensitive. Changed inputs invalidate affected descendants. Derived source windows/censuses/evidence/reductions may be reused while current, but execution-authority receipts never transfer across run/node/attempt/route/permission contexts. Sources: `records/design_atoms.jsonl:46,53`; `records/decisions.jsonl:37,44`.

## Later turns, results, and responses

13. Automated later turns reconstruct from bounded current state, decisions, admitted excerpts, and governed refs instead of append-only conversation history. Every model-visible tool, MCP, plugin, terminal, search, file, vision, memory, or data result passes through bounded evidence admission; oversized raw results remain in custody. Sources: `records/design_atoms.jsonl:42-43`; `records/decisions.jsonl:33-34`.
14. Every response is deterministically validated for schema, exact assigned IDs, evidence refs, uncertainty, and coverage before reducer or completion admission. Any repair is a visible, bounded, defect-only model role with a fixed attempt limit; invalid output is never silently merged. Sources: `records/design_atoms.jsonl:44`; `records/decisions.jsonl:35,45`.
15. Missing/stale/mismatched receipts, unavailable enforcement infrastructure, unknown routes, ambiguous identity, post-gate mutation, partial coverage, or unresolved meaning produce typed blocked outcomes. They never fall back to a raw prompt or optimistic completion. Sources: `records/design_atoms.jsonl:36,40-41`; `records/decisions.jsonl:27,31-32`.

## Evidence and claim limits

- A deterministic test proves only the tested deterministic contract.
- An isolated model-call experiment measures subject behavior under the fixed packet; it does not prove Puppet Master production integration or enforcement.
- A high-route pass does not imply a low-route pass.
- Route catalog presence is not provider-effective identity proof.
- Canonical planning, implemented runtime, executable integration, and certified behavior remain distinct states.

Four genuinely different Settings redesigns.

Each concept explores a distinct information-architecture thesis for Puppet Master's Settings system — not four skins of one design. They share realistic demo data, all eight themes plus reduced motion, the required narrow/squeezed and surrounding-shell states, and a uniform manager language. No concept is ranked here.

01
Editorial · command center

Control Room

One dominant search is the primary verb. Important areas are large editorial panels with a title, purpose, and live status — never filter chips. Notices carry one clear action. Workspace is a book-TOC: left category index + a continuous document.

02
Spatial · focus + context

Atlas

Settings is territory to navigate, not a list to filter. Destinations are named regions with boundaries and a you-are-here marker; region size encodes density. Workspace is focus+context: one region fills the canvas while a compact minimap keeps the whole map visible.

03
Single surface · disclosure

Stack

Settings never navigates away — everything is one surface that expands. Home is a prioritized stack: search, then status-critical, then destinations as expandable rows that grow inline into the full workspace. Only one expanded at a time. Disclosure is the whole model.

04
Continuous document · river

Stream

Settings is a river you read and jump within. Home is the headwaters: a persistent landmark rail of named markers + search + an opening status beat. Destinations are named sections; managers are channels you switch among via tabs within their section.

What every concept shares

A uniform manager language proves the design is a system, not one provider screen. Every manager renders through the same shell: search/filter, add/connect, health summary, resource rows, requested-vs-effective state, loading/empty/error/managed/unavailable states, and logs.

○ Search is central; no primary category pills
○ No emoji, no colored left-side borders
○ All 8 themes + reduced motion
○ Narrow/squeezed + surrounding shell
○ Provider/Agent/Model in every concept
○ 10 managers across the four
○ Requested vs effective everywhere
○ Slint-portable (no DOM-measured state)

Opus 5

Settings redesign — four concepts

Four genuinely different answers to the same brief, not one design in four skins. Each asks a different question about what a setting is addressed by — its location, its name, its route, or its effective value — and that question drives the home, the navigation, the disclosure model and the motion. They share demo data, the status vocabulary, the search index and the section maths; they share no layout. Nothing here is ranked.

Controls drive all four at once through the Concept Hub bridge.

How to read this. Each concept carries its own review strip with a demo-state switcher — calm, several things needing attention, catalogues refreshing, provider degraded, included usage exhausted — so the required states are inspectable rather than described. Actions that cannot really happen in a standalone page return a dated Simulated receipt naming the call a production build would make; none of them pretend to have signed in, installed a CLI, spent money or contacted a provider. No concept is recommended over another.

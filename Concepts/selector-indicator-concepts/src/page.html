Selector indicator concepts

Friendly and Basic draw their selected item with Glass's clear lens today, only re-tinted. That is why the title bar's page tabs and onboarding's current step look the same in all three. Below are three Friendly concepts and two Basic options, each shown in the real places with the product's own markup and colours, in light and dark. Glass stays exactly as it is; it is shown as the reference.

Today

Screenshots of the product as it is now (look-lane build b3456fac54, 2x). Same lens in all three looks: a soft rounded tinted chip with a hairline and a top catch light that glides with Glass's liquid stretch. In Friendly the chip's fill is about 1.03 to 1.06:1 against the bar, so selection reads almost only from the label's colour.

Friendly: three concepts

Friendly is warm and playful: Poppins, a cream page with a dot field, a 2 px ink outline on the window and the cards, hard offset shadows like a sticker sheet, Pip's speech bubbles, mint in the shell and coral in onboarding. Each concept keeps those colours and changes what the selection is, and how it moves, so it can never be mistaken for Glass's lens.

Basic: two options

Basic is plain and utilitarian: Inter, flat grey surfaces, 4 px corners, one blue accent, no glow. Both options drop everything that made Basic look like Glass (the translucent tint, the hairline, the catch light, the soft shadow, the liquid glide).

Contrast and High Contrast

Measured live in this page from the computed colours of the demos above (WCAG 2.2: 4.5:1 for text, 3:1 for the marks that show selected, hover and focus). Every concept also keeps the product's rule against side stripes, underlines and pill-shaped labels (DR-069): selection is always the item's own filled surface.

Where the indicator is used

One component draws every one of these: Liquid Glass's switcher. Each look turns it on and colours it with --pm-switch-* tokens; Glass sets none of them, so a change for Friendly or Basic cannot touch Glass.

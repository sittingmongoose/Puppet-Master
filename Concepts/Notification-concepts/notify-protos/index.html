Title-bar notification prototypes

Six approaches for the title bar. 01 is the polish candidate: stack + count replaces the status-bar bell, with deep-links and Plans-backed action cards.

01 — Rightward stack (polish candidate) Stack + count is the sole notify affordance (no bottom bell). Accordion inbox, theme-native cards, HITL/Permission/FileSafe/Concern actions, click → navigate. 02 — Bell + accordion Compact bell + badge. Flash preview under the bar, then accordion inbox. 03 — Severity chip rail Color chips pack left→right with +N overflow. Dense and scannable. 04 — Latest morph One pill shows the newest item + count; expands by morphing into a list. 05 — Drop then dock Center drop under the bar; important items fly into a mini dock. 06 — Fan deck Mild 2D fan of cards in the spacer; click unfolds a staggered column.

01 is selected for polish. Port to PMConcept8.html later: retire #pm6StatusBell / bottom toast stack and route unread through the title-bar stack.

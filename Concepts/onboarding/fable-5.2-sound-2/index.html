What changed since round one

Sound

  • One sound per moment. A click that causes several things is heard as its most important sound, then Pip. The stage's own noises stay under that. Try "A busy click" above.
  • No strum, no thump. The band's answers to every click are gone, and nothing the window or the stage plays carries anything low.
  • The song keeps going. A new screen or chapter never starts it again; parts join under the others and rise in.
  • Voices. Friendly, Basic, Retro Dark and Retro Light speak with the original sound and the new phrasing. Glass speaks in struck notes over a murmur of its old voice. NieR and Glass are close by, not down a hall. "Which take" above compares them with round one.
  • Retro Light's printer is a few spaced key taps and the margin bell, not a fast ratchet.

Songs, menu and stage

  • Four songs a look, the original tune among them, and a jukebox that plays them in turn. Bubble Drift, Tidy Trio and Open Glass are rewritten; Bit Bop sleeps to a lullaby of its own hook.
  • The sound menu (top right) has sound on or off, three levels, the song and the jukebox. It remembers what was set.
  • The stage keeps the music box in every look that has a floor. Its crank now turns the way a side crank does, and everything that moves in time is run by the compositor: nothing is redrawn per frame for it.
  • Heard, not only measured. Gemini 4 Neon listened to round one against the notes and to this round blind, old against new.

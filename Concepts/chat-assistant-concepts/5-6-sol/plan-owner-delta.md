## Supersession and retirement register

- Retain Send/Stop composer semantics. Do not restore Resend as a message action.
- Retire passive questionnaire expiration and `cmd.questionnaire.expire`; cancelled and submitted question records persist.
- Keep `Ask for approval`, `Auto accept edits`, `Auto`, and `Full Access`; do not expose Yolo/Regular or raw enums.
- Keep conversation rewind separate from file revert and safe-point restore.
- Keep the artifact workspace on the left and outside Context by default; no old right-panel shell assumption.
- Keep selection and status meaning out of colored left-edge accents.
- Keep provider acquisition/install/auth/readiness/update/repair/rollback outside Chat. Chat owns only the compact `Provider Setup Required` projection, exact-row deep link, and originating-operation return context.
- Provider CLIs are not bundled in Puppet Master core, included in the default native/server/container/WSL/Kubernetes baseline, pre-seeded as PM Tool Store packages, or silently acquired by Project, model, provider, Goal, Plan, WorkNode, agent, Auto, or On.
- Initial acquisition requires an explicit user Install or existing-installation selection, the official provider/package source, and the exact Host/Environment. Installation and authentication are separate; `Auto`/`On` are post-consent maintenance only.
- Bundling requires a later named user-approved exception for one exact provider CLI/platform/source. Catalog, adapter, or acquisition-class assignment cannot imply that exception.
- Preserve coalesced provisioning and reject stale continuation tokens. Keep protected `AuthBrowserSession` human-only and opaque.
- Keep Browser, Browser Workspace, Browser Runtime, BrowserAction and Site Reader terminology; do not introduce a PM Playwright facade.
- Keep app-wide notifications in the canonical title bar.
- Keep server-owned canonical state; do not revive local-first multi-writer sync claims.

## What it is

The editor's ⋮ menu → **Appearance…** opens a **Customize screen**, a sibling of the terminal's. It shares the
terminal's classes, its scheme catalog and its tile-and-chip grammar, and has:
- a live preview drawn by the editor's own renderer;
- a change meter;
- quick sets: The look's own, Calm, Easy reading, Show structure;
- Try it actions: Move the caret, Type, Go to a problem, Step back;
- six groups: Colors, Text, Caret, Lines & guides, Motion & effects, Behavior.

The real editor stays uncovered, framed beside the screen.

There are two entry points:
- the editor's ⋮ menu → Appearance… opens it for **This editor**;
- Settings → Editor & Terminal → Editor look → **Open Customize** opens it for **All editors**.

The editor's ⋮ menu also has **Indentation…**, which names the file's indentation and opens the screen at it.

Every look draws the screen its own way: Basic, Friendly, Glass (a Liquid Glass `menu` surface with `data-lg-dense`),
Retro and NieR, in light and dark, with Reduced Motion and narrow windows down to 360 px.

## The editor settings audit (pm7-editor-audit-sol, main d1dda811b6), item by item

| Audit item | Now |
|---|---|
| 1. Settings and Home were separate stores | one model; every row drives the editor, and Settings shows the editor's choices |
| 2. Four popover faces drew JetBrains Mono | canon's three faces, each drawn |
| 3. Size 8–32 shown, renderer clamped to 10–22 | 10–24 in half pixels in Settings, the model and the renderer |
| 4. Wrap, line numbers and syntax colours did nothing | wraps at word breaks, with indent and visual-row Up/Down; numbers off; plain text |
| 5. Jump highlight fixed at 5 s | the row's time: 0.5 s is gone by 1.5 s, and 10 s is still lit at 6.5 s |
| 6. Three auto-save modes never saved | after 0.5 s, after 1 s, on focus change; never untitled or read-only |
| 7. Tab caps ignored | held at the next open, on save and when a layout loads (15 tabs loaded as 4 under caps of 2 and 4) |
| 8. Space on a switch dropped the focus | the redrawn row's control takes the focus back (`kit.d/26-row-focus.js`) |
| 9. Formatter flags reset on reload | kept beside the fixture projection, per Project (`kit.d/28-toolchain-flags.js`) |
| 10. The retired strip row was still shown | retired in placement |

From the audit's gap list it adds:
- weight, letter spacing and ligatures;
- caret shape, width, blink, movement and trail;
- current-line highlight and relative numbers;
- indent guides with the active block lit, bracket pair colours, show spaces and rulers;
- wrap at a column and sticky lines;
- smooth scrolling, lines arriving, scrolling past the end, fading editors not in use, scanlines and glow;
- contrast, italic comments and bold keywords;
- indentation: tab size, indent with spaces or tabs, and following the file. The 2-space fixtures had their guides on
  every other level and Tab inserted 4 spaces.

It does not add a per-token colour editor or minimap detail controls (NOTES.md in the packet).

## What it is

The editor's ⋮ menu → **Appearance…** opens a **Customize screen**, a sibling of the terminal's. It shares the
terminal's classes, its scheme catalog and its tile-and-chip grammar, and has:
- a live preview drawn by the editor's own renderer;
- a change meter;
- quick sets: The look's own, Calm, Easy reading, Show structure;
- Try it actions: Move the caret, Type, Go to a problem, Step back;
- six groups: Colors, Text, Caret, Lines & guides, Motion & effects, Behavior.

The real editor stays uncovered, framed beside the screen.

There are two entry points:
- the editor's ⋮ menu → Appearance… opens it for **This editor**;
- Settings → Editor & Terminal → Editor look → **Open Customize** opens it for **All editors**.

The editor's ⋮ menu also has **Indentation…**, which names the file's indentation and opens the screen at it.

Every look draws the screen its own way: Basic, Friendly, Glass (a Liquid Glass `menu` surface with `data-lg-dense`),
Retro and NieR, in light and dark, with Reduced Motion and narrow windows down to 360 px.

| 1. Settings and Home were separate stores | one model; every row drives the editor, and Settings shows the editor's choices |
| 2. Four popover faces drew JetBrains Mono | canon's three faces, each drawn |
| 3. Size 8–32 shown, renderer clamped to 10–22 | 10–24 in half pixels in Settings, the model and the renderer |
| 4. Wrap, line numbers and syntax colours did nothing | wraps at word breaks, with indent and visual-row Up/Down; numbers off; plain text |

| 8. Space on a switch dropped the focus | the redrawn row's control takes the focus back (`kit.d/26-row-focus.js`) |
| 9. Formatter flags reset on reload | kept beside the fixture projection, per Project (`kit.d/28-toolchain-flags.js`) |
| 10. The retired strip row was still shown | retired in placement |

From the audit's gap list it adds:
- weight, letter spacing and ligatures;
- caret shape, width, blink, movement and trail;
- current-line highlight and relative numbers;
- indent guides with the active block lit, bracket pair colours, show spaces and rulers;
- wrap at a column and sticky lines;
- smooth scrolling, lines arriving, scrolling past the end, fading editors not in use, scanlines and glow;
- contrast, italic comments and bold keywords;
- indentation: tab size, indent with spaces or tabs, and following the file. The 2-space fixtures had their guides on
  every other level and Tab inserted 4 spaces.

It does not add a per-token colour editor or minimap detail controls (NOTES.md in the packet).

## Round 2 (2026-10-14 afternoon): Jared's answers

Rebased onto main `cae30c7f2d` (the eleven source commits cherry-picked; round 1's page-regeneration commits dropped
and the pages built again). The published page is now `a98e87302fa36536…`.

- **Token colours.** Customize → Colors → Token colours lists the editor's 17 kinds of word, each drawn in its colour
  on the code's ground.
  - A picker has the scheme's colours as swatches, hue, saturation and lightness sliders drawn in the colour being
    made, a hex box, and the contrast it reads at.
  - Resting on a swatch previews it on the preview and the editor.
  - A colour belongs to the scheme it was chosen in (`<scheme key>/<kind>`; Follow look is `look:<look>-<mode>`).
  - A colour under the text floor is drawn at the floor, hue kept, and the picker says so.
  - The look's or scheme's own puts a word back.
  - Model: `tokenColors` in `09-editor-look.js` (a map merged token by token, preview over tab over All editors), bound
    to the proposed row `code.editing.token-colors` (keyvalue, `proposed.d/27-editor-tokens.json`), shown in Settings
    under Editor look: more options.
  - The editor applies the colours as inline CSS variables on its code elements, so a slider drag rebuilds no row.
    When only token colours change, it redraws nothing else.
- **This editor / All editors like the terminal.** Both screens open from their own menu on the scope last picked
  for that tab (This editor at first). From Settings they open on All for that visit only: `opts.scope === 'app'`.
  Before, Settings' visit wrote the terminal's remembered scope.
- **One dock rule.** The preview docks only when the screen is not beside the real editor or terminal
  (`.pmt-cz-beside`) and the preview has scrolled out of view. The same `syncDock()` is in `77-customize.js` and
  `11-editor-customize.js`.
- **Saving & tabs.** Preview Tabs and Tab Sizing are placed in Editor & Terminal › Saving & tabs, with the two tab
  limits, by `placement.d/61-editor-runtime.json`. The five retired terminal rows are in its `retire_ids`, so they are
  drawn nowhere.
- **Liquid Glass Light edges** for the terminal's screen (`26-customize.css`), and `data-lg-surface="menu"
  data-lg-dense` on its dialog.
- **Slider cost.** A hue step was about 25 ms of script, style and layout on Nas1. It is now about 12 ms:
  - the editor skips its minimap and scrollbar redraw for token-only changes;
  - the screen writes text only when it changes;
  - the rows' spoken names wait for the kept step, because a rewritten name relaid the page through the hover-tag
    descriptions;
  - `.pmt-cz-body` keeps its scrollbar gutter.

  On the P1000 the drag draws every other frame, 30 a second, up from one in three or four. The e547340e03 message
  says "nearly every frame"; that is wrong, and this is the measured figure.

## Round 3 (2026-10-14 evening): every one-of-several selector keeps to one line

Jared saw Show spaces wrap onto two rows, with its moving fill stretched across both. `.pmt-cz-seg` was
`flex-wrap: wrap`, and the ink (`.pmw-ink`, 27-frames.js) spans the row's full height.

What changed:

- **No wrapping.** A row is now `nowrap` with `max-width: 100%`, and its choices are `flex: none`. A field's one grid
  column is `minmax(0, 1fr)`, so a row is measured against its own field.
- **`PMW.czFit(root)`** (11-editor-customize.js; the terminal's screen calls it too) fits a row that would overflow:
  1. its field's grid becomes one column (`data-cols="1"`);
  2. then the choices use short words (`data-fit="short"`, from a third entry in `seg()`; the full words become the
     choice's `aria-label`);
  3. then the row becomes a list (`data-fit="stack"`; the ink's same-line test makes it change at once).
- **When it measures:** after `place()` and `paint()`. Its key is read from attributes only (the dialog's width, look,
  mode, NieR parts, the shown choices, and a font-load epoch), so an unchanged screen costs no layout.

Verification:

  - Contexts: the editor and the terminal screen at 1920 beside, 1440, 1280, 900, 360, from Settings, and spread out;

# D4 private evaluator rubric

These eight controls are a predeclared **partial denominator**, not an exhaustive requirements inventory, injected runtime bugs, a maximum finding count, or a measured prior-knowledge score. The brief and Plan are deliberately underspecified. Score whether research closes consequential semantic gaps in that thin planning fixture. Record useful findings outside these controls separately; do not force them into this list or cap accepted findings at eight.

Score each control once: **full = 1**, **half = 0.5**, **missed = 0**, using its local criteria. Full credit requires a correct consequential mechanism, suitable primary evidence, and an actionable correction or explicit support boundary with a discriminating acceptance check. Merely copying a specification phrase or saying “validate metadata/use a library” is insufficient. Half credit is a materially useful but incomplete correction. Missed includes absence, wrong semantics, unsupported claims, or treating an optional capability as mandatory. If a finding contradicts itself, record the contradiction and award no full credit until resolved. Report the raw eight scores and points/8, clearly labeled partial-control coverage.

Separate discovery from accepted repair. Log finding ID, evidence, control mapping, proposed disposition, source authority level, and actual derivative-Plan landing. Discovery credit does not prove implementation or runtime conformance. An accepted plan repair needs reviewer-supported owner placement and preserved scope; optional breadth belongs in a pending product-decision record. Deduplicate repeated formulations of one mechanism, while retaining genuinely independent novel mechanisms outside the control denominator. Report unsupported/incorrect findings and scope violations separately so raw finding volume cannot conceal precision loss.

The source_rule fields summarize format semantics. The synthetic probes and full/half/missed judgments are original evaluator constructions/inferences, not examples copied from the standards, real test results, or assertions that a particular reader is broken. Do not demand these exact numerical examples from researchers; equivalent discriminating reasoning earns the same credit. Distinguish a malformed fileset from a valid fileset using unsupported features. A truthful documented support boundary can be correct; silently misreading admitted input is not. Do not add support requirements beyond the brief without recording a product choice.

Do not conflate mandatory metadata constraints with universal viewer obligations. Full codec coverage, a particular palette, HCS/collection presentation, advanced rendering, extra formats and additional interaction modes are not automatically approved. The local read-only/no-export/no-network/no-clinical boundary is fixed by the brief. A proposed feature that crosses it is out of scope, even if useful in another viewer. Existing generic responsiveness and stale-read cancellation requirements remain eligible for novel findings but do not increase this partial denominator automatically.

The sources were read from primary project specifications on 2026-09-09. The OME page identifies version 0.5 and displays 8 September 2026 as report metadata; this is **not evidence that all its semantics first appeared yesterday**. The live Zarr core page identifies 3.1. These controls use stable v3 behavior and do not require 3.1-only extension forms. Controls identify the relevant sections of the [OME-Zarr 0.5 specification](https://ngff.openmicroscopy.org/0.5/), [Zarr core](https://zarr-specs.readthedocs.io/en/latest/v3/core/index.html), [Bytes codec](https://zarr-specs.readthedocs.io/en/latest/v3/codecs/bytes/index.html), [Transpose codec](https://zarr-specs.readthedocs.io/en/latest/v3/codecs/transpose/index.html), and [filesystem store](https://zarr-specs.readthedocs.io/en/latest/v3/stores/filesystem/index.html); no secondary overview is relied upon. No implementation/reference-code claim is made without a pinned inspected artifact.

Researcher-input inspection: product-brief.md and Plans/Viewer.md describe the intended synthetic viewer, boundaries and broad acceptance only. They contain none of the private control IDs, adversarial numeric examples, full/half/missed rubric, or expected score. Generic requirements such as correct calibration/labels/errors are legitimate product intent, not evaluator leakage. Keep this entire evaluator directory out of case staging, task prompts, source manifests and researcher-visible references. Freeze hashes before launch and record any later change as a rubric revision rather than silently changing scoring.

They overlap one another and the controls; nine is not an exhaustive discovery count, a defect count or nine additional landings. The final fixture consolidates applicable correctness work into eleven groups. Optional capabilities remain in the unanswered decision packet.

The fourth comparison also sharpens signed calibrated time: scale 2 seconds and translation -5 seconds must produce -5 and -3 at indices 0 and 1. This refines coordinate correctness; it does not add a clock overlay. Its size/lifetime and failed-selection examples likewise refine existing resource/state obligations. They are not counted as extra independent families.

Native work included actual source acquisition, metadata/schema checks and retained-CSV parsing. It did not run Slide Scout, decode the sample images with a selected backend or demonstrate memory/latency acceptance. The [stage review](evaluation/comparison-stage-review.md) records retention and losses, and [provenance](provenance.json) distinguishes native contributions from evaluator synthesis and scope corrections.

## 1. The outcome we need

Build and test a process that can take a user's idea or incomplete plan; discover unfamiliar useful competitors, similar projects, analogous components, mechanisms, alternatives, implementation details, and failure histories; investigate primary documentation and relevant repositories; compare the research with the plan; and return a complete, source-grounded planning proposal.

The central problem is still that agents do not know what they do not know. Correctly reviewing a supplied source set is insufficient. Discovering sources without preserving their meaning is also insufficient. Preserve breadth, exact material obligations, governing conditions and versions, negative constraints, useful optional capabilities, user-owned choices, rejected/already-covered findings, uncertainty, and meaningful future validation.

**Optimize the complete usable result, not just one phase.** Seek repeatable 20–30% reductions in time/resource consumption, with 2× as the stretch. A smaller, honestly replicated gain is useful. Faster wrong, evasive, or incomplete outputs are not efficiency wins. Treat latency and resource consumption as separate outcomes: a latency improvement can require more work, and unknown billing cannot become a cost-saving claim.

A practical production recipe should work on one affordable provider/account and normally two to four candidate contexts. The experiment may fan out across many independent cases and methods; do not present that whole experimental swarm as the product recipe. Mixed-provider recipes are allowed as explicitly labeled secondary investigations, not a substitute for a usable single-provider reference.

This campaign does NOT audit or repair canonical Puppet Master plans, migrate ledgers, generate PlanUnits/WorkNodes, build production features, or run the surrounding Planning Wizard pipeline. Findings and proposed plan edits stay in isolated sandboxes. A later explicit task decides adoption.

## 7. Track B — twelve concrete options, including genuinely new ones

Each technique gets two matched cases. Cells B01, B04, B05 and B08 are **component tests** on a fresh frozen investigator/draft/source package. All others are **complete brief-discovery-to-proposal tests** against R0-ER13, with the change isolated where practical.

Control: a complete fresh final rewrite after the same source-backed critique. ### B02 — critic also finalizes (FULL)

Control: independent critic followed by a fresh reviser. Treatment: the fresh critic verifies and produces the complete final in the same native Goal.

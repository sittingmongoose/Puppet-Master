# Rehearsal cue-sheet revision synchronization

A small live-event team needs a cue-sheet component for its desktop rehearsal app. A sheet contains ordered cues, timing notes, role assignments and comments anchored to a cue. Multiple crew members edit from laptops that may be disconnected during a rehearsal: insert or reorder cues, change timing/text, and add discussion notes. Reconnection must preserve useful work and expose incompatible edits for an intelligible human decision; a comment must not silently move to the wrong cue after a reorder.

Stage staff also need an explicitly approved run sheet. Rehearsal edits should not silently replace that published version, and staff must be able to tell which version they are viewing. Research stable identity, list/text/comment synchronization, revision and publication boundaries, retry/reconnect recovery and useful conflict presentation. Compare plausible mechanisms and real implementation lessons. A modest team relay is allowed, but an always-online editor, enterprise account or new chat product is not. Unknown actual cue counts can remain unknown; they do not remove feasible mechanism research. Deliver a component proposal, not a deployed rehearsal service.

Research current primary documentation, governing implementation code and relevant real implementation history. Discover useful unfamiliar mechanisms and plausible alternatives without preselecting a product. After the one-shot release of our tentative exact plan, retain, change or reject its material choices with reasons. Deliver a complete self-contained proposal with source conditions, unresolved user-specific facts, trade-offs, and meaningful validation. Separate research or small checks actually executed from future validation proposed; building or deploying the future application is not required.

Complete all substantive research and planning obligations within a maximum of 60 elapsed minutes. Finish earlier when the complete output is ready. There is no minimum research duration, no requirement to consume the allowance, and no credit for waiting. The deadline includes arm-specific setup, provider queueing after admission, tools, native retries, handoffs, revision and delivery. Output quality is assessed independently of elapsed time.

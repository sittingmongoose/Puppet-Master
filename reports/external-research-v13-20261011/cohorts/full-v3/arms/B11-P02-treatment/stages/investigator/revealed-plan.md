# Tentative exact plan: Rehearsal cue-sheet revision synchronization

Our tentative design stores a sheet as an ordered array of cue objects and a comment list. Cues receive random IDs, but comments refer to the cue's array position for fast lookup. Each laptop saves an editable sheet snapshot and uploads it to a team relay on reconnect. The relay compares the client's last-edited timestamp with the current snapshot and keeps the newer sheet, retaining the other one as a backup. The receiving laptop then replaces its local sheet with the relay copy.

Conflicts are displayed as a side-by-side comparison of the two whole sheets, and a coordinator chooses which to keep. A Publish action marks the current snapshot approved; stage viewers fetch the latest approved snapshot when online and cache it for a disconnected rehearsal. We intend comments and role assignments to survive exports. Saving locally precedes success feedback; exchange retries can resend a snapshot. Review whether this preserves concurrent text/order/comment work, stable references, publication identity and restart/reconnect behavior. Keep publication deliberate and conflict decisions understandable; no always-online editing dependency is acceptable. The timestamp/backup scheme is a proposal for assessment, not a verified merge guarantee.

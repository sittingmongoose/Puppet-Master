# Final B12 selection

The first candidate, dependency-conditioned verification reuse, was rejected before any scientific admission after exact ER10 M05 inspection: it already reuses supported findings when their declared dependencies remain unchanged and researches the changed closure. Changing the receiving role would be insufficient novelty. All development cost remains charged; no scored attempt was replaced.

Selected instead: bounded speculative final writing during independent criticism, followed by one complete source-backed integration in the same fresh reviser. The [current primary asynchronous-agent paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.13360v2) supports continuing useful work while required information is pending and withholding a final answer until it arrives. [Agent-workflow speculation](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.07846v1) provides the explicit early downstream / staged generation pattern. This is a small schedule/prompt adaptation; it imports no Bayesian pricing system, training, full event loop or probability assumption.

[Speculative RAG](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/file/2ea06b52f613716e67458f5ab3fb7558-Paper-Conference.pdf) was also inspected: its multi-draft specialist/generalist architecture is a less direct analogue, so no multi-draft selection or larger verifier is used. Adaptive review stopping would risk incomplete full review. The selected method preserves independent complete criticism and all final obligations, with waiting inside the existing18-minute elapsed ceiling; extra work and failure are primary possible outcomes.

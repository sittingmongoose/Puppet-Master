# B12-v2 — Bounded speculative final writing

Prospective method, no candidate execution or efficacy result. The prior unadmitted dependency-reuse idea was rejected because ER10 M05 already contains its core; its concise rejection record and charged preparation lineage remain in the parent B12-v1/rejected-proposal/ lineage. The four input fixtures are unchanged.

**Hypothesis.** A fresh reviser can prepare source-backed full-proposal structure and prose while the independent critic checks the same frozen investigator draft. Bounded provisional work followed by one exact critique integration can shorten the serialized handoff/final-writing path, while the unchanged role/global elapsed ceilings and full critique gate expose extra work, anchoring and insufficient post-critique time.

**Exact change.** Root starts the ordinary independent critic and a fresh reviser together after investigator terminal freeze. The reviser prepares one provisional complete proposal for at most six minutes from its original preparation, inclusive of queue/setup and ending earlier when the provisional is ready. It then waits for one root-sealed terminal critique and resolves all material criticism/source/obligation duties before sealing a complete final. The critic sees only the frozen investigator material, never the provisional.

**Timing/topology.** execution_topology=b12-overlap-v1. Three candidate contexts, with two at most after investigation. Investigator30, critic12 and reviser18 are the original ELAPSED ceilings, clipped to armT0+60; reviser waiting consumes the18, with no receipt reset or extra writer. No assumed saving from context overlap. Queueing may erase overlap or make this fail; failures stay assigned.

**Expected failure.** Provisional anchoring, major corrections wasting early work, insufficient remaining integration time, premature final selection, or an invalid late-input binding. Every material criticism and original obligation remains required.

**Frozen measures.** Full six-axis original-final quality and defect identities; failure-inclusive paired delivery/resource outcomes; actual overlap, queue/setup/wait/residency, provisional-to-final correction/preservation and critique-release/final availability. No billing claim or population reliability inference from two cases.

**Primary basis.** Current v2 [Speculative Interaction Agents](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.13360v2) §§3.1-3.2 and [Cost-Aware Speculative Execution](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.07846v1) §§3.1-3.3 motivate pending-input work and withholding final commitment until required information arrives. Their full systems, training and reported speedups are not reproduced or imported. Exact private captures, bounded authored capsules and source/licensing locators are in evidence.md.

**Root admission requirement.** A pinned b12-overlap-v1 support snapshot must implement the one root-sealed terminal-critic release to the declared reviser reader within its original deadline. Sequential-v1 cannot execute this method. No new scheduler/framework, extra stage, candidate launch or helper edit is performed here. Only the three prompt delta files are candidate input; method-development evidence is excluded.

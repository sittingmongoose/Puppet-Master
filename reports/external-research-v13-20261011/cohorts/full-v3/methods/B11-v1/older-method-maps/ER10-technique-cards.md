## 6. Technique cards

These are prospective hypotheses, not established wins. Existing safe input navigation, credential isolation, stable source IDs and straightforward output saving are common infrastructure; do not deliberately break the control to make a technique look effective.

### M01 — Two substantive stages rather than three
**Control:** Fresh researcher, fresh critic, then a separate final revision.  
**Treatment:** Fresh researcher, then a fresh critic that checks and authors the complete final; preserve real critique dispositions.  
**Unit:** A coherent research-and-proposal module; integrated topology contrasts also exercise the complete brief.  
**Measure:** Final correctness and supported coverage; handoff loss; total latency, occupancy and output. Equal aggregate budget; record stage allocation.  
**Quality guard:** Do not import old I-07 answers or reuse its timing as a contemporaneous control. Fewer stages are not automatically better.

### M02 — Coherent multi-question batching
**Control:** Comparable related questions handled in separate bounded candidate research assignments.  
**Treatment:** One coherent assignment researches the same questions together and reuses their shared context.  
**Unit:** Two or three related questions with cross-cutting conditions; not unrelated full product domains.  
**Measure:** Repeated reading/writing and cold-start cost, same supported question coverage, cross-question confusion.  
**Quality guard:** Keep a per-question disposition and shared-condition map. Count all control jobs and all treatment work; no gratuitously wasteful baseline.

### M03 — Progressive, repository-guided evidence retrieval
**Control:** Reasonable native search/read workflow, without forced whole-repository scans.  
**Treatment:** Use available file/symbol/section navigation, read focused evidence, and expand to governing definitions and call paths when needed.  
**Unit:** Actual component behavior at a pinned version, including a condition outside the first matching passage.  
**Measure:** Source-reading time and delivered context versus correct mechanism interpretation, missed conditions and versions.  
**Quality guard:** Do not replace source inspection with an architecture summary or hide relevant sources from control. Navigation is an index, not evidence of truth.

### M04 — Exact evidence reuse across stages
**Control:** Each stage independently obtains its requested source bytes through ordinary access.  
**Treatment:** Same-case source cache resolves exact requested URLs/commits and extraction versions; critics retain independent query freedom.  
**Unit:** Repeated use of public documentation and code across research and criticism.  
**Measure:** Cold fetch plus subsequent cost, cache hits, source freshness and critique quality.  
**Quality guard:** Do not give treatment evaluator summaries or preselected answers. Content-address raw bytes, never share sibling answers; report cold and warm economics separately.

### M05 — Incremental research and already-covered reuse
**Control:** A legitimate full refresh after a predefined plan/source change.  
**Treatment:** Reuse exact supported prior findings whose declared dependencies remain unchanged; research the changed dependency closure.  
**Unit:** A no-op change plus a substantive source/API/plan change, with obligations at the dependency boundary.  
**Measure:** Refresh time, missed invalidations, false already-covered classifications, cold-seed cost and amortized cost.  
**Quality guard:** Both arms receive the same legitimate prior state. Similar wording is not equivalence; unresolved dependencies cannot silently be accepted as covered.

### M06 — Executed checks with an independent oracle
**Control:** Source-grounded checking using ordinary reasoning and existing general tools, without the specific oracle recipe.  
**Treatment:** Candidate selects bounded trusted operations, independent derivations, differential or metamorphic checks, then binds the result to its report.  
**Unit:** A consequential numerical, serialization, ordering or identity claim with correct and incorrect controls.  
**Measure:** Correct defect detection, false rejection, executed-result/report agreement, verification time and usage.  
**Quality guard:** Executing the claimed formula is not an oracle. A round trip between mutually wrong functions is insufficient. Check applicability, units, versions, and test discrimination.

### M07 — Critical-dependency-first verification with protected coverage
**Control:** Reasonable full-scope critic allocating attention in normal document order.  
**Treatment:** Verify assumptions that could invalidate the selected recommendation first, while reserving an explicit lower-risk and breadth check.  
**Unit:** A selected design with essential dependencies and independently useful optional discoveries.  
**Measure:** Critical-error detection and time to useful correction; full required coverage and missed lower-ranked findings.  
**Quality guard:** No top-k deletion of remaining leads. Unchecked optional evidence remains visible; a required promise cannot be relabeled optional to pass.

### M08 — Amendment-first repair and final-change checking
**Control:** Critic feedback followed by a normal coherent final rewrite.  
**Treatment:** Amend supported finding records locally; check every changed consequential claim and affected dependency before rendering the final.  
**Unit:** A draft containing supported material, local errors, uncertainty and interacting changes.  
**Measure:** Lost valid findings, new final errors, citation/result drift, repeated output and finalization latency.  
**Quality guard:** Required corrections can change or reject upstream claims. Exact retention of false content earns no credit; do not add an unbudgeted fourth full review.

### M09 — Parallel complementary critics at equal total work budget
**Control:** One critic reviews the declared bounded scope.  
**Treatment:** Two same-family critics cover complementary evidence/implementation risks in parallel, followed by explicit reconciliation.  
**Unit:** One bounded module whose two review responsibilities are meaningful and overlap on a small critical seam.  
**Measure:** Critical-path time, aggregate occupied time, unique correct defects, contradictions and reconciliation cost.  
**Quality guard:** Total candidate budget includes both critics and reconciliation. Majority agreement is not source truth; nested tasks consume the family concurrency allocation.

### M10 — Evidence-first criticism to reduce agreement bias
**Control:** Critic reads the proposal before checking the evidence.  
**Treatment:** Critic first derives a short answer to the same bounded question from legitimate sources, then receives the candidate claim and reconciles.  
**Unit:** Supported claims, plausible false claims and justified uncertainty on the same source task.  
**Measure:** False endorsement, false rejection, discovery of counterevidence and additional cost.  
**Quality guard:** Candidate critic is still flash-level. Do not leak evaluator-derived answers or turn the first phase into unbounded duplicate research.

### M11 — Early release/API and unit/domain binding
**Control:** Normal source-grounded research that resolves versions and units as it proceeds.  
**Treatment:** Bind the chosen component release, API/default behavior, coordinate/unit/type domains and applicability before dependent derivations.  
**Unit:** A component recommendation with an older issue, a fix, a current release and a nontrivial applicability condition.  
**Measure:** Avoided false incompatibilities and rework, correct affected-version boundaries, total reading and writing.  
**Quality guard:** A released fix is not proof all platforms are safe. Do not treat missing units as absent calibration or a comment as proof of every code path.

### M12 — Protected breadth, assumptions and cross-component analogies
**Control:** Competent brief-only open discovery without a diversity-specific recipe.  
**Treatment:** Explore different mechanism families and challenge product assumptions, including analogous components outside direct competitors.  
**Unit:** An open research question based on a thin plan/brief, not an assigned answer-bearing source list.  
**Measure:** Distinct useful supported opportunities, avoided risks, breadth, redundant leads and time to decision-ready findings.  
**Quality guard:** A fixed source count is not breadth. Preserve negative discoveries and uncertainty; no evaluator answer list or premium subject-matter hints.

### M13 — Implementation and issue-to-regression investigation
**Control:** Ordinary documentation plus relevant issue/repository reading.  
**Treatment:** Trace an implementation branch and a real issue through fix, regression test and applicable release; stop when the bounded decision is supported.  
**Unit:** A discovered component failure or improvement with potential planning implications.  
**Measure:** Useful new mechanisms, false defect generalizations, history-reading cost and actionable test proposals.  
**Quality guard:** An open issue is a lead, not consensus. A merged change is not automatically in the chosen release. Running one extracted function has a limited scope.

### M14 — Decision-focused single-source delivery
**Control:** One competent narrative research proposal with its normal source references.  
**Treatment:** Maintain one authoritative semantic finding set; render a concise decision view plus complete evidence, conditions and optional-lead views mechanically.  
**Unit:** A substantial output with decisions, alternatives, source conditions, validation proposals and open questions.  
**Measure:** Authoring/reading time, meaning preservation, downstream usability and evidence lookup failures.  
**Quality guard:** Shortening by omission is failure. The host may render and assign identities but cannot write scientific conclusions; do not require four manually synchronized catalogs.

### M15 — Parallel research scouts with bounded synthesis
**Control:** One candidate investigates the full bounded component question.  
**Treatment:** Two same-family scouts investigate complementary mechanism families concurrently, then one bounded synthesis reconciles the union.  
**Unit:** A question with independent source families and a real overlap/interaction to resolve.  
**Measure:** Latency, aggregate cost, supported discovery coverage, duplication and synthesis loss.  
**Quality guard:** No unlimited scout multiplication. Same total candidate-work ceiling; multi-account experimentation must not masquerade as a single-account product win.

### M16 — One broad discovery pass shared across related plan topics
**Control:** Two or three related sandbox plan topics each perform their normal independent discovery/comparison.  
**Treatment:** One flash-authored shared discovery inventory feeds topic-specific comparisons; independent topic obligations remain explicit.  
**Unit:** A small multi-topic project bundle sharing a component or failure mechanism.  
**Measure:** Whole-bundle completion time, duplicate acquisition, unique topic coverage and reuse invalidation.  
**Quality guard:** Include discovery/synthesis costs and prevent shared inventory bias from erasing topic-specific unknowns. Do not mix this task-level sharing with cross-arm answer leakage.

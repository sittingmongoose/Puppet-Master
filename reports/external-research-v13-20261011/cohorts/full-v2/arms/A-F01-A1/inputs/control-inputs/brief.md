# Disconnected field-survey edit journal

A regional ecology group already has a desktop survey application. Design its local edit journal and reconnection component for three field laptops that may remain disconnected for a week. Records contain site identifiers, observation notes, a confidence flag, and links to photos stored separately. Two people can edit the same observation while offline; deleting a record and adding a photo can also overlap. The group needs a comprehensible recovery path after a laptop loses power during a save or reconnect.

Research storage and synchronization approaches, identity and conflict representation, attachment references, and a small operator-facing explanation of what needs review. Preserve a recoverable history of competing edits without requiring the user to understand database internals. Assume up to fifty thousand observations per season; actual attachment sizes and connection speeds are unknown. The existing application's language and storage engine are undecided. Do not mandate a hosted account, upload photo contents automatically, resolve every collision by wall-clock time, or let a reconnect silently resurrect a deleted observation. A manual transfer option should remain possible when the network is unavailable. Recommend a bounded integration sequence and realistic failure-oriented validation.

Produce a research-backed software proposal, not an implementation. Explore relevant primary documentation, repository code, callers/tests, and issue-to-fix or release history where consequential. Compare feasible alternatives without assuming a winning product. Distinguish proposed validation from work actually executed. The exact draft plan is withheld during brief-only discovery and released once at the declared comparison point.

Complete all substantive research and planning obligations within a maximum of 60 elapsed minutes. Finish earlier when the complete output is ready. There is no minimum research duration, no requirement to consume the allowance, and no credit for waiting. The deadline includes arm-specific setup, provider queueing after admission, tools, native retries, handoffs, revision and delivery. Output quality is assessed independently of elapsed time.

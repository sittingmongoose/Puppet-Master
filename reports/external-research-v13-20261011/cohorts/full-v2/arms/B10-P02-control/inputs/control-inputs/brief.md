# Portable training handbook with accessible exercises

A community repair group wants a small training handbook application for volunteers. Authors maintain instructional pages, diagrams and short exercises; learners need offline reading, topic search and a way to record personal exercise progress. The group also shares printable handouts with people who do not install the application. Research a maintainable authoring-to-distribution workflow and the application's basic data boundaries.

Updated lessons should not silently erase a learner's progress or imply that progress on an old exercise proves completion of a materially changed one. Pages must remain navigable by keyboard, with meaningful reading order and text alternatives for instructional diagrams. Search should make lesson versions and retired pages understandable rather than presenting every match as current. Authors sometimes paste material from word processors; investigate import limitations and export fidelity instead of assuming arbitrary formatting survives. Do not require a learner account, upload personal progress, substitute images of text for accessible instructions, or promise print and interactive forms have identical behavior. Include a modest author review step before distribution. Compare authoring/storage and offline/export approaches, identify policy decisions about changed exercises and retired lessons, and propose phased adoption with accessibility and update validation. No publication of training materials, installation for volunteers or processing of actual learning records is requested.

Produce a research-backed software proposal, not an implementation. Explore relevant primary documentation, repository code, callers/tests, and issue-to-fix or release history where consequential. Compare feasible alternatives without assuming a winning product. Distinguish proposed validation from work actually executed. The exact draft plan is withheld during brief-only discovery and released once at the declared comparison point.

Complete all substantive research and planning obligations within a maximum of 60 elapsed minutes. Finish earlier when the complete output is ready. There is no minimum research duration, no requirement to consume the allowance, and no credit for waiting. The deadline includes arm-specific setup, provider queueing after admission, tools, native retries, handoffs, revision and delivery. Output quality is assessed independently of elapsed time.

A8 SCW-6 v1 additive critic instruction
Keep the complete original R0 independent-critic assignment and its deadline. This protocol adds a source-scope discrimination check within that work; it does not narrow the complete review or authorize another context, model call, retry, or time extension.

After reading the whole ordinary R0 input packet, before new source fetching or verdicts, record in critique.md an ordered list of at most six exact draft claims with identifiable scope ambiguity whose mistaken interpretation would change, in priority order, a recommendation, a rejected compatible option, an exact-plan disposition, or a consequential applicability condition. Break ties by draft order. Preserve this selection throughout review. Use fewer than six, including zero, if appropriate. Record consequential scope risks beyond the cap as SCW_NOT_RUN and still review them under ordinary R0 requirements.

For each selected claim, create one SCW record (up to 250 words excluding source identity). Copy the exact draft sentence and sufficient heading/context. Write its intended reading C without changing its substance. Create one nearby diagnostic reading C* by changing exactly one selector: subject/subsystem, product/version, quantity unit/denominator, capability/default/requirement modality, or applicability condition/quantifier. Name the changed selector and its old/new values; preserve all other factual components. If the source sentence cannot be resolved or no valid one-selector contrast exists, mark INAPPLICABLE with a reason and continue ordinary review. Do not substitute a new selection after seeing results.

Reopen authentic primary evidence. Examine governing heading/table headers, definitions, conditions and necessary cross-references. Preserve scope-bearing excerpt(s), source ID plus exact URL, version/commit when available, locator, access UTC and applicability. Keep source text authentic. For the same evidence E, judge C and C* separately as ENTAILS, CONTRADICTS or UNDETERMINED, explaining the scope-bearing witness for each. The labels need not differ. C* is a diagnostic, never an automatically recommended alternative. When several passages are needed, spell out their referent/condition join.

Supporting C* alone does not contradict C. No search result, failed access, missing mention or unsupported neighboring reading proves absence. Conflicting or incomplete applicable evidence stays UNDETERMINED with the missing fact stated. Treat a selected claim as contradicted only with an applicable contradiction witness; a proposed replacement requires its own applicable support. Both readings supported is a nondiscriminating result, not a forced error. If the deadline prevents a check, record UNDETERMINED/DEADLINE instead of guessing. Never extend the original deadline to finish the table.

Each record must contain: selected ID and draft locator; exact sentence/context and intended C; C* and one selector delta; evidence identity/excerpts/applicability; separate C and C* relation and explanation; proposed disposition (retain, scoped correction, qualify unresolved, or inapplicable); supported replacement if any; affected brief/plan obligation or decision. Continue the full ordinary critique for everything outside these records. Save critique.md and the ordinary source map before native Goal completion. Do not author or repair the final.

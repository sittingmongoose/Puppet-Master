## SCW records

### SCW-1 — FFmpeg advisory scope
**Draft locator / exact claim:** Proposal at a glance, ¶2; exact sentence is in the frozen selection. **C:** An unpatched FFmpeg 9.0.2 build must not be called safe for donated files because its MOV demuxer can recurse on many attacker-supplied PSSH boxes in crafted MP4. **C* / single selector delta:** Change only the applicability condition from crafted PSSH MP4 to any donated audio file. **E:** S06, exact URL/version/locator in the attached map. It describes crafted MP4 with tens of thousands of small PSSH boxes through 9.0.2; patched-version metadata is unknown. **C = ENTAILS**; **C* = UNDETERMINED**, since this advisory does not generalize the flaw to other media paths. **Disposition:** Retain with narrow scope. “Without a fix” is conditional; no fixed commit/release is established here. **Brief/plan:** compare stacks and describe support/security limits.

### SCW-2 — GStreamer advisory and packages
**Draft locator / exact claim:** Proposal at a glance, ¶2; exact sentence is in the frozen selection. **C:** The advisory's RTP raw-audio depayloader defect requires attacker-controlled SDP/RTSP negotiation, outside this local-file path; 1.28.8 fixes the described issue. **C* / single selector delta:** Change only subject/subsystem from the RTP depayloaders to the separate channel-position reordering helper and ask whether that helper is also confined to SDP/RTSP. **E:** S07/S10/S22 in the attached map. The official advisory describes the SDP-derived RTP condition and separately says a channel-reordering helper can access beyond an internal buffer above 64 channels; it does not specify that helper's invocation route. It says base/good 1.28.8 address the issue. **C = ENTAILS**; **C* = UNDETERMINED**: neither local-file reachability nor network-only confinement is established. **Disposition:** Qualify unresolved. Preserve the correct RTP-only distinction, add the separate helper condition and leave its route open pending caller/source review. The official download page listed macOS and Android 1.28.7 packages while the release index listed 1.28.8 stable, so verify the exact target bundle/modules before relying on the fix. **Brief/plan:** security and channel support boundaries; OS/package remain unknown.

## Ordinary critique: brief coverage

The proposal covers inconsistent suffixes, signature versus parser identity, per-stream metadata, duration provenance, two-channel audition, transcript association/revisions, permission holds, safe restart, accessible text status, and portable handoff. It correctly preserves originals, avoids cloud transcription and mandatory conversion, and does not choose a library without a versioned capability matrix. DROID is only a format identity signal. Missing transcripts remain neutral. The spreadsheet ID stays authoritative and filename normalization is candidate-only. The correction flow retains old transcript bytes and does not rerun audio intake.

**Materially incomplete — restart durability.** The proposed temporary copy, hash comparison, atomic rename and SQLite journal are directionally sound, but do not define a recoverable commit across payload and journal after a crash or power failure. Specify source mutation handling, durable flush/reconciliation, and how to handle an orphaned temp/final payload. SQLite's local-filesystem caveat is correctly supported, but the actual durability mode and recovery witness are not chosen or tested.

**Materially incomplete — GStreamer security/package scope.** The advisory contains a second channel-position helper condition above 64 channels. Its local-file reachability is unknown from the advisory; the draft must not dismiss or assert that route. The official download page also shows platform bundle lag from the 1.28.8 stable release. Keep GStreamer conditional on verifying the exact shipped base/good modules and reviewing the helper's callers.

**Minor wording.** “Missing plugins mean ... not that bytes are corrupt” could sound like proof of valid media. Change to “does not establish corruption.” The rest of the warning model is well separated.

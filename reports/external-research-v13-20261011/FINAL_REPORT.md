# ER13 final results

ER13 completed its finite 80-arm campaign. **No new optimizer is promoted:** the requested repeatable 20–30% saving, or 2× stretch, at comparable complete quality was not established. Retain the conventional three-context R0 reference. The two-context B02+B10 successor remains a testable option; the locked four-context successor did not deliver any of its four D treatment finals. This is research evidence, not canon adoption.

## What ran

The design contained two four-cell A factorial blocks, 24 B pairs, four C pairs and eight D pairs: **64 FULL and 16 COMPONENT arms**. All 199 actual candidate tasks/backing contexts ended with zero pending children and saved settlements (193 completed, six interrupted). There were 58 original FULL final freezes; six FULL finals were missing. All 16 component arms have retained artifacts, including two capped partial treatments that lack a complete final. There were 38 fresh paired/block original grader contexts, with no replacement original grading.

These are finite source labels, not 80 complete quality grades. Formal final axes are all COMPLETE for 56 arms, mixed COMPLETE/PARTIAL for 14, and all UNASSESSED for ten. Missing-final partial science, B10’s original evaluator infrastructure limitation, and its single separate coverage-completion review remain distinct. Source PASS33, complete-axis source PASS31 and strict delivered-usable UNKNOWN are separate observations. B11’s malformed required JSON and source/method/capture failures are retained.

All product science used requested GPT-6 Luna Max/Standard on Codex, with requested configuration recorded per stage. Muse Code and zcode had two charged route probes without qualified native Goal capability; their unstarted assigned slots were prospectively reassigned to Luna. The reserve used 2/16 starts, separate from the primary 80. Requested and observed model/configuration are distinguished; effective served effort/tier, billing and independent native-to-task linkage remain unproved. A user-authorized account change occurred prospectively during D; D6’s final control reviser used an isolated route-only fork. Earlier requests, clocks and scientific cards were unchanged; account/cache/service effects remain confounded.

## Measured effects and limits

The final supported metadata capture contains 264 contexts and 412 reported run records: 408 have start timestamps, 408 have end timestamps, and 406 have both. All 199 product and 38 original-grader contexts were closed. Root and the accounting correction were still active in their snapshot, so later review/publication/archive/cleanup is separate overhead. Product created-context residence sums to **133,129.859 seconds (36.98 context-hours)**; its union is 9,186.092 seconds. The experiment’s parallel swarm is not a production recipe. Residence, provider-turn wall, native intervals, inference and billing are different measures.

The quiet proxy is measured T0 to final-stage T3 completion, not exact scientific last-write or active inference. C1 auxiliary-freeze bounds and first R0’s unknown exact T0 remain separate. B03 retained waiting and B12 overlapping/forked work are charged. Failed/missing finals stay in assigned denominators; zero-pass quotients are undefined. Component preparation is a separate cold-use cost; its measured 9m23s segment excludes earlier unmeasured reads. No unrelated medians are added.

Saved native receipts identify 199 product births with 192 complete terminal observations. Their token subtotal is not a complete final token or billing total. Input/cache/output/reasoning billing decomposition, subscription affordability, independent physical session linkage and strict usable-pass counts remain UNKNOWN. The initial metadata collector used an unsupported run limit and lost its raw failures; the corrected finite capture and retained API diagnostic are documented without changing those earlier artifacts.

# Cancelable local document-preview jobs

A desktop reference manager generates previews and thumbnails for imported local documents. Large batches, quick item switches and edits can cause multiple obsolete render requests while newer visible items wait. Design a job component that keeps the UI responsive, favors current visible work without starving background imports, permits cancellation and shutdown, and never displays a stale preview as the current document. Expensive or unreliable format handlers may run in child processes; the app must recover from a crash or a job that stops making progress.

Research request identity, priority and resource limits, cancellation semantics, result/cache validity, persistence/restart boundaries and progress reporting. Compare viable mechanisms and useful real implementation history with platform and version conditions. We cannot assume all documents are small, trust every parser, require a GPU, upload documents, or install a machine-wide privileged service. Propose the component and its integration contract; a deployed renderer or universal performance benchmark is not required.

Research current primary documentation, governing implementation code and relevant real implementation history. Discover useful unfamiliar mechanisms and plausible alternatives without preselecting a product. After the one-shot release of our tentative exact plan, retain, change or reject its material choices with reasons. Deliver a complete self-contained proposal with source conditions, unresolved user-specific facts, trade-offs, and meaningful validation. Separate research or small checks actually executed from future validation proposed; building or deploying the future application is not required.

Complete all substantive research and planning obligations within a maximum of 60 elapsed minutes. Finish earlier when the complete output is ready. There is no minimum research duration, no requirement to consume the allowance, and no credit for waiting. The deadline includes arm-specific setup, provider queueing after admission, tools, native retries, handoffs, revision and delivery. Output quality is assessed independently of elapsed time.

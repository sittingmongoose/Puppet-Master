Design the in-process queue component of a desktop batch document converter using CPython 3.13.9 asyncio. Queue at most 20 waiting jobs and run four workers. Users need progress, a graceful stop that finishes accepted work, and a force-stop that abandons waiting work and reports it truthfully. Failures and cancellation must not become successful conversions. All jobs and output paths are owned by this component; external child-process behavior is a separate layer. Explain throughput/capacity estimates, accepted-versus-running counts, cleanup, alternatives and lifecycle invariants. Deliver a proposal with source-grounded reasoning and discriminating validation; neither arm is required to deploy a converter.

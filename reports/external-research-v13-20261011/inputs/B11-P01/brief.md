# Durable references to local project attachments

We maintain a cross-platform desktop project notebook. Add a component that lets a user attach references to existing local files without silently copying the files into our storage. Projects are moved between folders; attachments may be renamed or moved outside our app; some live on removable drives that are not always connected. Users need understandable missing-file states, deliberate relinking, and a way to move or export a project without implying that every linked file was included. Two editor windows can change a project's references.

Research how to separate a project's attachment record from a file's location and identity, how to find or verify a candidate replacement, what operating-system or filesystem differences matter, and how to preserve user control. Compare a simple portable approach with stronger mechanisms where their trade-offs are useful. We cannot require elevated privileges, an always-running whole-disk index, a cloud account, or automatic upload. We want a realistic component proposal and integration boundaries, not a general file manager or a promise to recover every file.

Research current primary documentation, governing implementation code and relevant real implementation history. Discover useful unfamiliar mechanisms and plausible alternatives without preselecting a product. After the one-shot release of our tentative exact plan, retain, change or reject its material choices with reasons. Deliver a complete self-contained proposal with source conditions, unresolved user-specific facts, trade-offs, and meaningful validation. Separate research or small checks actually executed from future validation proposed; building or deploying the future application is not required.

Complete all substantive research and planning obligations within a maximum of 60 elapsed minutes. Finish earlier when the complete output is ready.

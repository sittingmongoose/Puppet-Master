# Draft plan: Portable training handbook with accessible exercises

The draft represents lessons as versioned structured pages with headings, paragraphs, diagrams and exercise identifiers. Authors import supported text and review a rendered preview before creating a distribution package. Learners keep progress in a separate local store; no account or upload is required. Search includes topic terms and a current-or-retired label, while historical lesson versions remain inspectable.

An exercise keeps its identifier across revisions, and the draft initially carries its completed status forward unless the author manually flags a reset. That assumes authors can recognize every material change and that a simple boolean captures enough history. The proposal leaves a policy for edited exercises and retired-page search ranking unresolved. Package updates replace lesson assets but do not replace the learner store.

Keyboard navigation follows headings and explicit links. Diagram text alternatives are authored separately, and exports include a printable handout plus an offline readable version. The plan assumes a shared rendering pipeline can preserve reading order and text alternatives in both outputs; actual format and assistive-technology support need investigation. Imported word-processor formatting may be reduced to supported page structures, with losses shown to authors. Proposed validation covers update interrupted mid-install, progress migration, changed exercise meaning, retired-topic results, pasted tables and keyboard/screen-reader/print review. No lesson package has been published or learner data inspected. Adoption starts with synthetic lessons and one author workflow.

This is a provisional draft for review after the declared reveal, not an endorsed answer. Resolve material assumptions through applicable primary evidence; retain open user-data questions when they cannot be answered from sources. No future product deployment is requested.

Complete all substantive research and planning obligations within a maximum of 60 elapsed minutes. Finish earlier when the complete output is ready. There is no minimum research duration, no requirement to consume the allowance, and no credit for waiting. The deadline includes arm-specific setup, provider queueing after admission, tools, native retries, handoffs, revision and delivery. Output quality is assessed independently of elapsed time.

Propose a CPython 3.13.9 asyncio subprocess component for a desktop asset validator on Linux and Windows. Invoke a configured executable with separate arguments, stream both stdout and stderr, keep at most 1 MiB of in-memory log tail per job, and save complete logs up to a configured disk quota. Tool output may be arbitrarily large. Users need cancel/timeout, a clear terminal outcome and no orphaned owned child process. Do not treat a tool's nonzero exit as successful validation. Explain input handling, process and pipe lifecycle, Windows constraints, bounded storage, diagnostics, alternatives and realistic tests. A security sandbox and arbitrary descendant-process supervision are outside this initial component, but do not imply isolation you do not implement.

## Findings (reviewed 2026-09-26)

### F1 — `thread.branch`: unresolved applicability / source lineage (packet 2 — owner adjudication needed, NOT automatic admission or retirement)

- **Requirement:** `thread.spawn`, `thread.request`, `thread.await`, **and `thread.branch`** are durable logical operations with per-thread ordering, idempotency, and copy-on-write branching "where applicable" (`PM_Full_Thread.../02_FINAL_DECISION_REGISTER.md` §12, lines 185-188; coverage row `THR-001` in `12_COVERAGE_MATRIX.md`).
- **Current authority:** `orchestrator-subagent-integration.md:31646` and the command family (`UI_Command_Catalog.md:11487-11489`, `Commands_System.md:4067-4069`) enumerate and register **only** `thread.request`, `thread.spawn`, `thread.await`. `cmd.chat.branch_from_restore` exists but is a Chat restore-point operation, not an Orchestrator copy-on-write branch (`assistant-chat-design.md:1228-1242`). Current canon has not established that a general Orchestrator branch is applicable, its target identity, or its permission/lineage behavior.
- **Status:** open — unresolved applicability/source lineage. The packet's own "where applicable" qualifier means the absence of a `thread.branch` spelling proves neither a mechanical drop nor a retirement. Do NOT register `cmd.thread.branch` or silently retire the behavior on the strength of this audit.
- **Affected paths:** `Plans/orchestrator-subagent-integration.md` (thread-operation unit) + Shared Runtime owner — a one-line applicability pointer only after owner adjudication.

- **Current authority:** SIR-017 requires the gate families and evidence shape but freezes no numeric pass thresholds — correctly, since the numbers are provisional. No SIR-017 edit is proposed: writing them into acceptance criteria would convert provisional inputs into settled canon without evidence or an owner decision.
- **Status:** preserved-in-report as provisional calibration input. Not a canonical threshold, not a performance claim, not a runtime result.

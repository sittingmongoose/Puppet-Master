[S02, S03] | Product inference from notebook state model. | On source/dependency/environment changes mark all outputs from the interactive kernel session unverified. ## Preserved supported findings

- The nbformat 5.10.4 document format is appropriate for `.ipynb` interchange; its ordered cell list, execution counts and saved outputs are useful document state but not a complete execution history. [S01]
- nbclient is a plausible single-Python-kernel orchestration precedent for progress hooks, notebook-order execution and error/timeout handling, provided tag/error defaults are made explicit. It is not a sandbox. [S02]
- Jupyter message/session/parent IDs can correlate requests and output side effects and support a separate event ledger, but do not reconstruct all kernel state. [S03]
- DuckDB is an independent local SQL/tabular precedent. Its CSV/JSON/Parquet reader configuration and schema-inspection capabilities support the candidate architecture; they do not settle the product schema contract. [S04, S05, S13, S14]
- The issue → branch-specific fix → regression-test chain is real and consequential. The frozen proposal already respected the important v1.5.6 uncertainty and proposed exact-artifact testing; the final retains that useful gate and narrows the evidence claims further. [S07–S12]
- DuckDB streaming/spill documentation supports investigating a bounded path, not a blanket 5 GB guarantee. [S15, S16]
- Bubblewrap is a namespace/mount-construction precedent with caller-defined policy. The Linux-only, fail-closed execution choice remains reasonable if external resource enforcement and the actual policy are validated. [S17]
- Keep the data contract, read-only source handling, strict or explicit reject behavior, per-output provenance, SQL dialect label, ordinary folder exchange, schema-drift opportunity, JupyterLab alternative and non-goals from the frozen proposal. They remain useful choices rather than proven runtime behavior.

## Critical unresolved dependencies

The full compatible Python/Jupyter/DuckDB lock and wheel hashes are not resolved; no source hash-to-consumed-bytes adapter is tested; neither the CSV gate nor 5 GB target is executed; Bubblewrap plus cgroup/disk policy and Jupyter transport containment are untested; source adapters’ missing/null, conversion, evolution and row-ID behavior are untested; filesystem transaction/locking behavior is untested. They are explicit gates and proposed checks in the final proposal, not closed findings.

The sole executed check is W02, the isolated candidate-authored standard-library synthetic component test. It does not validate any of those dependencies or application behavior.

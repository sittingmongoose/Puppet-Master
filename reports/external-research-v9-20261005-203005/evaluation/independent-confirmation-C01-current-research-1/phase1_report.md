## Findings on A

**A-01 — MAJOR: decoded authority is excluded from exchange.** Proposal lines 36 and 140–145 make the decoded PCM cache the audio authority for compressed inputs and explicitly admit that decoding can differ across FFmpeg versions. Lines 97–100 exclude `cache/` from portable bundles while copying original sources. No mandatory bundled, hashed authoritative decode or bound decoder artifact reproducing that authority is defined. Thus an editor may exchange the original MP3/M4A but omit the exact PCM that the previous editor heard; the receiver must regenerate it under an unbound decoder, or cannot regenerate it. Recording a decoder identity and proposing future pin verification do not define a presently coherent exchange contract. This defeats the brief's heard-material provenance requirement (H1-02, H1-05, H1-09; Q1/Q3/Q6/Q7). The immutable-original policy, source hashes, and explicit decoder uncertainty remain valid and useful.

**A-02 — MAJOR: source anchors do not define project occurrences, and the boundary wording conflicts.** The clip schema at lines 49–51 permits multiple clips from the same media/range, but a cue has only a media/source-sample anchor; lines 58–60 require a particular clip's start/range to produce one project time. At equal source/project rates, two clips retaining source frames [0,1000) at project starts 0 and 2000 map the same source cue 250 to project frames 250 and 2250. No occurrence selection or explicit all-occurrences export policy is stated. Separately, line 62 says a cue at a butting exclusive end reads as the next clip's frame zero; when the next clip uses different media, that is a different source identity. The hidden/removed-boundary flags at lines 62/65 are helpful, but do not resolve these two meanings. This is a missing/contradictory implementable relationship, not evidence of a rendered failure (H1-02; Q1/Q3/Q6). Integer floor arithmetic itself remains a legitimate choice.

**A-03 — MEDIUM: Audacity external-dependency lesson exceeds the cited page.** Lines 45/98 attribute an external-audio dependency and a “Check dependencies” flow to the cited current AUP3 manual. A's source catalog explicitly records the dependency-dialog retrieval as 404 and bases the lesson on “references the original audio material.” The independently inspected page describes a unified single AUP3 project and internal disk-space/undo reuse; that phrase does not establish a reference-in-place external-file dependency or the claimed dialog. Preserve A's content-hash/relocation design, but this asserted source fact is unsupported by the selected evidence (H1-07/H1-10 research analogue; Q2/Q3). [Audacity project manual](https://manual.audacityteam.org/man/audacity_projects.html), local capture `independent-primary-captures/audacity-projects.html`, SHA-256 `6ed6a9c1e0ee2d8c2cc888eb48dbe6083c58d0ab49982006f2202dd3cc516ed8`, lines 189–200 and 256–262.

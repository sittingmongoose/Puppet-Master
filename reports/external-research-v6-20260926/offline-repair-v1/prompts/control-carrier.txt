Carrier: observations followed by a drafted report.

Save out/observations.md incrementally, one material observation per block:
### O-NNN
- source: source handle and lines actually read
- quote: verbatim source text
- claim: the supported proposition or explicitly qualified lead
- conditions: version/configuration/scope/exceptions
- implication: consequence for this project
- open question: uncertainty, contradiction or next check
Never delete an earlier block; qualify it in a later block naming the earlier ID. Preserve observations as they are recorded; do not reconstruct an earlier acquisition record from the final answer.

Then write out/draft.md as one coherent CURRENT report: supported propositions linked to observation IDs, exact conditions, Plan fit citing Plan lines, one disposition (correction, optional_capability, product_choice, covered, unsupported, unresolved), exact constraint, consequence and distinguishing validation proposal. Keep proposals explicitly unexecuted. Include current deferred items with reasons, unresolved disputes, and coverage (sources read, unvisited questions, unknowns). Current uncertainty is substantive report content, not historical material to discard.

Save superseded/removed/replaced material separately in append-only out/history.md, with identity, previous whole record, new disposition and change reason. Save chronological out/snapshots/NNNN.md copies of substantive draft revisions before replacing them. Do not put a historical appendix, superseded text, copied observation inventory or prior snapshots inside out/draft.md. Current source citations/quotes and a currently unresolved dispute belong in the current report; earlier abandoned versions belong in history. Never erase history. The goal is complete when out/draft.md is a complete current report and observations, available revisions and history are saved. If an earlier record was never saved, disclose that gap instead of inventing it later.

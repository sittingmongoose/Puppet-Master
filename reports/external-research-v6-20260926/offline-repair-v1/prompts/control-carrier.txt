Carrier: observations followed by a drafted report.

Save out/observations.md incrementally, one material observation per block:
### O-NNN
- source: source handle and lines actually read
- quote: verbatim source text
- claim: the supported proposition or explicitly qualified lead
- conditions: version/configuration/scope/exceptions
- implication: consequence for this project
- open question: uncertainty, contradiction or next check
Never delete an earlier block; qualify it in a later block naming the earlier ID.

Then write out/draft.md as one coherent report: supported propositions linked to observation IDs, exact conditions, Plan fit citing Plan lines, one disposition (correction, optional_capability, product_choice, covered, unsupported, unresolved), exact constraint, consequence and distinguishing validation proposal. Keep proposals explicitly unexecuted. Include deferred items with reasons, unresolved disputes, and coverage (sources read, unvisited questions, unknowns). Preserve removed/replaced material in an appendix with its identity and reason. The goal is complete when out/draft.md is a complete coherent report and observations are saved.

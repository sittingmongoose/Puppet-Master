## Question and matched conditions

One matched pair per application compares observations followed by a drafted
report against incrementally maintained complete findings and a deterministic
report rendered from those findings. The experimental variable is the combined
record-maintenance/report-production policy, not JSON syntax in isolation.

Both arms get the same common instructions, original case bytes, brief and
Viewer plan, catalog, source access, app/model/Max, fresh workspace and context,
1800-second and 160-response ceilings. No live sources, cross-arm outputs,
premium assistance, semantic host edits, evidence bundles, known answers,
reference IDs, historical evaluations or evaluator-selected clues are admitted.
The control carrier follows the original investigator policy; both arms get the
same generic absence, source-authority and unexecuted-proposal rules.

| Serial order | App | Policy | Assignment |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Muse Code / Muse 1.3 Contributor / Max | observations then draft | I1-M-control |
| 2 | same | maintained complete findings then render | I1-M-maintained |
| 3 | zcode / GLM 5.3 Flash / Max | maintained complete findings then render | I1-Z-maintained |
| 4 | same | observations then draft | I1-Z-control |

No best-of, retry, replacement slot, extra smoke test or account probe is in this
proposal. The order is counterbalanced between apps, but there is one pair per
app and no replication: report descriptive paired differences, not significance
or generalization. The familiar OME-Zarr case is a development case.

## Separate quality endpoints

Independent Opus 5.5 xhigh evaluation stays evaluator-only. Reserve two fresh
reviewers, one per app pair, each at most 2700 seconds and 160 responses. They
reuse the existing OME reference key, scoring guide and input-eligibility
limitations; no new answer key is authored here. Requested versus observed
effort is recorded separately. Astra/Sol development tests do not replace them.

Within each pair, assign X1/X2 privately. First assess final reports, lock those
judgments, then open upstream acquisition records and map preservation. Withhold
prompts, policy names, operational logs and treatment labels. The rendered format
may reveal treatment; record partial unblinding rather than claim perfect
blinding or alter substantive content to hide it. A renderer status UNVERIFIED
means no flash verifier has checked the finding; evaluator credit depends on
its meaning and source support, not that label.

1. Acquisition: eligible source-grounded complete findings and facets captured
   before final report production; novel supported findings separately; partial
   leads, uncertain propositions, unsupported claims, contradictions and missing
   eligible facets separately. Read counts are coverage activity, not quality.
2. Preservation: map each supported upstream finding/facet to final output as
   retained, narrowed, lost, contradicted or visibly unresolved; separately map
   conditions, implications, validation proposals and uncertainty. Use only the
   arm's supported upstream substance as the denominator; publish numerator and
   denominator, with N/A for zero. Do not count copied falsehoods as success.
3. Derivation: distinguish a recorded lead from an established implication and
   an adequate distinguishing test. A title, citation or test-shaped sentence
   does not by itself earn complete-finding or executed-validation credit.
4. Adverse outcomes: consequential false dismissal/confirmation, fabricated
   absence, unsupported specification override, lost material conditions or
   validation proposals, and silent approval of a product choice. Publish each;
   extra findings do not offset these losses.

Retain the reference whole-item statuses and facet-level losses separately.
Preservation may be mechanically high under a renderer while acquisition remains
weak or wrong. This is the hypothesis to test, not a promised quality improvement.

## Finite capacity and stop rules

Four candidate assignments: at most 7200 seconds and 640 parent/native responses.
Two evaluator assignments: at most 5400 seconds and 320 responses. Total scheduled
model span: 12600 seconds (3h30m), with a 14400-second phase wall ceiling including
host work. Host preparation/rendering cap: 300 seconds per slot within that phase.
Unused capacity is not transferred, and no extra assignment is admitted. Muse
reminder-child calls are recorded separately; their token cost remains unknown
where not exposed and they are not implied to be covered by a parent response
count. Existing driver time caps bound the containing assignment.

Quota or harness failure stops the schedule. A capped/incomplete semantic result
is retained and its paired slot may run within its original ceiling, as in the
existing policy; malformed model carriers are an arm result, not a retry reason.
A failed/incomplete evaluator gives no quality pass and no automatic replacement.
If evaluation cannot finish under the reserved capacity, retain partial findings,
report unassessed scope and stop for a separate decision.

Record per-arm goal wall time, host work, parent/child responses, exposed input,
cache, uncached input, output/reasoning tokens, source calls and unique sources,
repeat reads, rendered/record bytes, and tool-result bytes where available.
Candidate, evaluation and Astra/Sol development usage are separate ledgers;
unknowns remain unknown. Shared-account percentage changes are not attributed
to this experiment. No savings claim follows from fewer reads alone.

Advance only if the independent evaluations show no material preservation loss
or consequential false correction in the maintained arm, with acquisition at
least as strong by finding/facet identity and the measured time/usage tradeoff
reported. One pair cannot establish reliability. Even a promising I1 result
requires a separately authorized end-to-end comparison through a fresh,
same-app/same-flash-model corrected verifier before any end-to-end claim.
That later verifier is not one of the six proposed I1 assignments.

Method.
1. 2. Grade each delivered.md alone. Upstream-only material stays lost or narrowed.
3. Check the other direction: every material delivered claim that is unsupported, overstated or contradicted by the sources or Plan (counterevidence and consequence); false covered, unsupported or not-applicable dismissals; lost qualifiers, negation, versions or scope; internal contradictions. Verify decisive claims in case/sources, not by model authority. Keep bounded unknowns distinct from defects.

5. Only after steps 2-4 are saved: compare upstream/ with delivered.md for handoff loss (material observations acquired upstream but dropped or distorted in delivery). Record final coherence and decision burden (blocking decisions, nonblocking choices, clarification questions, redundant questions).
6. Coverage accounting: what you did and did not assess. Anything not assessed is PENDING_HIGH_END_VERIFICATION, never a pass.

# D-M16-A: One discovery bundle for two offline map topics

A small offline map viewer has two related sandbox topics sharing downloaded raster tiles. Topic 1: decide a bounded resumable tile-acquisition/refresh policy under intermittent links. Topic 2: decide a bounded stale-tile display/retention policy when a newer tile version exists. Investigate public primary docs/code; discover useful mechanisms for both and preserve topic-specific unknowns. This is one two-topic diagnostic bundle.

This is one bounded diagnostic module. Address these six material obligations; do not produce a full application plan:

1. Give a separate disposition for acquisition and refresh.
2. Give a separate disposition for stale display and retention.
3. Identify shared identity/freshness dependencies and reuse invalidation.
4. Preserve topic-specific uncertainties instead of assuming shared evidence settles both.
5. Return at most eight distinct material findings across the bundle.
6. Deliver a bounded topic comparison and discriminating checks, counting shared discovery and synthesis.

Sources mode: OPEN_DISCOVERY_BRIEF_ONLY. No selected sources, component names, answer-bearing bibliography, or designer conclusions are provided. Each arm selects its own primary docs/code during work. Freeze source identities as discovered and report versions/conditions. Do not add one arm's discovered corpus to the other.

Deliver one complete source-linked bounded recommendation with conditions, uncertainty, and proposed checks. Soft ceiling: 1100 words / eight material findings. Do not omit governing conditions to fit the soft ceiling. Supplied drafts, if any, are legitimate untrusted test inputs, not truth or evaluator judgments. No execution of downloaded project code or installers.

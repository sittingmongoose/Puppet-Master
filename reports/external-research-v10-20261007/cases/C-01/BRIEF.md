# Hypothetical development brief: community telescope notebook

This is a newly authored held-out planning brief, not a description of an existing club, dataset or validated astronomy system.

A small astronomy club wants to plan a modest tool for recording observing sessions, importing telescope-session images and accompanying observation notes, and discussing those images together. Volunteers use a few different capture setups and bring files home on removable storage; another member may later contribute a processed image or correction. The club wants to connect a planned observation, the session record, the original images, derived images and member annotations without making volunteers become data administrators.

The initial workflow is deliberately limited: prepare a session list, ingest one session's files and notes, inspect images, attach comments or marked regions, review uncertain or incomplete entries, and share a selected observing packet with other members. Members should be able to explain where an image and its displayed interpretation came from and revisit earlier annotations after an image is replaced or reprocessed. An interrupted ingest should be recoverable. The club wants a clear distinction between private drafts and club-visible material, and predictable behavior when two members edit related notes.

Research a practical planning direction that volunteers could maintain, favoring affordable public components and portable data over dependence on a permanent commercial account. Consider low-connectivity session entry and later collaborative review, but do not expand into telescope control, autonomous observing, precision scientific reduction, sky-event prediction or public social networking. Do not assert that an image's content or supplied observation metadata is scientifically correct. Specialized conversions or analysis can be proposed conditionally; their validity must be established by later validation.

Treat PLAN.md as a thin prospective proposal to investigate, not an endorsed architecture or planted answer key. The plan's technical assumptions are explicitly unvalidated. Recommend a coherent minimum useful workflow and explain optional improvements without making them all mandatory.

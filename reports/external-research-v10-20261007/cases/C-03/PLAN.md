# Frozen sandbox plan: small ensemble score desk

Status: thin prospective planning snapshot for a hypothetical small ensemble. This document contains proposals and unvalidated assumptions only. No listed behavior is a music-processing fact, component guarantee or established test result. It is read-only research input, not Puppet Master canon.

## B-P1 — Intended boundary

Proposal: begin with one ensemble, a librarian-led intake workflow and member rehearsal review. Import authorized pages/documents and optional editable notation; organize editions and parts, add personal/shared markings, choose a rehearsal list and export a packet. Composition, catalog acquisition and performance evaluation are outside the initial product.

## B-P2 — Library identity

Proposal: record a work, an edition within that work and a part within the edition, plus contributor and submitted provenance. Keep the original document and each replacement separately. Use supplied titles/instrument labels initially, with librarian correction before publication. Equivalence between differently titled files or similarly named editions has not been established.

## B-P3 — Digitization and rendering

Proposal: render a page preview and, where feasible, attempt an optional structured interpretation for passage navigation. Keep the original page available and require human review before calling interpreted content checked. Assume for initial discussion that an import/export path can connect structured notation to a useful preview; loss, layout differences, unsupported content and recognition reliability remain unvalidated. No recognition, rendering or conversion component is selected.

## B-P4 — Passage notes and personal markings

Proposal: support text notes and page-region marks, with private personal markings and director-published ensemble notes. Store a page number and a user-entered passage/measure label alongside a marking. Initially ask the librarian to reconnect shared notes across parts and recheck markings after page replacement. Whether those references are durable through different pagination, edition changes or notation conversion remains an open assumption.

## B-P5 — Rehearsal review and collaboration

Proposal: let the director select ordered works/passages and let each player download the relevant published parts before rehearsal. Keep annotations locally while disconnected and submit later. A first discussion could use the latest published librarian revision for the shared library while retaining earlier versions; reconcile concurrent note changes and prevent a player's personal marks from becoming shared without an explicit action. No collaboration mechanism is selected.

## B-P6 — Handoff, access and retention

Proposal: member and librarian/director roles, explicit publication and a downloadable rehearsal packet with a manifest of edition/part identities and chosen notes. Keep permission/provenance information entered by the librarian; the software does not assert ownership from a file's presence. Withdrawal, old downloaded packets, deleted personal notes, backups and replacement-part access need a practical policy. Prefer public, maintainable components without assuming all formats preserve all information.

## B-P7 — Validation to propose

Proposal only, not executed: use a small legitimate varied score set to examine import/render/optional interpretation, unchecked-versus-reviewed content, differently paginated parts, a replaced page with existing marks, disconnected rehearsal access, concurrent personal/shared notes and a packet re-open. Research should propose decisive observable checks and limitations without building the tool or inventing musical-recognition ground truth.

# Hypothetical development brief: small ensemble score desk

This is a newly authored held-out planning brief, not a description of an existing ensemble, score collection or validated music-processing system.

A small musical ensemble wants to plan a modest rehearsal-library tool. Members bring scanned paper parts, phone photographs, digital score documents and sometimes editable notation exports for music they are allowed to use. The group wants a librarian to organize works, editions and instrumental parts, connect page images with a useful structured representation when feasible, and prepare a clearly labeled rehearsal packet. Musicians should retain personal pencil-like markings while the director can publish shared rehearsal notes.

The initial workflow is limited to importing a small authorized score set, reviewing any digitization result, linking parts to the selected edition, annotating passages, preparing a rehearsal list and handing out a portable packet. Members need to tell which edition and part they are looking at, whether an automatically interpreted passage has been checked, and what happens to their markings if pages are replaced or a different part version is uploaded. A rehearsal note should remain understandable when two parts paginate differently. Do not assume perfect recognition, identical measure labeling or interchangeable notation/rendering representations.

Research a practical planning direction volunteers could maintain, favoring affordable public components and portable outputs over dependence on a permanent commercial account. Include sensible use when a rehearsal room has poor connectivity and later shared review. Do not expand into a public score marketplace, acquisition of copyrighted catalogs, automatic music composition, polished engraving replacement or live audio-performance assessment. No private score collection is supplied; any examples used for isolated research checks must be legitimate and their permissions explicit.

Treat PLAN.md as a thin prospective proposal to investigate, not an endorsed architecture or planted answer key. Its technical assumptions are explicitly unvalidated. Recommend a coherent minimum useful workflow and preserve optional improvements without making every opportunity mandatory.

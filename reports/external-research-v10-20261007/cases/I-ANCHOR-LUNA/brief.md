# Field inspection system: fresh integrated ER10 case

A regional water utility needs an offline-first inspection application for crews who work without connectivity for several days. Crews may concurrently annotate the same asset, attach photographs and location readings, and later synchronize over unreliable mobile links. Supervisors need a web review view and reliable history explaining whose edits arrived and what conflicts remain. Use affordable public components and avoid assuming a permanent third-party SaaS service. Investigate approaches the thin plan misses, including analogous systems beyond direct competitors. This is a planning research proposal, not an implementation assignment.

Research obligations: choose and inspect genuine primary public sources; compare at least two substantially different mechanisms and explain the important alternatives; investigate actual component/code behavior at a pinned version; trace a pertinent issue, fix and regression test through release applicability, or explicitly justify why an equivalent implementation history is the useful evidence; cover conflict handling, duplicate/reordered delivery, retention/deletion, access control, attachment behavior and observable tests without inventing guarantees; compare against the frozen plan; preserve product choices, useful optional opportunities, justified uncertainties and validation proposals; provide a complete source-grounded proposed-change artifact after same-family criticism. Distinguish proposed tests from executed ones. No canon edits, purchases or unrelated services.

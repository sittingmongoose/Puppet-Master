# Community archive digital preservation

A community archive has small digitization stations and a volunteer-managed public catalog. It ingests photographs, oral-history audio and scanned documents, keeps original bytes and derivatives, deduplicates safely, supports rights/restricted access, and must detect silent corruption and recover from interrupted ingest or failed storage. Sources may contain sensitive originals, so public browse and internal preservation differ. Discover affordable public mechanisms, analogous systems and hidden risks before comparing against the thin plan.

Integrated obligations: real primary public-source selection from the brief; compare materially different mechanisms and useful alternatives; inspect actual component/code behavior at a pinned version; investigate a pertinent issue/fix/regression test and its release applicability, or justify an equivalent implementation history; compare every relevant discovery against the frozen sandbox plan; preserve supported corrections, product choices, already-covered dispositions, optional discoveries, justified uncertainties and useful validation proposals; complete same-family criticism and final proposal. Distinguish executed checks from proposals. No product implementation, canon/main/WorkNodes, accounts or purchases.

# ER10 methods and interpretation

Working documentation. Four logical slots remain in execution or assessment; this document does not close the campaign or alter any original grade.

The prebound design has 40 logical comparison slots and 80 control/treatment arms: 24 targeted cases covering sixteen techniques, twelve complete integrated cases, and four locked confirmation repetitions. An arm may contain a common seed, multiple candidate Goals and handoffs. Prospective successors, failed stages and independent evaluations remain recorded attempts of the same logical slot.

All candidate pairs use the same prebound family, effort, account, brief and relevant source access except for the declared treatment. Codex candidates use Luna Max on `codex_gmail`; Muse uses Contributor Max through Muse Code; GLM uses Flash Max through ZCode. Root and independent Sol evaluation use the same authorized Codex account. Candidate native Goal state, T3 task terminal state and artifact delivery are separate observations.

Initial targeted envelopes were generally fifteen minutes. Named final prospective versions use longer matched envelopes when frozen before scientific starts; their success cannot be reported as meeting the original ceiling. All earlier failures, timing overruns, missed activation receipts and partial outputs remain in the attempt history. A later complete version is the authorized latest version, without choosing the best scientific result across attempts.

Integrated topology anchors compare competent three-stage controls against two-stage finalizing critics. Method cases test declared recipes under the same full application brief. The speed track locks 45-minute controls against 22.5-minute treatments with full obligations unchanged. Its results do not change the separate confirmation recipe, which was prospectively locked before held-out case content was opened. Confirmation has two domains with two independent repetitions each, with fixed order and no result-driven tuning.

Independent Sol reviewers receive frozen scientific outputs, the original brief and their exact primary-source evidence. They receive no economic advantage, expected winner, counterpart grade or parent analysis. Reviews start after the exact candidate pair is frozen and quiet; explicitly authorized failed-native or missing-final exceptions admit only diagnostic assessment of existing material. Those exceptions do not qualify native lifecycle, reconstruct absent artifacts, or give a missing output a speed advantage. Visible method labels, advisory filesystem scopes and disclosed prior observations limit blinding.

Reporting keeps five questions separate: whether the native pipeline was observed; whether a usable final was delivered; which declared scientific and primary-source obligations were assessed; whether process/provenance requirements were established; and what latency/work/usage was measured. A source-correct bounded judgment does not certify an unobserved discovery chronology, runtime behavior or historical execution. Two FAIL grades do not establish equal quality, and differently grouped defect counts are not a ranking.

Latency uses its stated actual boundary. Aggregate occupied stage time includes nested work; overlapping work is not added to a critical-path wall interval. Shared seeds are charged fully to each cold arm and once to aggregate campaign work. Queue, handoff, interrupted delivery, failed attempts, independent evaluation and host preparation remain distinct. Renderer execution already inside an arm is not added twice; helper development and unknown qualification costs remain separate cold/amortized fields.

SDK usage is selected recorded telemetry joined to exact owned native sessions. Input, cached input, output and reasoning subsets remain distinct. Cumulative counters are differenced or selected once per session; native Goal counters are not SDK-generated output or billing. Candidate method work, independent review, coordination, operational helpers and unattributed work have separate totals and coverage. Missing telemetry, historical identity gaps, root partial overhead, remaining provider quota and billing remain explicit unknowns.

Reproduction preserves the original tasks/configs, candidate outputs or recorded absences, frozen source locators and byte identities, executed witnesses, original independent judgments and attempt metrics. Lawfully published minimal evidence and a single justified private fragment archive have different access scopes. A hash does not reconstruct a missing mutable source. Scoped source-cache deletion follows verified GitHub retention and essential witness preservation; remaining reproduction limitations are reported.

## Material defect D04

The candidate offers `read_masks(class_band) != 0` **or** inversion of `read(masked=True).mask`, then specifies `valid_count = count(mask != 0)` (semantic lines 59 and 95; final lines 66 and 96). The first path is sound. The second needs an additional shape condition.

Rasterio 1.3.10’s released [`_io.pyx`](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rasterio/rasterio/1.3.10/rasterio/_io.pyx), raw lines 631–673, explicitly supplies `np.ma.nomask` when all band flags are `all_valid`. Its band-selection rules are at 438–450 and 515–520. The [NumPy 1.26 getmaskarray definition/example](https://numpy.org/doc/1.26/reference/generated/numpy.ma.getmaskarray.html#numpy.ma.getmaskarray) distinguishes scalar no-mask from an array matching the data shape; the [mask guide](https://numpy.org/doc/1.26/reference/maskedarray.generic.html#modifying-the-mask) also describes this scalar representation.

Analytical counterexample: an allowed all-valid 2×2 source band can have scalar no-mask. Inverting that scalar produces one truthy validity value. Counting its nonzero entries yields one, whereas there are four valid cells. Multiplication by affine cell area carries that error into the valid-area summary. This is a deduction from primary release/documentation facts, **not an executed raster test**. Selecting the band explicitly alone does not eliminate the scalar case.

The missing condition is to retain the selected-band mask array path, or normalize the selected-band masked read with `np.ma.getmaskarray(data)` (or equivalent expansion to the band grid) before inversion/counting. An all-valid no-nodata 2×2 case and a multiband shape case are useful proposed checks. The candidate already proposes full/empty-mask validation, but no passing result establishes this condition. This defect affects one offered construction path; it does not invalidate the conditional majority/nearest comparison or the preferred mask-read path.

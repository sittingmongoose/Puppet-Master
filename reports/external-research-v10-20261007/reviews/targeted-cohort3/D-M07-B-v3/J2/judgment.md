**F3 / C3 / P3 (final lines 13 and 29): real error-channel support, consequential guard defect.** P:764–766 and 893–895 document infinity for transformation errors and exceptions with `errcheck=True`. The final permits an `errcheck` guard as an alternative to `isfinite`.

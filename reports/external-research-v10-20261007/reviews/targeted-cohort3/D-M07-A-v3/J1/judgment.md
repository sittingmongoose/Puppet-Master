**Finding 2 / draft item 2 — core locking accepted; absolute mechanism claim rejected.** C sqlite3WalBeginWriteTransaction L3690–3736 acquires WAL_WRITE_LOCK and rejects a changed read header with SQLITE_BUSY_SNAPSHOT. **Finding 3 / draft item 3 — supported mechanism, narrower crash condition required.** C L139–143 identifies a transient wal-index. walIndexRecover L1374–1403 takes recovery-related exclusive locks; L1482–1518 reconstructs entries from validated WAL frames and records commit boundaries, and L1560–1588 rebuilds headers/read marks.

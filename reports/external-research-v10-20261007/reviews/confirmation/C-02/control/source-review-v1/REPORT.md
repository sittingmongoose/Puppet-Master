## Material finding

**D01 — Processing-method provenance was dropped from the integrated final without a disposition.**

- **Exact inputs/claim locations:** researcher-v1/artifact.md **line 39** explicitly records a derivative's source asset(s), producing tool/version, parameters/settings, producer, creation time and output hash. Critic-v1/artifact.md **line 41** retains that processing record; **line 58** directs carrying the provenance model forward. Final reviser-v1/artifact.md **lines 12, 28–34, 44–47, 63, 76, 80, 85 and 93** retain general catalog/export “provenance,” derivative identity/links, import attribution, selected plane/stretch and preview transforms, but never specify the producing tool/version/settings record or an explicit unknown processing-method state. Final preservation_check.md's O6/O7 completeness assertion therefore exceeds what its standalone proposal retains.
- **Governing requirement:** frozen BRIEF.md paragraphs 1–2 require connecting originals/derived images and explaining where an image and its displayed interpretation came from. **A-P2** retains processed-image lineage; **A-P6** requires usable provenance in handoff. O6 and Source axis S5 require preserving substantive accepted decisions/conditions into the final. This is a provenance-record requirement, not a demand to perform scientific reduction or verify a contributor's processing claims.
- **Independent primary evidence:** [W3C PROV-O §3.1](https://www.w3.org/TR/2013/REC-prov-o-20130430/#description-starting-point-terms) distinguishes an output entity, the activity that generated it, inputs it used, and responsible agents. It also permits a simpler derivation link when the activity is unknown or irrelevant. Neither PROV-O nor this review requires RDF or a particular provenance standard.
- **Reasoning and consequence (review inference):** preserving the submitted derivative's bytes, parent link and import receipt cannot recover which processing method/settings produced it. Parser version describes metadata interpretation; preview stretch/transform describes display preparation; neither is the external derivative's production record. The final has not explicitly deferred or declared that record unknown. A packet could meet its stated hash/ID/plane checks while dropping the predecessor's processing explanation. That loses a consequential accepted planning decision and weakens the brief's explainability/handoff objective. Parent links themselves are preserved; this finding does **not** claim that all lineage is absent.
- **Classification:** material preservation and integrated-provenance coverage loss; S1/S5 and O6/O7 fail. It is not a claim that a selected component malfunctioned. No candidate rewriting or feedback was performed.

Three substantially different mechanisms and multiple outside analogies remain; removing these optional leads alone is nonmaterial. D01 concerns the retained processing-record decision, not mandatory adoption of PROV-O.

No incorrect rejection of PouchDB based on #8298, of W3C interoperability generally, or of valid center-coordinate APIs was found. Withdrawal of the earlier ambiguous rectangle phrase is justified; the new local schema remains a proposal. Processing provenance is the material exception. Source identities for retained consequential claims are resolvable; source-count totals were never quality criteria.

Not choosing a database/auth/decoder/host, not claiming release-specific test equivalence, and leaving retention/operator/observation meaning/signature needs to club evidence are **justified abstentions/unresolved product choices**.

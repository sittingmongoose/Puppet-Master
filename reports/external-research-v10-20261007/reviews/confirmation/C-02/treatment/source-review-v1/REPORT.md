## Material findings

**D-01 — FITS missing-scale rule omits the governing default.** F:13,39,58,90 and final preservation check's O3 assertion concern A-P2. F:58 instructs: “do not infer UTC if absent or ambiguous.” FITS 4.0 §9.2.1 defines UTC when TIMESYS is absent; §4.4.2.2 also supplies date-era defaults and distinguishes legacy two-digit DATE-OBS. Absence alone therefore does not establish ambiguous format semantics. Keeping raw values and refusing to certify observation correctness are appropriate, but neither removes the documented interpretation. The final does not distinguish a deliberately stricter no-normalization policy from the standard-derived default. This is a material missing applicability condition in the proposed time interpretation, not a demand to trust a device clock or silently rewrite headers. Evidence **E04**, R05, PDF printed pp.13,37; extracted lines 1094–1121 and 2734–2756. [FITS Standard 4.0](https://fits.gsfc.nasa.gov/standard40/fits_standard40aa.pdf).

6: WebKitGTK 2.54.1 released!
7: This is the first bug fix release in the stable 2.54 series.
8: What’s new in the WebKitGTK 2.54.1 release?
9: Allow pasting in-memory image data from clipboard.
10: Add User-Agent quirk for Amazon Luna.
11: Align the filter surface with the device pixel grid.
12: Fix rendering of scaled or transformed no-repeat background images.
13: Fix rendering of preserve-3d layers when a layer crosses the camera plane.
14: Fix SkiaCompositingLayer filter rendering under transforms.
15: Do not rasterize the part of a tile that the clip drops.
16: Skip non-composited frames when damage is empty.
17: Fix text deleted by input method delete-surrounding in contenteditable.
18: Fix several crashes and rendering issues.
19: Thanks to all the contributors who made possible this release.

## 2. Source-condition audit (consequential conditions per source, for the reviser to carry)

- S1 (ZIM): MD5 checksum is integrity-only and unauthenticated — trust must come from signing (S7), never from the checksum alone; namespaces hidden since 2021 means readers URL-decode before libzim; chunk-splitting only between clusters. S1 carries no single spec date (major 6, minor 3 at retrieval) — cite as such.
- S2 (FTS5): requires `SQLITE_ENABLE_FTS5` at build time; trigram substrings <3 chars never match (documented condition, not a defect); unicode61 diacritics default is remove_diacritics=1 with the U+1ED9 exception, `remove_diacritics=2` is the documented fix (as the draft correctly states); no CJK segmentation without trigram/custom/`fts5_locale`.
- S3 (DOMPurify): 3.1.x–3.4.6 pages unfetched (U3) — the "continuous chain" claim is bounded to 3.4.7–3.4.16 as summarized on the fetched page; tags GPG-verified (key 5F0CDACD24BB6BF4); no CVEs shown.
- S4 (WCAG 2.2): Rec 12 Dec 2024; SC 4.1.1 removed in 2.2 — a final proposal that still cites 4.1.1 would be citing a superseded criterion; kiosk-relevant AA additions (2.4.11, 2.5.7, 2.5.8 24×24, 3.2.6, 3.3.7, 3.3.8) and standing criteria are correctly enumerated; 2.4.13 Focus Appearance is AAA, not AA.
- S5 (rename(2)): atomic-replace guarantee quotes are about path replacement generally; the bounded notes do not establish that a plain `rename()` may replace a non-empty target directory (F3, the load-bearing finding of this critique — see §3). NFS voids atomicity — kiosks must swap on local disk. Brief both-resolve window exists.
- S6 (zipfile): `extract`/`extractall` sanitize but `zipfile.Path` does not; containment recipe is abspath+commonpath; re-extraction overwrites silently; ZIP-bomb exhaustion named. Language-pinning caveat F4 applies.

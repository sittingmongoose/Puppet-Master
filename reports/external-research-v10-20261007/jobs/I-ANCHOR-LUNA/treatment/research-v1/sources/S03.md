## Bounded excerpt

> PUT, DELETE, and safe request methods are idempotent.

## Research use

PUT to a client-chosen stable event URI is a sound retry shape if repeated same-content writes have the same intended effect.

## Limits

The server’s semantics must enforce this; RFC method properties do not provide event deduplication or exactly-once delivery.

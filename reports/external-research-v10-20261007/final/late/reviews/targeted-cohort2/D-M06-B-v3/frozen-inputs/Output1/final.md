# D-M06-B final — affine raster coordinates and inverse applicability (control arm, candidate v3)

Recommendation for a GDAL 3.9.0 raster query module receiving six-coefficient geotransforms, including rotated/sheared and singular cases. Sources: the frozen GDAL 3.9.0 Geotransform Tutorial (`geotransform.rst`) and released `gcore/gdal_misc.cpp`, byte-verified (sha256) against `INPUT_MAP.json`; identities, versions, and line locators in `sources.json`. The common seed draft and fixtures were treated as untrusted inputs. **Witnessed** = stated by those frozen bytes; **proposed** = asserted here on candidate authority; **executed corroboration** = observed by the sandboxed witness (`witness-receipt.json` — process evidence only: offline single-process run, isolation enforced, exit 0, 22/22 checks on the tested fixtures; it upgrades nothing to witnessed).

## Material findings

**F1 — Binding: coefficient order, units, origin (witnessed).** GT(0) x-origin, GT(1) w–e pixel resolution, GT(2) row rotation, GT(3) y-origin, GT(4) column rotation, GT(5) n–s pixel resolution, negative for north-up (geotransform.rst 15–20); forward equations X_geo = GT(0) + p·GT(1) + l·GT(2), Y_geo = GT(3) + p·GT(4) + l·GT(5) (27–28). gt[0], gt[3] and all scale/rotation terms carry CRS units (metres or degrees); p, l are unitless pixel/line offsets; det carries (CRS units/pixel)². *Condition:* any six-coefficient GDAL-style geotransform. *Uncertainty:* a 1-based-indexed draft transposes the matrix silently — confirm indexing basis before any comparison. *Proposed check:* transcription control (literal formula vs matrix form) — executed, exact agreement over 8×6 cases.

**F2 — Corners vs centers (witnessed distinction; caller duty proposed).** Continuous (p, l) space has origin at the top-left corner of the top-left pixel; its center is (0.5, 0.5) (geotransform.rst 30–32). The world-file loader converts a pixel-center convention to the corner origin by subtracting half of GT(1), GT(2) from GT(0) and half of GT(4), GT(5) from GT(3) (gdal_misc.cpp 2189–2193). *Proposed:* callers holding integer indices must map (col+0.5, row+0.5) before querying; feeding integers tests the corner grid. *Uncertainty:* intent is not recoverable from coefficients alone. *Executed corroboration:* world(0.5,0.5) − world(0,0) = 0.5·(GT1+GT2, GT4+GT5) to 2.4e-15; corner ≠ center for every nonconstant map.

**F3 — Rotation/shear is the general case; north-up is special (witnessed).** GT(2), GT(4) are first-class rotation terms, only "typically zero" (geotransform.rst 17, 19); north-up (both zero) is the named special case (34–38). *Proposed:* assess any algorithm on the full 2×2 block gt[1], gt[2], gt[4], gt[5]; hard-coding GT(2)=GT(4)=0 fails rotated/sheared inputs by construction. Rotation vs shear is read from the whole block (orthogonal columns vs not), not GT(2)'s sign. *Executed corroboration:* rotated fixture's block is orthogonal (adjugate inverse equals transpose/det); sheared fixture is not (scale-relative ratio 0.882).

**F4 — Invertibility criterion (proposed math; degenerate behavior executed).** The forward map is affine with matrix A = [[GT1, GT2], [GT4, GT5]]; the inverse exists iff det = GT1·GT5 − GT2·GT4 ≠ 0. Both degenerate fixtures give det exactly 0.0 in binary floating point, and the guard refuses them before any division (executed). *Condition:* reject |det| = 0 exactly; do not attempt a pseudo-inverse in this module.

**F5 — Tolerance must be scale-relative (proposed; concrete value unresolved).** Near-singular inputs are unstable: the near-singular fixture has det ≈ 2e-6 but scale-relative ratio r = |det| / (|GT1·GT5| + |GT2·GT4|) ≈ 1e-6 and inverse amplification ≈ 2e6 — formally invertible, numerically untrustworthy. An absolute floor alone misjudges scale: the geographic fixture has |det| = 1e-6 yet r = 1 (perfectly conditioned). *Proposed:* invertible iff r > 1e-9; flag ill-conditioned if r ≤ 1e-4. *Uncertainty:* no tolerance value is witnessed in any source; these figures are engineering choices that must be justified per dataset — carried as unresolved.

**F6 — Independent controls, not mutual round-tripping (proposed method; partially witnessed basis).** A forward routine and its inverse can be consistently wrong together, so round-tripping is weak evidence (executed: 36 round-trips agree to 3.3e-10 — corroboration only). Required controls: (a) derive the inverse independently by elimination from the two witnessed forward equations (adjugate), never by inverting the forward routine; (b) hand-computed single-point expectations with derivations (executed: 13 of 13 matched); (c) an algebraic identity on structured blocks — transpose/det where columns are orthogonal (executed); (d) an external trusted operation: the fit-from-GCPs path GDALGCPsToGeoTransform is witnessed to exist (gdal_misc.cpp 1811, 2006) but is not executable in the offline sandbox, so it remains a recommended external control, not an executed oracle. *Open:* whether the reviewed module may invoke such controls is a design decision outside this module.

**F7 — Witnessed/proposed separation and applicability (proposed discipline).** Witnessed here: the six-coefficient binding, forward equations, corner/center text, north-up special case, world-file half-pixel shift, and the existence of GCP-fit call sites (citations above). Proposed here: the inverse pseudocode, all thresholds, the caller +0.5 duty, and the control policy. *Applicability:* valid for six-coefficient affine geotransforms in any CRS over continuous pixel/line space; not applicable to polynomial or RPC georeferencing; executed on the tested fixtures only — GDAL itself was never executed, so these results support, but do not by themselves license, full forward/inverse claims.

**F8 — Extent is caller policy (proposed, with executed note).** The affine map is total on R²: out-of-extent queries ((−1,−1), (10.5,5.5)) returned finite values. Containment enforcement belongs to the caller, not the coordinate math; a query module must not reject such inputs as mathematical errors.

## Recommendation

Adopt the general affine forward/inverse pair with the F4/F5 guard, require F6's controls before any numerical rollout, and keep F2's convention decision explicit at the caller boundary. Remaining open items: per-dataset tolerance justification (F5) and external GCP-fit control availability (F6). Full method evidence, substeps, and timings: `report.md`; source identities and locators: `sources.json`.

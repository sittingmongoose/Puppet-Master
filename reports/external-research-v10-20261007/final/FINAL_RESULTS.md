# ER10: research acceleration under a full quality guard

This final edition closes the original finite queue of 40 comparison slots and 80 logical arms. Closed means an honest terminal disposition, including incomplete and unassessed work. It does not mean 80 source-correct outputs or 40 qualified experiments. **No repeatable, quality-preserving twofold speedup or affordability improvement was established.** The original independent judgments, missing observations, failed attempts and revised prospective budgets are retained.

The strongest practical evidence is narrower: coherent batching sometimes reduced repeated work substantially while preserving conditional recommendations; fewer substantive handoffs sometimes produced a better final; amendment-based preservation had opposite outcomes in its two targeted cases. Those observations support bounded trials with explicit conditions. They do not justify replacing the full research scope, declaring equal quality from two FAILs, or deploying a general faster recipe.

## Single-account recommendation

Use a complete scoped research pass followed by substantive criticism and a final that preserves every material condition and product obligation. A critic may author that final when the scope fits and its correction/preservation dispositions remain explicit; this is a conditional workflow choice, not a proven twofold upgrade. For two or three coherent related questions, batching is the most promising narrow latency observation, provided per-question answers and shared conditions are checked. Exact own-arm byte caches and mechanical views can reduce duplication or synchronization risk, but should not be sold as measured quota savings here.

Prioritize source-condition and applicability checks over multiplying scouts. Keep supported discoveries, optional ideas, uncertainty, existing-coverage decisions and proposed versus executed validations distinct. A small trusted execution must discriminate between right and wrong controls; a self-consistent wrong formula is not validation. Preserve a writing reserve within the actual cold deadline and use a delivery path the author can reliably satisfy.

For a normal subscriber's one project, start conservatively with one researcher and its successor; admit parallel complementary work only when its whole cost and reconciliation are justified. Experiment fan-out used additional concurrency to finish the finite queue. It does not establish a normal user's single-account latency, quota headroom, or affordability. Sol independent evaluations are measured experiment cost, not free production rescue, and their findings were not fed back to scored candidates.

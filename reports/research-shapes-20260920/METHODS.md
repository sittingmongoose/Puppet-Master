# Phase 1 screen methodology

This document records the method fixed before scoring. Results and verdicts remain pending. `preparation.json` is the historical prelaunch checkpoint, with the hashes of the accepted native qualification, participant freeze, scoring freeze, source copies, cost policy, and public schedule commitment.

## Scheduled comparisons

The main screen schedules two predeclared replicates of A, B, and C on thin OME-Zarr, followed by two replicates of each on the bounded Jujutsu case: twelve slots. The hidden schedule randomizes configurations within each case. Every slot, failure, and missing comparison is retained.

| Configuration | Frozen execution shape |
|---|---|
| A | The copied bounded-campaign scheduler, with its readiness and handoff rules, connected to the common native executor and host tools. OME-Zarr starts fresh discovery, implementation, history, reconciliation, and comparison work; Jujutsu starts from the 88 inherited leads. A common independent verifier follows the upstream campaign. |
| B | One investigator, followed by the common independent verifier. |
| C | A brief-led scout and a Plan-led scout with isolated acquisition views, followed by an assessor after the scout inventories are sealed, then the common independent verifier. |

Jobs execute serially in all configurations. The model is `gpt-6-astra`, effort `xhigh`, service tier `default`. The source universe, shared host tools, executor, qualification, and limits are frozen. This measures the configurations in this native harness; it does not establish behavior in other harnesses or with another tool surface.

Phase 1A has four separate slots: reconciliation followed by comparison twice, and combined assessment twice, using the same frozen evidence and Plan context. Phase 1B has nine separately labeled oracle-assisted probes: normal retrieval, an evaluator-selected sufficient-source bundle without an answer, and a different output organization for each of label-value metadata identity, terminal owner receipts, and Jujutsu garbage-collection retention. Component work and diagnostic findings do not enter main arm scores.

## Native execution and limits

The original frozen lab is preserved. The native generation uses a physical copy of its admitted inputs plus separately frozen native execution code and prompts. The installed Codex runtime, prompts, tool schemas, fixed model catalog, source files, budget policy, and production qualification are bound by hashes.

The shared tool surface has eight host research functions and three qualified inert declarations. The fixed job-local model catalog controls tool routing identically in every slot. Qualification inspected native and proxy request boundaries; provider request bodies are not rewritten. The child filesystem and TCP boundaries, startup exceptions, and residual limitations are recorded in the external qualification evidence. This is a bounded execution claim, not a claim that every possible operating-system communication channel is isolated.

The campaign policy allows $1,500 of captured usage and 216,000 processing seconds. Phase 1A and 1B together have a $250 cap. Each job has at most 160 responses and 3,600 seconds. Inherited per-run limits are $250, twelve admissions including a reserved verifier, and 14,400 processing seconds. Processing time uses the active-run wall-interval definition in `cost-policy.json`; development and evaluation timing are reported separately.

One campaign holds the account lease. Every job requires a fresh typed rate-limit check. A warning or unavailable required account state holds dispatch. There are no automatic paid retries, account resets, credit purchases, or best-of selections. Unknown research-response usage retains a reserve and holds further dispatch. Captured parent/helper usage is imported incrementally into the allowance without charging overlapping cumulative intervals twice.

## Blind semantic scoring

The root evaluator scores at high effort. Launch, monitoring, hashing, and mechanical tables use Medium helpers. The evaluator can see opaque run IDs, the case, and the delivered semantic artifact. Configuration mappings, private execution structure, costs, and clocks remain hidden until all sixteen main/component scores, or explicit unscorable outcomes, are locked. Semantic text and neutral handoff structure can still permit configuration inference; that residual limitation is retained.

Each score concerns one coherent verifier-delivered artifact. A partial delivery keeps its gaps. An absent delivery is unscorable; upstream drafts do not become a synthesized arm result. The evaluator preserves an immutable first semantic pass before reading neutral upstream handoffs, then records acquisition, interpretation, Plan connection, preservation, and contradiction resolution separately. Upstream-only findings remain absent or narrowed in the delivered score.

The fixed references are eight non-exhaustive OME-Zarr controls and 110 historical Jujutsu findings. Historical classifications require revalidation against the admitted frozen source and Plan snapshots. Every reference receives an exact retained, narrowed, lost, or contradicted status with reasoning. Materially mistaken references retain their identity and receive explicit adjudication; missing decisive sources remain visible limitations. Findings are never combined into a net score.

Unsupported assertions, novel supported discoveries, correct non-findings, investigated coverage, handoff fidelity, and decision burden remain separate. Code claims require inspection of pinned implementation and relevant callers. Each decisive source check records the source identity, URI/version, file hash, line interval, and excerpt hash. Source/schema inspection does not certify runtime behavior. Evaluator-created deductions receive no participant discovery credit.

Jujutsu coverage retains all 88 inherited intake leads, including four unresolved-intake entries, and the twelve historical missing-source-directory limitations. Intake presence, headings, catalog hits, and inherited job counts are not proof of investigation. Blocking decisions, nonblocking choices, clarification questions, and redundant questions retain their substance and counts. Clarification questions require an explicit supplemental evaluator record because the frozen score schema has only blocking, nonblocking, and redundant decision lists.

## Economics and interpretation

Costs are captured-token valuations under the frozen historical experimental policy, expressed as API-equivalent dollars. They are not verified cash invoices, current public tariffs, or subscription-credit conversions. Inclusive input, cached input, cache-write input, and output are reported without double counting; reasoning is part of output. Physical attempts and native response/counter identities remain distinct.

Three cost scopes remain separate: development, recurring main-run execution including verification and finalization, and evaluation. Component/diagnostic execution belongs to development and its combined subcap. Unknown totals stay null, with any known subtotal shown separately. Failed attempts remain in the economics.

Three clocks are reported: active processing, observed waiting, and start-to-verified. The policy's waiting submeasures are nonadditive with active run wall time. Failed or unverified results have no verified endpoint; their start-to-stop time and missing-endpoint reason remain visible. Native elapsed time is not labeled pure inference.

After reveal, both repeats are reported individually. Main comparisons use the predeclared matching and also show all four control/candidate repeat combinations as a sensitivity view. Improvements and regressions stay separate. Local assertion IDs are compared across runs only after explicit root semantic identity alignment. Two repeats support descriptive results, not statistical equivalence or generalization.

The screening target is at least a twofold recurring-cost or verified-latency saving without loss of supported findings or new consequential unsupported assertions. Mechanical ratio eligibility requires coherent deliveries and completed verification on both sides; a ratio alone cannot establish a screen pass. A candidate merits expansion only when both cases and repeats support the target without material semantic regression. A localized defect or missing measurement needs a named repair. A measured target miss or repeated consequential error fails the screen. Missing the target does not trigger retries.

## Evaluation evidence

`evaluation-preparation.json` binds the evaluator's source-only checks, exact Jujutsu coverage inventory, and mechanical comparison-tool validation. These files are outside participant inputs. They contain no participant scores and make no claim about the current runs' quality.

# Pilot pricing basis — verified September 8, 2026

Astra standard API rates per million tokens are $10 input, $1 cached input, $12.50 cache writes and $50 output. Over 272K input tokens, the full request uses twice the input/cache rates and 1.5 times output; Fast doubles applicable rates. The reused collector checks native request sizes, cache categories and service configuration before producing an equivalent. [Official model pricing](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models/gpt-6-astra)

Astra standard Codex rates are 250 input, 25 cached-input and 1,250 output credits per million tokens. Credits and API dollars are separate measures. [Official Codex pricing](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/pricing)

The OpenCode Go DeepSeek V4 Flash tariff per million disjoint input/output/cache-read tokens is $0.22/$0.66/$0.007 off-peak and twice those amounts at peak. Peak is weekdays 01:00–04:00 and 06:00–10:00 UTC. These are allowance valuations, not evidence of cash debited. [OpenCode Go tariff](https://dev.opencode.ai/docs/go/)

Raw native usage and runtime catalog estimates are preserved. Search charges, failed-request charges not captured in usage, parent/controller work and actual account billing are not established by these figures. No all-in billed total or mixed-tariff cash-saving ratio is claimed. The historical collector is reused unchanged; its unrelated provider rate tables are not used in this pilot.

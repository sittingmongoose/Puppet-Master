# Low-frequency noise in a shared rehearsal building

Case ID: ER12-C-R1-01-FRESH
Slot ID: C-R1-01
Domain: building acoustics

## Project situation

We run a small rehearsal building beside a residential courtyard. The bass complaints are intermittent: some happen during drum sessions, but one tenant says the worst evenings include no drumming. Rooms were rearranged last winter; nobody kept a reliable before-and-after log. We can borrow a recorder and a basic sound meter for a later pilot, and we can temporarily move freestanding panels. The building manager wants a defensible investigation before spending money on more treatment. These are hypothetical project facts, not measurements.

## Request

Use the available 60-minute research window to prepare a research proposal that distinguishes plausible transmission paths and identifies a useful first pilot. Explain what existing research can and cannot tell us about this particular building, the mechanism behind each serious option, what implementation details or earlier installations would be worth investigating, and what would change the recommendation. A diagram or simple model is welcome if it clarifies the investigation; we need no renovation design.

## Requirements to preserve

- Keep courtyard disturbance and sound inside the rehearsal rooms as separate outcomes; a quieter recording indoors is not evidence of reduced disturbance outside.
- Compare at least one source or scheduling intervention with at least one path or room intervention, including a useful low-cost option if expensive treatment is unsupported.
- Explain how an observation plan could distinguish airborne transmission, structural coupling, and unrelated background events without assuming only one is present.
- Include the changed room arrangement and the history of a real comparable implementation as research opportunities, with the relevant differences stated.
- Specify a proposed pilot, its comparison condition, likely confounders, and a decision that could be made from it; label proposed checks separately from any checks actually executed during research.

## Boundaries

- Do not recommend structural alterations, landlord enforcement, permanent installations, or construction as part of this assignment.
- Do not invent complaint counts, acoustic measurements, building materials, or a verified cause from the anecdote.
- Do not treat a phone recording or a manufacturer absorption claim as a validated prediction of courtyard benefit.

## Still unresolved

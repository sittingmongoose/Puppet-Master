# Reviser source index — D-R1-01-treatment

This index is limited to sources in the assigned investigator and critic maps plus this stage’s bounded primary-source checks. The stable investigator IDs S01–S14 are preserved; reviewer checks R01–R10 add observations without rebinding them. Exact versions, locators, access operations, conditions, exceptions, applicability, and limitations are in [source-map.json](../source-map.json). The linked studies are comparators, not evidence that the hypothetical cooperative’s product was tested.

## Product moisture, texture, and handling

- [S01 — Arimi et al. 2010, Crackerbread water activity and crispiness](https://repository.must.ac.ke/handle/123456789/243); abstract accessed in reviser check R04, full PDF fetch timed out. Its critical interval is not transferable.
- [S02 — Hough et al. 2001, sensory texture of commercial biscuits](https://bibliotecadigital.exactas.uba.ar/collection/paper/document/paper_00224901_v32_n1_p57_Hough); four biscuit types and sensory cues, with no snack-specific threshold.
- [S03 — Esveld et al. 2012, sorption in cereal crackers](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0260877411004687); morphology-dependent comparator, not a transferable rate.
- [S07 — Le-Bail et al. 2019, BRICE checking and breakage report](https://www.cerealsgrains.org/publications/cfw/2019/November-December/Documents/CFW-64-6-0078.pdf); geometry, center/surface water, and early checking observations in studied biscuits.
- [S14 — Hagenloch 2016, modeling product cooling for snack crackers](https://scholarworks.wmich.edu/honors_theses/2700/); accessible abstract names temperature, depth, and line speed, without transferable settings.

## Comparable package implementations

- [S04 — Baele et al. 2021, cookie and milk-powder packaging](https://biblio.ugent.be/publication/8713140); reviser directly re-opened institutional abstract in R10. Cookie and powder results are kept separate.
- [S05 — Balestra et al. 2019, differently packed sunflower-oil biscuits](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6801301/); full article re-opened in R02, including batch split, constructions, 35 °C/50% RH, 105-day storage, and separate texture results.
- [S06 — Jo et al. 2022, filler-embedded LDPE zipper bags](https://www.mdpi.com/2304-8158/11/12/1704); publisher article text retrieved in R03, while direct open returned HTTP 429. Conditions and varied snack outcomes are bounded.
- [S12 — Vercelino Alves et al. 1999, biscuit packaging material and sealing](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/875608799901500107); publisher abstract rechecked in R01. Use “impaired-seal comparison,” not a claim of an in-use failed implementation.
- [S13 — Aucejo et al. 2000, humidity response of EVOH films](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/108201320000600211); conditional layer-specific caveat only.
- [S08 — Ilhan et al. 2021, heat-seal integrity review](https://repository.tudelft.nl/record/uuid:923a6fa2-112d-4088-be4a-8a68caa03c07); process/material/contaminant/later-process factors.

## Package measurement references

- [S09 — ASTM F1249-25](https://store.astm.org/f1249-25.html); active film/sheet WVTR method, reviser check R08. The method does not characterize finished package seals or shelf life.
- [S10 — ASTM F88/F88M-23](https://store.astm.org/f0088_f0088m-23.html); active seal-strength method, reviser check R06. Measures a sealed strip and failure mode.
- [S11 — ASTM F2096-11(2019)](https://store.astm.org/standards/f2096); critic check C12 and upstream source map. Gross-leak bubble test; destructive; method applicability to nonporous packaging is limited.
- [R05 — ASTM F2338-24, vacuum-decay leak detection](https://store.astm.org/f2338-24.html); official scope includes flexible nonporous packages. It is a candidate only after validation on the actual package.
- [R09 — ASTM F2095-07(2021), pressure-decay test for flexible packages](https://store.astm.org/f2095-07r21.html); destructive and conditional on actual package contents, volume, and method setup.

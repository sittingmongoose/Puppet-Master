## Product moisture, texture, and handling

- [S01 — Arimi et al. 2010, Crackerbread water activity and crispiness](https://repository.must.ac.ke/handle/123456789/243). One Crackerbread study; activity range, probe/acoustic method, and comparator critical interval. The publisher full text was not retrieved; the institutional bibliographic record and abstract were accessible.
- [S02 — Hough et al. 2001, commercial biscuit sensory texture](https://bibliotecadigital.exactas.uba.ar/collection/paper/document/paper_00224901_v32_n1_p57_Hough). Four commercial biscuits; trained and consumer panel; preference and crispness perception differed by product and measure.
- [S03 — Esveld et al. 2012, water sorption in cereal crackers](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0260877411004687). Primary cracker sorption study; morphology-dependent moisture dynamics. Publisher search result provided the abstract; direct full-text access is subscription-limited.
- [S14 — Hagenloch 2016, modeling cooling of baked snack crackers](https://scholarworks.wmich.edu/honors_theses/2700/). Accessible abstract identifies temperature, cracker depth, and line speed as model variables; the sponsor-proprietary thesis body is not available.
- [S07 — Le-Bail et al. 2019, BRICE checking and breakage project report](https://www.cerealsgrains.org/publications/cfw/2019/November-December/Documents/CFW-64-6-0078.pdf). *Cereal Foods World* proceedings, PDF page 110 (printed page A110): round/thick versus rectangular/thin pierced biscuit, 15-day monitoring, water mapping and timing of checking.

## Comparable package implementations

- [S13 — Aucejo et al. 2000, humidity response of EVOH film](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/108201320000600211). Four EVOH copolymers at 23 ± 2 °C; water permeability increased up to two orders above aw 0.75. Applies only if the actual film contains EVOH; full article is restricted.
- [S12 — Vercelino Alves et al. 1999, biscuit packaging material and sealing](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/875608799901500107). Retail biscuits from 6 manufacturers/3 machine types; rollpack and horizontal-flowpack seals contributed to package water-vapor transfer above the film-area calculation under 38 °C/90% RH. Full methods are restricted.
- [S04 — Baele et al. 2021, cookies and dry-food packaging trade-offs](https://biblio.ugent.be/publication/8713140). Institutional record/abstract; construction descriptions and cookie storage condition. The full text is listed as a download but was not used here.
- [S05 — Balestra et al. 2019, differently packed biscuits](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s13197-019-03918-z). Publisher version-of-record abstract; film variants and accelerated condition. Full article methods were not available from the publisher preview; the primary article’s indexed full-text result supplied the methods details, and the direct PMC page open was blocked by recaptcha.
- [S06 — Jo et al. 2022, filler-embedded LDPE zipper bag](https://doi.org/10.3390/foods11121704). Open article PDF accessed via [Semantic Scholar mirror](https://pdfs.semanticscholar.org/2017/8835b7ce4d6cda54ea236c1101aaf287563f.pdf); published MDPI article has full material and test-method detail.

## Package integrity and measurement methods

- [S08 — Ilhan et al. 2021, heat-seal integrity review](https://repository.tudelft.nl/record/uuid:923a6fa2-112d-4088-be4a-8a68caa03c07). Final published review; seal-leak factors grouped by process, material, contaminants and later processes.
- [S09 — ASTM F1249-25](https://store.astm.org/standards/f1249). Active water-vapor transmission method for plastic film/sheeting; use with specified sampling and test conditions.
- [S10 — ASTM F88/F88M-23](https://store.astm.org/f0088_f0088m-23.html). Active seal-strength method; force/failure mode and process consistency, not a standalone leak test.
- [S11 — ASTM F2096-11(2019)](https://store.astm.org/standards/f2096). Active internal-pressurization bubble method for gross package leaks; destructive; not a film barrier or food-safety test.

# Independent critique — D-R1-01-control

## Review basis and overall assessment

I reviewed the original brief, complete investigator discovery and draft, the carried source map/index and all 15 source notes, the revealed plan, and its reveal receipt. The SHA-256 values of the current discovery and revealed-plan files match the receipt exactly. The proposal is substantially faithful to the brief and plan: it keeps the case hypothetical, separates texture, package integrity and safety, compares practical film/seal and process alternatives, and proposes a traceable blocked pilot with a decision rule. It does not diagnose the returns or claim a safe life, compliance, readiness, or measured case outcome.

No material false correction or invented cooperative data was found. The draft is candid about the deadline and evidence limits. The findings below concern completeness and experimental interpretation, not a direction to reduce scope or to write a final.

## Findings

### F1 — Material incomplete: the required research window was not completed

**Classification:** material incomplete.  
**Draft locator:** “Clause-by-clause disposition — Request” and “Executed work and limits.”

The brief explicitly asks for the full 60-minute research window. The draft itself says only about 26 minutes were available before the investigator’s binding deadline. That is a direct, acknowledged shortfall against the original request. It does not make the work performed false, and the investigator correctly did not claim otherwise. It does mean the complete discovery and evidence-depth requirement cannot be treated as satisfied merely because the draft covers every brief heading. The deadline conflict is an upstream constraint; a critic cannot certify the missing time as research performed.

### F2 — Material incomplete: production and maintenance history remains a proposal for future inquiry

**Classification:** material incomplete, with external records still unresolved.  
**Plan locator:** “Implementation and history opportunity.”  
**Draft locator:** “Comparable implementation and production evidence,” the closing paragraph of that section, and the requested supplier/customer records and operating-burden items.

The draft gives useful comparable operating evidence: the commercial package survey (S004), package/seal transfer work (S003), product/package studies (S005, S006, S014), and seal-process studies (S012–S013). The checked sources disclose relevant constructions and test conditions, and the draft consistently limits transfer. However, a market survey and laboratory studies are not an authenticated production change history, corrective-action record, or maintenance-heavy implementation. The draft explicitly says it found no named supplier correction or production-line maintenance record and asks for those records. That is an honest gap, but the revealed plan specifically asks for unsuccessful or maintenance-heavy implementation reports as well as claimed improvements. Production history therefore remains incomplete pending named, comparable operating evidence or cooperative/supplier records.

### F3 — Material incomplete for causal attribution: candidate-film arm changes a package system

**Classification:** material incomplete / design interpretation needs clarification.  
**Draft locator:** “Second: a small, blocked pilot with distinct interventions,” arm D; “How findings would redirect the work.”

Arm D compares a candidate film with its construction-appropriate qualified seal against the incumbent configuration. If the candidate requires different seal settings or process changes, arm D estimates the effect of changing the package implementation as a whole, not the film barrier alone. That is a valid practical procurement comparison, and the draft does request recording the process changes. The interpretation should stay at that system level unless a matched seal condition or larger factorial design separates material from seal/process effects. Otherwise a result could be attributed too narrowly to “film.”

### F4 — Honestly unresolved external inputs: owner-defined endpoints and cooperative records

**Classification:** honestly unresolved external input; handled appropriately in the draft.  
**Draft locator:** “Owner decisions required,” “Decision rule and stop/expand conditions,” and “Unresolved.”

Ingredients, current and prior film construction, batch linkage, seal equipment, cooling and storage history, customer-valued texture, and laboratory access were not supplied. The draft correctly states what those gaps prevent, defers numerical conditions and thresholds, and identifies what a modest pilot could still learn. No correction is called for until those inputs exist.

## Independent source checks and evidence limits

The critic’s source map preserves all 15 IDs and exact source bindings. I independently checked primary publisher or official pages for S001, S003–S006, S009–S015, with the per-source locator and access limitation recorded there. Among the consequential checks:

- S001’s publisher abstract supports the stated Crackerbread test range and 0.51–0.59 result; the draft correctly says not to transfer it.
- S003’s university-affiliated record supports the roughly 25% seal contribution for its tested packages; exact test temperature/RH ranges remain unavailable and the draft says so.
- S004’s publisher full text confirms the 108-package survey, package measurements, room-temperature testing and T-peel method. Its mixed products and unknown histories do not diagnose this snack.
- S005 and S006 confirm the study-specific model/material outcomes. Neither supplies a cooperative shelf-life endpoint.
- S009 is an old FDA technical guide, and S010 is explicitly a non-binding January 2025 draft. S011 supports only general Salmonella persistence context. The draft does not turn these into a case safety conclusion.
- S012 supports the tested PE-film contamination thresholds and seal-bar effects; S013’s primary abstract supports its LLDPE-specific liquid-contamination comparison. Both are explicitly barred from becoming a cooperative seal setting.
- S014’s publisher abstract confirms the formulation-dependent simulation fit (R² 0.90 versus 0.51); S015’s publisher record supports product-specific sensory thresholds. The draft avoids transferring their values.

S002’s publisher fetch was blocked and the accessible PubMed page supplied no text in this review; S007 and S008 were checked through the carried primary-source notes rather than independently reopened. Their draft uses are bounded and explicitly described as limited or exploratory, so I found no unsupported cooperative conclusion there. S003’s exact environmental ranges and the unprovided cooperative’s actual package/process history remain unresolved, not silently filled in.

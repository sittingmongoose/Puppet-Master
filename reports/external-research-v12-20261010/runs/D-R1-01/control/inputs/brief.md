# Moisture changes in a cooperative dry-snack package

Case ID: ER12-D-R1-01-FRESH
Slot ID: D-R1-01
Domain: food packaging science

## Project situation

A small food cooperative wants to study why a dry baked snack sometimes loses its crisp texture in sealed sample bags. The cooperative changed both the bag supplier and the cooling routine in the same month. Some returned samples have poor texture even though the bag looks intact. A supplier has offered another film, but the cooperative would rather understand the evidence before ordering a pilot batch. The scenario is hypothetical, and no food or package tests have been supplied.

## Request

Use the full 60-minute research window to prepare a research proposal about moisture and texture changes across packaging and handling. Explain plausible mechanisms, compare genuinely useful alternatives, and investigate the history and operating details of comparable implementations. Give us a later controlled pilot design and a reasoned provisional path, with uncertainty visible. This is a research proposal, not a production packaging specification.

## Requirements to preserve

- Separate texture retention, package integrity, and food safety; an improvement in one does not establish the others.
- Compare a packaging-material or seal intervention with a cooling or storage-practice intervention and a useful option that does not require changing film.
- Consider initial product moisture, equilibration, ambient humidity, seal variation, and handling history rather than assuming nominal film barrier performance determines shelf behavior.
- Investigate implementation or production history, including what changed simultaneously and what comparable reports disclose about actual package construction and conditions.
- Propose a pilot comparison with meaningful observations, traceable batches, confounder control, and a decision rule; distinguish proposed testing from searches or calculations actually executed.

## Boundaries

- Do not declare a safe shelf life, food-safety compliance, or sale readiness from the supplied anecdote or a film data sheet.
- Do not perform food experiments, recommend ingredient additives, or prescribe a production seal setting in this assignment.
- Do not invent sensory results, moisture values, storage conditions, or a verified package failure.

## Still unresolved

We have not specified ingredients, bag construction, seal equipment, cooling duration, storage distribution, batch records, packaging dimensions, or the texture measure customers value. The cooperative has not committed to laboratory access. State what these gaps prevent and what a modest pilot could still learn.

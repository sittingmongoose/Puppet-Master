## S01 — historical ceramic-industry survey

**U.S. EPA, _Development Document for Effluent Limitations Guidelines and Standards of Performance: The Clay, Gypsum, Refractory and Ceramic Products Industries_, April 1975, Draft contractor report, Contract 68-01-2633.** [Primary report](https://nepis.epa.gov/Exe/ZyPURL.cgi?Dockey=9100XGMU.TXT) · Accessed `2026-10-10T05:11:46Z`.

- Cover pp. 1–2 says draft contractor report, for review/comment, not official EPA publication and not reviewed by EPA.
- Section V §3.7.2, printed p. V-64: variable pottery solids, clays/glaze solids and sometimes metal oxides/salts; intermittent, small, infrequently measured flows.
- Section V §§3.7.4–3.7.5, pp. V-67–68: treatment used by surveyed plants; one small art pottery recycled after settling; one more complex plant filtered sludge underflows, recycled filtrate, and landfilled filter cake; many records deficient in metals analysis.
- Section VII §§2.1, 1.0 and 1.5, pp. VII-2–4: fine clays may resist settling; glaze-containing process streams; segregation/control; intermittent flow; settling-tank performance varies by particles and configuration.
- Section VIII, pp. VIII-23–24: historical pottery treatment/implementation context.
- **Applicability:** historical industrial evidence only; not a current standard, not a local authorization, and not a direct small-shop prediction.

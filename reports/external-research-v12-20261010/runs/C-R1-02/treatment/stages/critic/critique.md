## Overall assessment

The investigator package is a strong, scope-faithful research proposal. It distinguishes suspended solids from dissolved constituents, rejects visual clarity as an endpoint, compares flow-managed settling, conditional clay-only filtration, source segregation and contained no-treatment handling, and carries operating limits, cleaning work and residual fate through both mechanisms and feasibility. It treats the extra-bucket episode as confounded by lower workload and proposes a workload-matched baseline rather than claiming causation.

The draft gives a usable staged discussion plan: resolve stream/authority questions, log representative baseline conditions, then consider a partner-approved comparison with paired sampling, operating logs and stop/escalation rules. Its proposed future work is clearly separated from desktop source review actually completed. It does not claim a sample, lab test, permit check, waste determination, discharge, installation, performance result or safety validation. No material wrong claim or material omission was found in the bounded review. The primary sources checked support the consequential historical, rule, method, manufacturer-use and operating-condition claims, with their limits acknowledged.

The only located defect is a minor source-ID policy text mismatch (C1). The lack of site-specific data and permissions is honestly unresolved external input required by the brief, not a defect to repair by invention. This critique is not authority to reduce scope or select an option.

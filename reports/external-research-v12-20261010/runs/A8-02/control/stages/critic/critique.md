## Review result

The investigator package preserves the assignment’s scope and constraints and corrects the released plan’s unsafe assumptions. I found no material factual error in the final draft’s consequential claims checked against primary evidence. The Beam issue/fix example is accurately bounded to its Spanner connector; the product documentation supports the TTS delivery/retention conditions, LoRaWAN frame-counter rules, PostgreSQL CSV behavior, and InfluxDB duplicate-write warning.

The final draft still needs one targeted completion: obligation 1’s route comparison is mostly implicit in two separate route descriptions. The discovery contains a fuller comparison, but the final draft should carry the relative low-power-radio and CSV tradeoffs forward so it stands as the complete proposal. One discovery sentence also treats device batching and power behavior too generally. The freshness distinction is sound, but the precise basis of the indicator should be stated. These findings do not justify narrowing the requested scope or excluding CSV.

## Released-plan corrections and preservation

The draft correctly rejects the revealed plan’s arrival-time-as-observation-time and late-record discard assumptions; rejects globally unique sequence counters across reboot without evidence; separates network deduplication from application replay; and rejects a live connected demo as necessary validation. It preserves the optional CSV path rather than converting authorization into a mandatory feature. Owner assignments and all five negative constraints remain intact. The useful TTS/MQTT/time-series leads remain investigation candidates rather than preselected winners.

The report is a critique, not authority to change scope. The localized incompleteness requests above should be adjudicated during revision without dropping the low-power or CSV route, the delayed-event analogy, or any original obligation.

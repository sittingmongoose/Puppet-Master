**Observed operation.** Reopened RFC Editor HTML and inspected the iTIP cancellation and recurrence-instance procedures.

**Governing condition/default/exception.** CANCEL is a scheduling message from an organizer to affected attendees; a single-instance operation identifies the component with UID and RECURRENCE-ID. It carries scheduling/attendee behavior absent from a local file snapshot edit.

**Applicability.** Supports the draft’s distinction between changing a local .ics export and notifying meeting attendees.

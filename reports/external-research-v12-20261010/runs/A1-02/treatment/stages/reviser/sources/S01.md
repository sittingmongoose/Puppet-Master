**Governing condition/default/exception.** UID is the persistent component identifier; RECURRENCE-ID is the original DTSTART slot and has the same value type and local-time form as master DTSTART. The recurrence set is DTSTART plus RRULE/RDATE minus EXDATE; exclusions win, duplicates collapse, and a DTSTART/RRULE mismatch is undefined. A TZID references a matching VTIMEZONE; recurring VTIMEZONE rules must cover every recurrence. With TZID DTSTART, UNTIL is UTC DATE-TIME; COUNT and UNTIL are mutually exclusive and COUNT includes DTSTART. DATE-TIME folds choose the first instant and explicit gaps use the pre-gap offset; Erratum 4271 changes generated-gap handling and is recorded under S12. §3.2.20 requires preservation of unknown value-type token data only; IANA/X-name properties may be ignored by generic applications.

**Applicability.** Primary file-format contract for the requested adapter; it does not select the product subset, duplicate policy, or guarantee cross-reader behavior.

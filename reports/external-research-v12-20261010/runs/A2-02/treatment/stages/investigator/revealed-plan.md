## Pilot intent

A university reading room wants researchers to annotate selected pages from digitized manuscripts supplied by several collections. A note may target a whole page or a rectangular region, include a transcription, and be exported for later reuse. Collections retain their image masters and their existing descriptive catalogues. The initial pilot covers 100 pages with different image dimensions.

The team has tentatively discussed Mirador or Universal Viewer. These are investigation leads, not selected winners.

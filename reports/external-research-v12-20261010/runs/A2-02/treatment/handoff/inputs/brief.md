# Portable reading-room annotation workspace

## Assignment

A university reading room wants researchers to annotate selected pages from digitized manuscripts supplied by several collections. A note may target a whole page or a rectangular region, include a transcription, and be exported for later reuse. Collections retain their image masters and their existing descriptive catalogues. The initial pilot covers 100 pages with different image dimensions.

You have a 60-minute whole-topic research envelope. Produce a planning proposal, not an implementation. Use public primary documentation, relevant public source code and released issue/fix history where they bear on the proposal. Discover realistic products, mechanisms and alternatives independently; no product, source or conclusion is preselected. A bounded historical example and useful unfamiliar opportunities matter more than an exhaustive catalogue. Clearly distinguish observed external behavior, your inference and a local product choice.

## Exact numbered obligations

1. Explain page identity, region coordinates and annotation portability across viewers or changed image sizes.
2. Compare two manuscript/image viewers and an analogous annotation mechanism; separate native capability from an integration proposal.
3. Describe manifest/version compatibility, external-image availability and export/import behavior without modifying source masters.
4. Investigate a relevant released compatibility change or issue/fix chain and explain which pilot operations it affects.
5. Preserve and investigate this supported optional scope: a downloadable transcription-only bundle for scholars without a compatible viewer. It is an authorized option, not a mandatory feature or an established technical capability; recommend conditions, retain it when supported, and explain any evidence-based exclusion.
6. Make owner decisions explicit: The collections curator owns stable page identifiers; the reading-room lead chooses whether annotations are private by default. Neither decision may be silently inferred from a viewer default.
7. Preserve negative constraints: Do not copy entire collections, alter collection masters, promise that all viewers share every annotation feature, or introduce mandatory public publication of scholars’ notes.
8. Deliver one coherent evidence-backed research proposal covering obligations 1–8, with recommendations, source/version applicability, useful discoveries, unresolved owner inputs, and a validation table separating checks actually executed from checks merely proposed. Do not report research sources or future tests as executed product validation.

## Research boundaries and deliverable

Keep the investigation at the pilot scale above. No installs, accounts, production access, purchases or live writes are needed. Read-only public retrieval is appropriate; any permitted local discriminating check must be disclosed with its actual inputs and result. A check you did not run remains proposed or NOT_RUN. Deliver a complete proposal with a compact comparison, evidence-linked recommendations and prioritized validation; there is no requirement to build a prototype. Owner inputs may remain explicit decisions, but they do not excuse investigating publicly answerable questions. This brief is the complete discovery input.

## S4 — 2022 short-route field study

## S5 — 2023 PCM packaging field study

[Publisher DOI](https://doi.org/10.3390/en16135191) · [Official publisher PDF](https://mdpi-res.com/d_attachment/energies/energies-16-05191/article_deploy/energies-16-05191.pdf?version=1688571133). One Portugal orange-crate route; SHT30 modules in ventilated housings measured air among produce, with a PCM/empty-alveoli comparison. No core-temperature or delivered-quality endpoint.

# S01 — Library of Congress Preservation Supply Specifications index

- URL: https://www.loc.gov/preservation/resources/specifications/index.html
- Version: dynamic web index; accessed 2026-10-10T05:56:16Z. It states that the linked PDFs are the most recent specifications.
- Locator: “Label Stock” listing, entries 700-701 through 700-714.
- Observed operation: opened the official index and reviewed the listed titles/dates.
- Findings: the index distinguishes labels for paper/box-board from labels for plastic/metal, and distinguishes laser from thermal-transfer printer use. The plastic/metal choices are dated 2016-09-30.
- Governing condition / exception: choose a specification by both application substrate and printer type; an index entry is not product certification for this store.
- Applicability: direct evidence that substrate and process are separate selection dimensions. The case’s sleeve resin and printer technology are unknown, so it cannot select an applicable spec yet.

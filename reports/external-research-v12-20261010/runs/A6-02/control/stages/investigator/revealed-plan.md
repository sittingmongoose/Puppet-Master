## Pilot intent

A regional museum network wants a search catalogue linking to digitized objects from five collections. Each collection supplies descriptive records and some statement about permitted reuse. Visitors need to see the supplied statement and its source, even when a record is incomplete or changes later. The network does not own all underlying objects or digital reproductions. This is metadata/workflow research, not a legal determination of rights.

The team has tentatively discussed Europeana-oriented aggregation or Omeka S collection publishing. These are investigation leads, not selected winners. The mechanism needing investigation is controlled statement identifiers, linked records and source provenance; a useful historical inquiry concerns metadata-mapping or rights-statement vocabulary migration history. Do not let these leads prevent discovery of a better fit or an analogous mechanism.

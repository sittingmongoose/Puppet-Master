# Independent critique — volunteer oral-history deposit proposal

**Run/stage:** A5-01-treatment / critic  
**Assessment basis:** The complete original brief, investigator discovery, draft proposal, source map and index, released plan and plan-reveal record were reviewed. I compared each numbered obligation and the draft's explicit plan dispositions, then independently reopened the cited primary documentation listed in the critic source map. No audio, application, product workflow or accessibility conformance test was run. This is a review, not a revised proposal or an authority to narrow scope.

## Overall assessment

The investigator substantially corrects the revealed plan's unsupported one-field, filename-linking and universal-survival assumptions. The draft gives a coherent sidecar-first recommendation, a genuinely different optional BWF-on-copy route, and BagIt as a portable fixity/transfer mechanism. It handles identity, descriptive authority, transcript versioning, original/derivative lineage, the optional transcript/access-copy path, owner decisions and negative constraints with useful specificity. Its versioned BWF MetaEdit history is relevant and appropriately described as mutable release-history evidence rather than observed present-day behavior. Product validation remains clearly distinct from research and proposed checks.

Two material gaps remain before this can be considered fully faithful to the whole input: interviewer-provided notes have no explicit disposition in the proposed deposit model, and the EBU paraphrase blurs semantic non-support of unknown chunks with pass-through behavior by conforming BWF applications. One product-interface assertion is unsupported by the exact source cited. Two narrower wording/count issues do not change the substance of the recommendation.

## Findings

| ID | Classification | Draft locator | Independent evidence and assessment |
|---|---|---|---|
| CR-1 | **Material incomplete** | Recommendation, line 9; Workflow A and BagIt rows, lines 19–21; identity paragraph, lines 39–45 | The brief says volunteers supply interviewer notes as well as WAV recordings and photographs. The package recommendation names the original, sidecars, photographs and derivatives, and assigns IDs to recordings and photos, but does not state whether or how the received interviewer notes are retained, identified, linked to the interview/recordings, or covered by consent/access review. This leaves one supplied input outside the described deposit model. The critique does not decide whether every note must be preserved or published; the proposal needs an explicit disposition and owner/policy boundary for them. |
| CR-2 | **Material incomplete** | Workflow B limitations, line 20; obligation 1 disposition, line 29; embedded-metadata conditions, line 49 | The draft says EBU indicates unknown chunks “are ignored.” EBU Tech 3285 v2, section 2.1, p. 8, distinguishes interpretation from preservation: applications are not required to interpret or use unspecified private chunks, and their data integrity is not guaranteed; however, BWF-compliant applications “shall pass these chunks.” Thus a reader may ignore their meaning, while pass-through is a conformance expectation, not a guarantee for arbitrary WAV editors or chunk integrity. The draft elsewhere correctly calls for selected-tool/version rewrite testing, but the broad paraphrase can be read as saying unknown chunks are simply dropped. Clarify the distinction because the brief specifically asks about optional fields and rewrite behavior. |
| CR-3 | **Unsupported** | Workflow B portability cell, line 20 | The draft says BWF MetaEdit offers “GUI/CLI” across platforms and cites METAEDIT. The directly checked project page establishes BWF support, listed platform binaries, version 26.08.1, import/edit/embed/export functions and the audio-data-only MD5; that page does not establish the GUI/CLI claim. This is unsupported by the cited locator, not proof that no official CLI exists. Cite a released official interface source or qualify the statement. |
| CR-4 | **Minor locator/wording** | Recommendation, line 9 | “A payload manifest then checks every packaged file's bytes” is broader than RFC 8493 section 2.1.3: the required payload manifest covers payload files; a tag manifest for tag files is optional. The later BagIt row, line 21, partly supplies this distinction. Limit the sentence to payload files or separately state whether tag files are covered. |
| CR-5 | **Minor locator/wording** | Validation record, line 102 | The sentence refers to “the last five rows” as the product/workflow checks; the table has six post-research rows (inspect WAV, BagIt, BWF round-trip, transcript workflow, accessibility/consent, end-to-end). The inspect-WAV row is already explicitly NOT_RUN, so this is a count/locator ambiguity, not evidence that a check was reported as run. |

The draft labels them as decisions and does not claim to have obtained them. These are valid unresolved inputs; research cannot settle them. |

No other material wrong or unsupported correction was identified in the reviewed package. In particular, rejecting the plan's claim that one comment field can hold all metadata, that filenames make durable photo links, and that embedded metadata survives every editor is consistent with the format evidence. The draft also preserves the plan's explicitly authorized optional transcript/listening-copy path instead of treating it as mandatory or excluding it for lack of a tested implementation.

## Disposition against the exact numbered obligations

1. **Workflows and analogous preservation mechanism — substantially met.** Sidecar-first and BWF-on-a-working-copy have clear portability, recorder, field, encoding and maintenance tradeoffs; BagIt is correctly framed as opaque-payload packaging/fixity rather than descriptive authority. Apply CR-1 and CR-4.
2. **Identity, authority, revisions and relationships — met for audio, transcript and photo records.** Session/file IDs, received names, hashes, sidecar authority as a local choice, transcript revisions, interviewer approval and sibling derivative links are concrete. The missing interviewer-notes disposition is CR-1.
3. **Optional embedded metadata conditions — substantially met.** The draft separates generic WAV from BWF, discusses ASCII and field limits, optional chunks, version compatibility, XML-editor caveats and duplicate-only testing. Apply CR-2; no broad compatibility or losslessness claim is otherwise made.
4. **Round-trip/encoding history — met.** The named 0.2.2, 21.07, 23.04 and 25.04 changes explain a relevant failure/change path. The mutable master/no-fixed-commit caveat and proposed representative-value test are explicit. No local result is claimed.
5. **Optional transcript and accessible listening copy — met with conditions.** It remains optional, human-reviewed, linked to the original and subject to consent/access, staffing and target testing. W3C guidance is treated as guidance, not a local conformance result. The proposed playback and transcript checks remain proposed.
6. **Owner decisions — met as unresolved decisions.** Archivist, interviewer and consent/restriction reviewer roles are explicitly separated; no software default or possession-as-consent inference is used.
7. **Negative constraints — met.** The investigator reports no alteration, install, code execution, ASR requirement, losslessness promise or consent inference. No contradiction appears in the recommendation.
8. **Coherent proposal and validation separation — substantially met.** Recommendations, evidence/applicability, useful discoveries, open owners and a status table are present. The table does not promote public-source review or future testing into executed product validation. Apply CR-1 and the minor CR-5 wording clarification.

## Overall assessment

The investigator produced a coherent, evidence-backed pilot proposal that addresses all eight brief obligations and the released plan's eight clauses. It corrects the plan's reliance on STL, retains the authorized preview-and-note option conditionally, separates design intent from machine settings, assigns the named owner decisions, and does not claim universal compatibility. The issue-history example is bounded to the old builds and is not presented as a current defect. No product validation is represented as executed.

I found no material false correction and no scope decision that should be reversed. One material incompleteness remains around the optional 3MF Materials extension's version pairing: two current Consortium sources appear to conflict, and the draft does not call out that conflict. This affects the optional extension path, not the proposed Core-only 3MF default. The open owner inputs (actual CAD tools, project examples, receiving matrix, and owner-provided intent) are appropriate pilot decisions, not excuses to skip public research.

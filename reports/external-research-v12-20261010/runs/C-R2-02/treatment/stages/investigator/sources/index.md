# Source index — investigator stage

This is the bounded, navigable source list for discovery.md and source-map.json. Source IDs are stable across both files. Access timestamps are exact response-observation times recorded in source-map.json. No source PDFs or full text were copied into this folder.

## Reporting and method
- [S001 — PRISMA-S (2021)](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13643-020-01542-z): items for other methods, exact per-source strategies, citation searching, prior search reuse, and deduplication.
- [S002 — Cochrane Handbook Chapter 4, v6.5.1](https://www.cochrane.org/authors/handbooks-and-manuals/handbook/current/chapter-04): source discovery and cautious stopping/performance checks.
- [S003 — Cochrane Chapter 4 Technical Supplement, v6.5](https://training.cochrane.org/chapter04-tech-supplonlinepdfv65270924): source classes, citation checking, grey literature and general web search.

## Directly related prior implementation
- [S004 — INSPQ, Urban Heat Island Mitigation Strategies: 2021 Update](https://www.inspq.qc.ca/sites/default/files/publications/3327-urban-heat-island-mitigation.pdf): Appendix 1 gives its Québec-focused six-database and bilingual grey-literature methods.

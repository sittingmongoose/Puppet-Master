# Critic source index

This index navigates the independent primary-source checks. Stable IDs are preserved exactly as in the investigator map; each ID still resolves to its original exact URL. Review locators, versions, access details, conditions, and applicability are in [source-map.json](../source-map.json). No source page or paper was copied into this directory.

## Standards and measurement method

- [STD-1996-1](https://www.iso.org/standard/59765.html) — ISO 1996-1:2016, community-noise quantities/assessment and no universal limit; official abstract/status.
- [STD-1996-2](https://www.iso.org/standard/59766.html) — ISO 1996-2:2017, outdoor sound-level measurement and effort/uncertainty; official abstract/status.
- [STD-16283-1](https://www.iso.org/standard/55997.html?browse=ics) — ISO 16283-1:2014, airborne room-to-room insulation; official scope, status, and amendment listing.
- [STD-16283-3](https://www.iso.org/standard/59748.html) — ISO 16283-3:2016, airborne façade insulation; official scope/lifecycle.
- [STD-12354](https://www.iso.org/standard/70242.html) — ISO 12354-1:2017, airborne room-to-room calculation from element/junction data; official model limits/lifecycle.
- [STUDY-HOPKINS](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0003682X0500068X) — Hopkins & Turner, publisher abstract for low-frequency room-to-room protocol.
- [GUIDE-FHWA](https://www.fhwa.dot.gov/Environment/noise/measurement/handbook.cfm) — FHWA-HEP-18-065, government measurement guidance used for method principles, not local limits.

## Implementations, room treatment, and product example

- [CASE-EMSLIE](https://www.acoustics.asn.au/conference_proceedings/AAS2008/papers/p64.pdf) — 2008 AAS proceedings paper; private drum studio, marked not peer reviewed.
- [CASE-GJERS](https://www.acoustics.asn.au/conference_proceedings/AAS2024/papers/p36.pdf) — 2024 AAS proceedings paper; drum/classroom sound and vibration measurements.
- [CASE-AIRBED](https://researchportal.lsbu.ac.uk/en/publications/flexible-control-of-acoustic-conditions-in-a-music-rehearsal-spac-3/) — LSBU institutional record for Henry Wood Hall internal reverberation treatment.
- [STUDY-SONG](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10138595/) — UCL institutional record/abstract for absorber location in coupled dwelling layouts.
- [PROD-SYLOMER](https://www.getzner.com/media/914/download/Data%20Sheet%20Sylomer%20SR%2011%20EN.pdf?v=6) — official SR 11 data sheet; load/frequency conditions and disclaimer.

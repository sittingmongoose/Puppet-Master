# Investigator source index

This directory is the navigable evidence map for the pre-plan discovery. Source IDs are stable and resolve only to the exact URLs below; do not silently rebind them. Full identity, version, UTC access, locator, observed operation, conditions/defaults/exceptions, and applicability are in [source-map.json](../source-map.json). Findings are paraphrased with locators; no source pages or copyrighted PDFs are copied here.

## Standards and measurement method

- [STD-1996-1](https://www.iso.org/standard/59765.html) — community-noise quantities, source characteristics, no limit.
- [STD-1996-2](https://www.iso.org/standard/59766.html) — environmental level determination; measurement effort and uncertainty.
- [STD-16283-1](https://www.iso.org/standard/55997.html?browse=ics) — airborne room-to-room field insulation.
- [STD-16283-3](https://www.iso.org/standard/59748.html) — airborne facade field insulation; lifecycle says “to be revised.”
- [STD-12354](https://www.iso.org/standard/70242.html) — airborne flanking model with element/junction data; lifecycle says “to be revised.”
- [STUDY-HOPKINS](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0003682X0500068X) — low-frequency microphone-position protocol; publisher abstract only.
- [GUIDE-FHWA](https://www.fhwa.dot.gov/Environment/noise/measurement/handbook.cfm) — highway-noise handbook used only for background/path/measuring principles, not local limits.

## Real implementation and reversible approaches

- [CASE-EMSLIE](https://www.acoustics.asn.au/conference_proceedings/AAS2008/papers/p64.pdf) — 2008 AAS proceedings, non-peer-reviewed drum studio case with separate indoor and boundary observations.
- [CASE-GJERS](https://www.acoustics.asn.au/conference_proceedings/AAS2024/papers/p36.pdf) — drum/classroom case with slab, resilient platform, accelerometer and meter comparisons.
- [CASE-AIRBED](https://researchportal.lsbu.ac.uk/en/publications/flexible-control-of-acoustic-conditions-in-a-music-rehearsal-spac-3/) — flexible airbed treatment for Henry Wood Hall internal reverberation.
- [STUDY-SONG](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10138595/) — absorber location in coupled dwelling layouts; abstract used, PDF retry timed out.
- [PROD-SYLOMER](https://www.getzner.com/media/914/download/Data%20Sheet%20Sylomer%20SR%2011%20EN.pdf?v=6) — manufacturer SR 11 load/frequency-dependent data sheet, revision 04-2025.

## Evidence boundaries

The case studies and standards identify methods and conditions, not the rehearsal building’s cause or likely effect size. They do not support a courtyard prediction from indoor absorption claims. No local rule or particular sound meter has been identified.

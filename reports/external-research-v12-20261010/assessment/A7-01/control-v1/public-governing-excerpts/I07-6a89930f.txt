227: 3MF
228: The preferred file format also used to save Project files.
229: STL
230: Both binary an ASCII format is supported.
231: STEP
232: The STEP file is triangulated during import.
233: OBJ
234: Information such as material and texture is ignored during import.
235: AMF
236: Even though AMF is supported, we suggest using 3MF instead. We described the reasons why we prefer 3MF in this article.

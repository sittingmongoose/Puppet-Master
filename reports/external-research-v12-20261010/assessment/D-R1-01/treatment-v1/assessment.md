[I04](evidence/index.md#i04), [I15](evidence/index.md#i15); [final.md:31](ER12_RUNTIME/runs/D-R1-01/treatment/stages/reviser/final.md:31).

[I16](evidence/index.md#i16); [final.md:30](ER12_RUNTIME/runs/D-R1-01/treatment/stages/reviser/final.md:30).
- **Leak-test scopes and controls are different from film transmission or strip strength.** Provides a conditional integrity-method menu and a qualified oracle instead of an assumed universal screen. [I09](evidence/index.md#i09), [I10](evidence/index.md#i10), [I11](evidence/index.md#i11), [I12](evidence/index.md#i12), [I13](evidence/index.md#i13); [final.md:96](ER12_RUNTIME/runs/D-R1-01/treatment/stages/reviser/final.md:96).

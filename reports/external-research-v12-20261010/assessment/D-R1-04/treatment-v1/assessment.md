## Useful discoveries and meaningful alternatives
- **D1:** Capacity-conditioned offline bypass can be intentional; pass-by alone is not capture failure. Practical consequence: Recover intended ponding/bypass geometry and observe prior fullness before choosing routing repair. Evidence E02, E03; [final L19](ER12_RUNTIME/runs/D-R1-04/treatment/stages/reviser/final.md:19), [final L70](ER12_RUNTIME/runs/D-R1-04/treatment/stages/reviser/final.md:70), [final L109](ER12_RUNTIME/runs/D-R1-04/treatment/stages/reviser/final.md:109), [final L110](ER12_RUNTIME/runs/D-R1-04/treatment/stages/reviser/final.md:110).
- **D2:** Recession can include storage, underdrain and overflow pathways; visible decline does not isolate native-soil infiltration. Practical consequence: Inspect layer/drain records and keep outcomes/measurement terms separate. Evidence E01, E09, E11; [final L25](ER12_RUNTIME/runs/D-R1-04/treatment/stages/reviser/final.md:25), [final L29](ER12_RUNTIME/runs/D-R1-04/treatment/stages/reviser/final.md:29), [final L120](ER12_RUNTIME/runs/D-R1-04/treatment/stages/reviser/final.md:120), [final L134](ER12_RUNTIME/runs/D-R1-04/treatment/stages/reviser/final.md:134), [final L136](ER12_RUNTIME/runs/D-R1-04/treatment/stages/reviser/final.md:136).
- **D3:** Groundwater interacting with media can reduce available capture void space; national guidance does not diagnose this site. Practical consequence: Retain groundwater as a conditional boundary for qualified records review, not an asserted explanation. Evidence E09; [final L31](ER12_RUNTIME/runs/D-R1-04/treatment/stages/reviser/final.md:31), [final L112](ER12_RUNTIME/runs/D-R1-04/treatment/stages/reviser/final.md:112).
- **D4:** Pretreatment depends on sheet versus concentrated flow, debris and maintenance; tested sump gross-solid retention can decline under overflow.

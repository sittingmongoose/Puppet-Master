## Independent bounded observation

Offset recovery, stale-offset rejection, optional extensions, and per-PATCH checks are supported. Protocol acknowledgements do not establish gateway crash durability or an assembled object's full-file hash. Authentication, URL structure, and the added idempotent session/status contract are implementation choices.

## Governing scope and conditions

Core required; creation/expiration/checksum/termination/concatenation optional and advertised. Expiry is known at creation for this chosen fixed policy. Termination resource cleanup is SHOULD; the stronger cleanup queue is an original design choice.

Domain: Tus client/server; byte offsets and upload lengths are nonnegative integer bytes.

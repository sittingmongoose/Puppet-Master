## Independent bounded observation

AWS lists 48.8 TiB object maximum, 10,000 parts, 5 MiB–5 GiB parts, and at most 1,000 listed parts per response. The last part has no minimum. Independently computed: 6 GiB / 16 MiB = 384; 20 MiB = 16 MiB + 4 MiB.

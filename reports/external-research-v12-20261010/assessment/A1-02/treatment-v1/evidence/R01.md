UID is TEXT and identifies the component; RECURRENCE-ID defaults to DATE-TIME, matches master value type/local form, and identifies the original slot. EXDATE wins over RRULE/RDATE inclusions; duplicates collapse; an unsynchronized DTSTART/rule is undefined. INTERVAL is positive and defaults to 1. COUNT includes DTSTART; COUNT and UNTIL are mutually exclusive; UNTIL for TZID DATE-TIME DTSTART is UTC DATE-TIME and is inclusive. Missing recurrence fields derive from DTSTART. A matching VTIMEZONE must cover recurrence instances. Folds use the first instant; gaps use the preceding offset. DTEND gives exact recurrence duration; DURATION gives nominal duration, with day/week components differing across transitions. §3.2.20 preserves unknown VALUE-type data; generic readers may ignore extension properties. New VEVENTs need UID/DTSTAMP and, without METHOD, DTSTART.

Governs the offline interchange design. Product subset, raw-byte preservation, upsert keys, tombstones and UI input rejection are product decisions. The earlier skip-gap text must be read with R03 and the later computed-time paragraph.

VEVENT CANCEL is an organizer scheduling notice to affected attendees. UID identifies a series; cancelling one instance requires RECURRENCE-ID, while whole-series cancellation omits it. The cancellation sequence increases; message/component and attendee constraints govern the transaction.

A useful analogy for original-slot addressing; its notification and organizer obligations do not turn offline snapshot editing into an invitation cancellation.

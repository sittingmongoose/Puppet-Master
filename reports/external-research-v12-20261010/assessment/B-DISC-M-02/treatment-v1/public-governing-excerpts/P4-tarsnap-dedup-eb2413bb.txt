218: 0218: How can we handle binary data?
219: 0219: First, let's remind ourselves of two benefits of "wordification":
220: 0220: Variable-sized chunks:
221: 0221: Words have different numbers of characters, so splitting data
222: 0222: into fixed-size chunks (say, every 4 characters) would not be
223: 0223: ideal.
224: 0224: Context-dependent splitting:
225: 0225: We don't try to guess where we should split our data before
226: 0226: we've looked at the data.  Words are split in spaces —
227: 0227: in other words, the location of each split depends on the context
228: 0228: (data).
229: 0229: Retaining those benefits when dealing with binary data requires some
230: 0230: complicated processing.  Tarsnap's method of doing this is outlined
231: 0231: in section 2.1 of the presentation

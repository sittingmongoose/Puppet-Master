## Material remaining finding M1

R1 item 2 says: “A constructor or synchronous `start()` exception releases the stream and reports `error`.” R2 says: “If failure occurs before any nonempty chunk and the track is still live, finalize that recorder and try the next candidate.” The first instruction is unconditional; R1 cleanup stops acquired tracks. The final supplies no reacquisition step for the retry. [R1](ER12_RUNTIME/runs/A3-02/control/stages/reviser/final.md:39), [cleanup](ER12_RUNTIME/runs/A3-02/control/stages/reviser/final.md:44), [retry](ER12_RUNTIME/runs/A3-02/control/stages/reviser/final.md:54), [default fallback](ER12_RUNTIME/runs/A3-02/control/stages/reviser/final.md:55).

The governing capture standard makes an app-stopped track ended and an all-ended stream inactive; the recording standard rejects starting on that inactive stream. Thus a new recorder cannot restore this stopped microphone track. This is a source-backed inference about incompatible plan instructions, not an invented observation that a browser or implementation failed. [Media Capture and Streams](https://www.w3.org/TR/2025/CRD-mediacapture-streams-20251009/#dom-mediastreamtrack-stop) (§4.2, §4.3.1; local [E02](evidence/E02-text.txt) lines 391–394 and 913–927), [MediaStream Recording](https://www.w3.org/TR/2026/WD-mediastream-recording-20260316/#dom-mediarecorder-start) (§2.3; local [E01](evidence/E01-text.txt) lines 225–240).

This matters to R2 and original P11’s capture-preservation clause. A named candidate’s constructor/start exception before data is an expressly anticipated case. The plan aborts microphone availability before its remaining-candidate/default strategy can run, even for a permitted situation where another native candidate would work on the original stream. Treating R2 as an unstated exception to R1, or inventing reacquisition, would repair the text. The conflict was already present in [draft R1](ER12_RUNTIME/runs/A3-02/control/stages/investigator/draft.md:29) and [draft R2](ER12_RUNTIME/runs/A3-02/control/stages/investigator/draft.md:44), was missed by the critic, and remains unchanged in final.

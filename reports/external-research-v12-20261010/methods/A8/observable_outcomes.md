# Observable outcomes and root-owned test definition

## Prospective comparison

Root alone selects and dispatches two fresh cases with R0 and A8, using the same full brief, exact plan, initial source conditions, model/provider/effort/tier, stage topology, deadlines and independent assessment scope. No case selection, answers, assessment or scientific dispatch occurred in this setup. This document defines observations; it does not replace campaign qualification/promotion rules or invent a significance claim from two pairs.

A8 investigator is unchanged. Critic/reviser receive only the frozen additive blocks. Record final prompt hashes and effective settings before interpreting the contrast. Keep the complete drafts, critiques, finals and source maps for independent full-source assessment.

## Mechanism observations

For every selected record, an assessor can inspect: selection order and draft locator; exact C and C*; whether only one scope selector changed; authentic source applicability and its scope-bearing witness; correctness of each relation; critic proposed disposition; final disposition and governing evidence. Count selected, completed-valid, inapplicable and deadline-unresolved records separately. Contrasts with no discriminating evidence are not successful error detections. Report material errors outside the selected six as well.

Primary semantic endpoint: independently assessed material source-semantic errors remaining in each complete final, with particular attention to unsupported false corrections, wrong component/version/unit/default claims and supported alternatives wrongly rejected. Also count correct material draft claims made materially wrong in final, incorrect draft claims correctly repaired, and new omissions in the final. Compare the entire topic scope, not only the selected subset. A semantic error classification requires primary-source adjudication, not the candidate's self-label. Six well-formed records are not a source pass.

Breadth endpoint: independent assessment of every original brief obligation, exact-plan clause disposition, useful discovery and alternatives, applicable conditions/defaults, decisions and proposed-versus-executed validation, consistent with the pinned reference's complete assessment. A8 cannot count as a quality-comparable improvement if its final loses material scope, gains an unsupported consequential decision, or fails source review.

Secondary efficiency endpoint: candidate delivery time, additive occupied agent time and independently assessed-result time separately. Count source operations in critic/reviser, including repeated navigation to the same governed passage and checks that add no new fact/version/applicability distinction. Same-URL reopening alone is not wasted work: independence and changed context can justify it. Report raw counts and reasons rather than claiming a saved-query score proves efficiency. Include setup/method preparation in campaign cost; no hidden free preparation. Record unavailable billing as UNKNOWN and do not sum ambiguous overlapping token counters.

## Interpretation and disconfirmation

- Evidence consistent with the mechanism: valid scope pairs expose actual unsupported scope transfers, the reviser applies correct supported repairs without deleting supported options, and independently assessed complete-final semantic failures decrease against paired R0 without breadth loss. This remains a small pilot observation.
- No demonstrated semantic benefit: both arms have zero material semantic errors, treatment has no valid opportunities, or selected contrasts add paperwork without changing independently judged outcomes. Zero errors on two cases cannot establish reliable improvement.
- Evidence against the proposal: A8 introduces a false correction, loses material scope, misses accessible governing context because of witness overhead, or overruns existing ceilings. A source pass with a deadline overrun remains time-ineligible.
- No efficiency claim unless source-quality comparison qualifies both arms and measured delivery/occupied work support it. Lower occupied work with a quality failure does not win. Faster delivery with more occupied work is reported as that tradeoff.

No pass/fail, result number, price estimate, throughput guarantee or winning designation is prefilled. Operational native Goal provenance, requested/effective settings, frozen handoffs and source quality remain separate qualifications.

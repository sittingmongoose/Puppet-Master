# Mechanism comparison with R0

| Dimension | Pinned R0 | A8 SCW-6 |
|---|---|---|
| Topology | Blind investigator, independent critic, fresh complete reviser | Identical three contexts and sequence |
| Discovery and reveal | Full brief; freeze discovery before exact-plan reveal | Investigator unchanged; no plan leakage |
| Critic's factual check | Independently check consequential claims against primary evidence | Same complete check, with a locked set of up to six one-selector claim contrasts |
| Evidence judgment | General consequential factual/condition check | Same authentic evidence must receive separate judgments for C and C* with a scope-bearing witness |
| Propagation of correction | Reviser explicitly adjudicates each criticism and rechecks disputes | For selected records, support for adjacent C* cannot establish C is false; replacement requires its own support |
| Coverage | Preserve the full scope, evidence, alternatives and plan comparison | Same full scope; contrast table is auxiliary, with explicit zero/inapplicable/deadline outcomes |
| Bounds | 30/12/18 stages inside absolute 60 minutes | Same budgets/settings; six contrast records maximum, no new stage |
| Expected effect | General independent review | More detection of scope substitution; fewer false negative capability claims and false corrections |

The causal treatment is the paired evidence decision rule and its use in correction adjudication. A tuple/table alone is bookkeeping; a protocol that merely records citations without separate scope entailment is not SCW. Generic additional skepticism, more browsing, a new model, a retained author, or shorter prose is not the intervention.

This differs from STORM-style perspective exploration, PaperQA2-style retrieval/ranking and evidence synthesis, GraphRAG-style entity/community indexing, and IGRPO-style trained iterative policy refinement: it changes neither discovery diversity, retrieval architecture nor training, and builds no graph. It also keeps the original three stages rather than adding ER11's separate factual verifier. These are mechanism-level distinctions, not an exhaustive audit of those implementations or unseen ER11 variants. Claimify motivates context preservation; A8 does not reproduce its multi-stage extraction pipeline. The proposed fixed-evidence pair, bounded pre-verdict selection and correction permission rule constitute the original operational proposal.

The method directly targets source semantics. Reduced duplicate clarification browsing is plausible if a witness carries the necessary scope into revision, but independent reopening still occurs. It may increase work. Only measured delivery/occupied time and independent source quality can support an optimization claim.

# Predicted failure modes and safeguards

| Failure mode | Observable signature | Required response |
|---|---|---|
| Extraction changes C before checking it | Intended C omits a clause, negation, attribution or condition from the quoted draft | Mark extraction defective; reviser restores context and ordinary review |
| C* differs in more than one selector | Subject and modality, or version and denominator, change together | INAPPLICABLE; do not retrospectively choose an easier claim |
| Easy or artificial contrasts | Diagnostic differs in an obviously irrelevant subject rather than a plausible scope confusion | Independent assessor flags no meaningful mechanism exposure |
| Confirmation on lexical overlap | Source shares terms but governs a different component/version | Require referent/condition witness; retain uncertainty if unresolved |
| Forced opposite labels | Both readings fit E but critic marks one false because it is the alternative | Record both independently; no forced exclusivity |
| Absence treated as contradiction | Unsuccessful search or missing mention becomes a capability rejection | UNDETERMINED; no false absence claim or supported-option removal |
| Witness truncation | A table heading, exception or necessary cross-reference is missing | Reopen governing context; inspect joins; no quote-length shortcut |
| Correct label, wrong replacement | C is refuted but the new claim has no applicable support | Do not adopt replacement; distinguish error from established remedy |
| Overhead crowding out breadth | Six records consume critic budget while another brief/plan obligation is omitted | Independent full-scope failure; retain original deadline and scope |
| Selection misses real risk | Material error occurs outside the six, or zero records are produced | Report noncoverage; normal R0 review remains required |
| Reviser follows confident critic | A wrong SCW finding reaches final despite original evidence | Independent final and false-correction assessment; no automatic label trust |
| Honest uncertainty used to evade research | UNDETERMINED appears despite accessible governing evidence and adequate time | Assess evidence exhaustion against original R0 scope; uncertainty label is not a pass |
| Paired tests confounded | Settings, full briefs, reveal boundary or budgets differ between arms | Do not attribute outcomes to SCW; root records operational qualification separately |

The six-record and word bounds may be too expensive for the existing twelve-minute critic. This stage has not tested feasibility. There is no restart, extra worker or budget increase in the frozen method. The protocol can be ineffective even when every format field is present; hashes and relation labels do not certify source correctness.

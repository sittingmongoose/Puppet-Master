# A8: Scope-Contrast Witness, version 1 (SCW-6)

Status: frozen transferable original proposal, ready for root-dispatched testing; effectiveness untested. Setup has a bounded-read deviation documented below. Target: source-semantic errors, particularly false corrections and scope substitutions. Redundant-work savings are a secondary hypothesis, not a promised result.

## Why this mechanism

The pinned ER11 reference reports confusion between storage-type and ANN-index limits, a false feature-applicability correction, a wrong-domain implementation claim, a price-bundle unit error, and loss of a published default. These are the only historical observations used; no linked case artifacts or assessments were opened. They motivate testing whether a critic can distinguish two nearby propositions using the actual governing evidence. They do not establish incidence, a speed bottleneck, or A8 effectiveness.

The proposed intervention is a **paired source-entailment decision rule**, rather than another verification agent: hold authentic evidence fixed, change exactly one scope selector in a consequential claim, and require separate evidence judgments for both readings before a correction can propagate. For a selected claim C and nearby reading C*, record E=>C and E=>C* independently, each as ENTAILS, CONTRADICTS, or UNDETERMINED. Quote the scope-bearing witness and identify its referent. Never assume one member must be false. C* is a diagnostic hypothesis, never an automatically recommended alternative.

## Executable procedure

1. Run the R0 investigator unchanged. Preserve blind discovery, one-time exact-plan reveal, complete draft, source map, and immutable discovery bytes.
2. The existing independent critic reads the entire ordinary R0 packet. Before fetching new sources or deciding verdicts, select at most six draft claims whose mistaken interpretation would change a recommendation, rejection, exact-plan disposition, or consequential applicability condition. Select by consequence in that order, then draft order; choose only claims with an identifiable scope ambiguity. Save the ordered selection in critique.md. Fewer than six, including zero, is permitted; do not invent risks to fill a quota.
3. For each selection, copy the exact draft sentence and enough surrounding heading/context to preserve its meaning. Resolve the intended subject and conditions from that context. Choose one minimally changed alternative by replacing one selector: subject/subsystem; product/version; quantity denominator/unit; capability/default/requirement modality; or applicability condition/quantifier. Preserve every other factual component. If no meaningful one-selector pair exists, mark INAPPLICABLE, keep the slot, and continue normal R0 review.
4. Reopen authentic primary evidence, using the R0 source handoff as navigation rather than trusting its conclusion. Inspect the governing paragraph, heading/table headers, definitions, conditions, and necessary cross-reference. Record scope-bearing excerpt(s), exact URL/version/locator/access UTC, and a separate relation for each reading. Evidence may contain multiple passages with an explicit referent/condition join; do not truncate until a favored reading appears supported. Do not modify evidence to make a contrast.
5. For a selected C, classify a demonstrated contradiction as an error; classify unsupported or ambiguous evidence as UNDETERMINED. Supporting C* alone is never proof that C is false. Both readings supported may mean the evidence is nondiscriminating. Both unsupported means no established reading. Preserve these outcomes instead of manufacturing certainty. A proposed replacement must itself have applicable support. Conflicting sources remain disputed unless governing version and applicability resolve the conflict.
6. Save no more than six compact witness records within critique.md, alongside the complete ordinary R0 critique. All other claims retain ordinary R0 review; the six-claim cap is not a scope reduction. No extra model call, child, retry loop, deadline, or research pass is authorized by this protocol.
7. The existing fresh complete reviser rechecks the witness against primary evidence and explicitly dispositions every selected record. Retain supported C; repair contradicted C only to supported scope; qualify genuinely unresolved C without asserting absence or discarding compatible options. Preserve the full brief, all plan clauses, discoveries, alternatives, defaults, conditions, user decisions, and proposed-versus-executed validation in the self-contained final. The critic's relation labels remain contestable.

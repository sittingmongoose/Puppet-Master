## Useful unfamiliar approaches with conditions intact

The following findings identify practical opportunities, not empirically validated interventions. Full source versions, governing selectors, access bounds, counterevidence and original implementation/history notes remain in each arm's authored maps and independent assessment evidence.

Do not promote A1, A1+A8, an isolated screen saving, a role substitute, or an instrumentation repair into a qualified production optimization.

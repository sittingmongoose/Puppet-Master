## Product brief — the only substantive starting input
A desktop build client fetches immutable artifacts ranging from 50 MB to 2 GB over unreliable home connections. Users may pause, restart the app, and later resume. The origin may redirect to short-lived signed URLs and may replace an artifact under the same human-facing name. A partial download must never be exposed as a completed artifact; users need an understandable integrity failure. There is no existing design to repair.

Your bounded discovery topic is the resume-and-integrity mechanism for one artifact, not account authentication, storage pricing, UI design, or a complete transfer system. Discover four meaningfully different approaches or variants, their mechanisms and failure boundaries, and inspectable implementation/history leads. Do not assume that transferring fewer bytes proves integrity. Network range support, identity changes, and interrupted finalization matter.

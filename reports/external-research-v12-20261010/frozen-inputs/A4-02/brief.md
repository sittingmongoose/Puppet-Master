# Recurring rehearsal calendar distribution

## Assignment

An amateur orchestra needs a read-only rehearsal calendar usable in common desktop and mobile calendar clients. Events recur weekly, sometimes move to another room or time, and may have one cancelled instance. Members travel across time zones. The secretary edits the source schedule; members should be able to retain their own reminders. The pilot is one season, not an enterprise calendar platform.

You have a 60-minute whole-topic research envelope. Produce a planning proposal, not an implementation. Use public primary documentation, relevant public source code and released issue/fix history where they bear on the proposal. Discover realistic products, mechanisms and alternatives independently; no product, source or conclusion is preselected. A bounded historical example and useful unfamiliar opportunities matter more than an exhaustive catalogue. Clearly distinguish observed external behavior, your inference and a local product choice.

## Exact numbered obligations

1. Explain recurring series, moved or cancelled instances, stable identity and time-zone behavior for the pilot.
2. Compare two distribution products/routes and one analogous publish/subscribe mechanism; distinguish file import, feed subscription and server synchronization.
3. Bind recurrence and refresh claims to the exact client/server operation and versions investigated; identify remaining compatibility uncertainty.
4. Investigate a relevant recurrence/time-zone release or issue/fix chain and its consequences for a season update.
5. Preserve and investigate this supported optional scope: a downloadable one-season calendar snapshot for members who cannot subscribe. It is an authorized option, not a mandatory feature or an established technical capability; recommend conditions, retain it when supported, and explain any evidence-based exclusion.
6. Make owner decisions explicit: The secretary owns event identity and amendments; the orchestra manager decides whether room details are public. Local reminders belong to members and must not be overwritten by policy.
7. Preserve negative constraints: Do not access member calendars, require account migration, promise immediate refresh on every client, or conflate importing a file with maintaining a live subscription.
8. Deliver one coherent evidence-backed research proposal covering obligations 1–8, with recommendations, source/version applicability, useful discoveries, unresolved owner inputs, and a validation table separating checks actually executed from checks merely proposed. Do not report research sources or future tests as executed product validation.

## Research boundaries and deliverable

Keep the investigation at the pilot scale above. No installs, accounts, production access, purchases or live writes are needed. Read-only public retrieval is appropriate; any permitted local discriminating check must be disclosed with its actual inputs and result. A check you did not run remains proposed or NOT_RUN. Deliver a complete proposal with a compact comparison, evidence-linked recommendations and prioritized validation; there is no requirement to build a prototype. Owner inputs may remain explicit decisions, but they do not excuse investigating publicly answerable questions. This brief is the complete discovery input.

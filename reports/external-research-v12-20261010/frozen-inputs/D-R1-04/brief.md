# Uneven runoff entry into a demonstration rain garden

Case ID: ER12-D-R1-04-FRESH
Slot ID: D-R1-04
Domain: urban stormwater research

## Project situation

A neighborhood demonstration rain garden appears to receive water through one curb opening during some storms, while flow passes the opening during others. Volunteers recently added mulch and cleared nearby leaves, and a construction delivery may have compacted part of the edge. The city partner wants a research proposal before considering repairs. There are no surveyed levels, rainfall records, soil tests, or catchment measurements. All site details are hypothetical.

## Request

Use the full 60-minute research window to develop a proposal that distinguishes runoff capture problems from storage or infiltration limitations. Explain mechanisms and useful alternatives, investigate installation and maintenance history, and propose a modest later observation or test program. The deliverable should support discussion with a qualified site partner, not provide a construction or drainage design.

## Requirements to preserve

- Treat entry capture, temporary storage, infiltration, and downstream effects as separate outcomes.
- Compare inlet or surface-routing ideas with maintenance or soil-management investigation and a useful observation-first alternative.
- Consider storm intensity and duration, antecedent wetness, sediment or leaf accumulation, and local geometry rather than attributing the problem solely to compaction.
- Investigate original installation and recent maintenance history and explain the limits of transferring evidence from other rain gardens.
- Propose comparisons across events, feasible measurements, uncertainties, and an escalation or stop condition; distinguish proposed field validation from research or modeling actually executed.

## Boundaries

- Do not excavate, change a curb, redirect runoff, perform infiltration tests, or prescribe final civil-engineering dimensions.
- Do not claim flood protection, pollutant removal, or regulatory compliance from visible ponding or a literature example.
- Do not fabricate rainfall, soil properties, catchment area, grade, or construction damage.

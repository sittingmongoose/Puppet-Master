# I02 — independent review, adjudication, and pilot decision

**Candidate:** ER11 B-02 / treatment / critic (M13)  
**As of:** 2026-10-09 UTC  
**Scope:** The six-month pediatric hearing-device loan pilot in the supplied brief. This is a source-backed product and workflow assessment, not legal advice, a product selection, or a claim that any workflow was configured or tested.

## Recommendation

Do not choose a platform from public pages alone. Run a short synthetic-data vendor screen before the pilot, then select only a product that proves the data, cleaning/check, access, privacy, reminder, outage and accessibility conditions below. Lend Engine is the strongest *lending-workflow demonstration lead* by public price and feature fit, but its current child-data policy, reminder behavior, retention, backup and SSL statements require written clarification. Do not put real child or caregiver information into it before clinic privacy review and written vendor answers. If those answers or the task trial fail, move to a supported configurable platform or an existing hearing-clinic system; do not default to self-hosted or unsupported custom software when the clinic has no systems administrator.

Keep the pilot standalone. Do not transfer full clinical records or build an appointment-system connection in this approved first-year scope. Store only the smallest approved loan facts. Device assignment must be online-authoritative or follow an approved, numbered outage procedure; a stale offline screen is not a reliable shared inventory. Keep synthetic testing and any clinic policy decisions separate from a purchase commitment.

## Required workflow and data boundary

The workflow must distinguish device custody from readiness for reassignment:

`Available → Checked out → Returned / awaiting clean → Cleaning complete → Functional check passed → Available`

A failed or incomplete check remains blocked and is routed to hold/repair. Return alone must never release a device. The checkout/reservation control must consult this readiness state, not merely display a note or open work order. This is the necessary correction to P1: the brief says staff must know that returned devices have been cleaned and checked before reassignment. A named release role, event history with actor/time, and specific state labels are useful implementation controls, but the clinic can choose their exact form after a synthetic trial.

Keep the reusable device record separate from the family loan/fit history. At minimum, each loan must link the device identifier, return date, caregiver contact preference, and any applicable interpreter request. Fitting settings need a clinic-approved record scoped so the next family cannot see the prior family's settings. Whether that is a loan event or a separate clinician-governed fit record, which opaque patient/loan reference is needed, and which staff can view or amend fields are clinical/privacy-owner decisions; this review does not choose them. Never import a full chart to make inventory work. A caregiver contact reference should come from the clinic-approved source, and only the information needed for the reminder/handoff should be carried into the pilot.

Reminders are part of the requested pilot: they must be understandable, follow the recorded caregiver preference, identify the return action/date and clinic contact route, and avoid fitting settings, diagnosis or other unnecessary detail. The brief does not prescribe which communication channels to offer, consent/opt-out handling, or how interpreter support is delivered. The clinic must approve those policies and the vendor workflow. If a system cannot honor the selected preference or show failed delivery, it does not meet the requirement.

The waiting-room workstation must not expose one family's data to the next. Verify individual staff access, the displayed view, lock/reset behavior and what remains after logout, back navigation, reopening, browser cache, downloads, print and notifications. A timeout is one possible control, not proof by itself. Test physical screen visibility from the waiting area as well. On an outage, stop new assignments unless the clinic approves a controlled minimal paper queue with one reconciliation owner; do not treat two disconnected copies as authoritative or release an uninspected return.

## Product alternatives and evidence

- **Lend Engine:** Its USD page lists Plus at USD 25/month and Business at USD 50/month, 10 sites and up to 2,000 items/contacts on Plus, unlimited staff accounts, staff check-in/out prompts and email reminders. Automated backups are listed only on Business. The product page describes accessibility-equipment lending, cleaning/repair routing and configurable prompts, but does not prove a hard block until cleaning *and inspection* pass. Its live policy says the service does not address children under 13 and says it will delete under-13 PII it discovers, with a toy-library exception for child age/date-of-birth data with guardian consent. That policy does not resolve whether staff-entered child/caregiver-linked clinic records are allowed. It says EU hosting, staff access, permanent deletion of inactive accounts after over 60 days without administrator access, and day-before transactional emails; suppressing transactional emails is described as removing the member email. Its policy says Free/Standard accounts are non-SSL while the current price table lists SSL on all plans. These are vendor questions, not legal conclusions; obtain written answers about child-related data, clinic-entered records, hosting/contract, SSL, retention, backups, reminder preference and exit before any identifiable use. [Pricing](https://www.lend-engine.com/pricing?p=usd), [privacy policy](https://www.lend-engine.com/privacy-policy), [loan workflow](https://www.lend-engine.com/features/loan-management-software).
- **Blueprint OMS:** A relevant hearing-clinic lead: the vendor advertises hearing-device inventory, location stock, loaner/trial history, loan agreements and return recall. Public evidence reviewed did not show price, a cleaning gate, child-data suitability, accessibility, or whether a small pilot can run without a broader practice-management deployment. Ask only if already licensed or a small pilot is available; do not assume the clinic has it. [Inventory and loaners](https://blueprintsolutions.us/features/inventory/).
- **AssetTiger:** Low-cost general inventory candidate with return dates, status, maintenance and local offline scan queueing. Public evidence does not establish safe conflict resolution, a transaction-scoped family data model, or task-level accessibility; its VPAT is vendor-authored and is not an end-to-end clinic test. Consider for device-only inventory only if a split loan record is acceptable and reconciliation is owned. [FAQ](https://www.assettiger.com/faq), [VPAT](https://www.assettiger.com/content/vpat_template_2.5rev_wcag_2025.pdf).
- **Hosted Snipe-IT:** Unique asset tags and check-in/out can help central custody; hosted pricing is listed at USD 399.99/year with provider-described maintenance and backups. Reviewed custom fields attach at asset-model level and encrypted fields become unsearchable/unsortable, so the public docs do not establish a safe loan-specific family record. If tested, do not store family fields on a reusable asset unless isolation is demonstrated and approved. [Overview](https://snipe-it.readme.io/docs/overview), [custom fields](https://snipe-it.readme.io/docs/custom-fields), [pricing](https://snipeitapp.com/pricing).
- **Cheqroom:** Its maintenance documentation distinguishes availability flags from operation requests. The official operations page says an open work order does not change item availability; the clinic would need a separate configured unavailable flag and must test who can clear it. Business lists USD 275 per administrator/month for up to three locations; two starting seats would be USD 6,600/year before paid maintenance/operations add-ons, tax or services, whose price is not public on the reviewed page. Get an itemized quote and test the flag/work-order interaction. [Operations requests](https://knowledge.cheqroom.com/helpcenter/operation-templates-overview), [maintenance](https://knowledge.cheqroom.com/helpcenter/how-do-i-perform-maintenance-and-repair-in-cheqroom), [pricing](https://www.cheqroom.com/pricing/).
- **AppSheet:** Can model Device and Loan separately. The current price page lists Core at USD 10/user/month; ten paid users would be USD 1,200/year before any other costs, while some Workspace subscriptions include Core and up to ten test users are listed at no cost. Verify the clinic's actual entitlement. Offline clients cache data and queue changes; the app needs an online initial launch. AppSheet documents last-writer-wins specifically when clients edit the same row, so stale changes to a shared device row can overwrite custody/readiness; this does not make every offline workflow unsafe, but simultaneous two-site allocation must be prevented or tested. A named app owner and support path are still needed. SMS requires a separate Twilio configuration per the vendor help. [Pricing](https://about.appsheet.com/pricing/), [offline and sync](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/10107724?hl=en), [concurrent updates](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/10104702?hl=en), [SMS](https://support.google.com/appsheet/answer/11509435?hl=en).
- **myTurn:** Multi-site lending, barcode checkout and overdue reminders make it a useful quote/demo alternative; the reviewed product page did not establish price, pediatric privacy, access controls, clean/check gating, outage resolution or accessibility. [Product overview](https://myturn.com/home/).
- **Small purpose-built tool / current split lists / paper:** A purpose-built app can encode explicit states and permissions, but adds build, backup, security, accessibility and maintenance work without a systems administrator. The current separate lists are already inadequate for readiness. Keep a controlled numbered paper log only as an outage procedure with minimum details, secure storage and a named reconciliation owner.

All listed prices and live vendor statements are time-sensitive and not quotes or total cost. The review did not establish any candidate as safe or suitable for identifiable clinic records.

## Validation plan: proposals only

1. **Governance/vendor gate:** with synthetic data only, obtain written answers about child/caregiver data, region, contract, backup, retention/deletion, SSL, support and export. Resolve the director's fitting-note authority and identifiable-history retention before any real records. Confirm whether reminder channels/preferences and failed delivery are supported.
2. **Sequential loans:** on one device, create synthetic family A then B. Verify all fit/contact/interpreter information is associated with the approved loan/fit record and that B cannot find A through staff views, search, reports, exports, history or device fields.
3. **Readiness gate:** return a device; immediately attempt reserve/checkout. Verify it stays blocked through cleaning, inspection pending and failed inspection; only a passing inspection makes it available. Separately attempt checkout with an open work order but no availability flag in Cheqroom, or equivalent in the selected tool.
4. **Two sites/outage:** attempt simultaneous use of one device from both sites and test stale-client return/check-out/reconciliation. Verify duplicate assignment is impossible or that the approved pause/paper queue preserves custody and is reconciled before release.
5. **Workstation privacy:** exercise family A, lock/logout/reopen/back, then family B on the actual waiting-room workstation; inspect cache, search/autocomplete, notifications, exports/print, screen positioning and the next-family boundary.
6. **Accessibility:** the staff screen-reader user completes lookup, handoff, return, cleaning/check, failure hold, release and reminder using the real assistive technology and keyboard. Record task success and blockers; check text state, control names/roles/states, focus, forms/errors and relevant status messages without color dependence or unnecessary announcements.
7. **Reminder:** use controlled recipients to verify the caregiver's recorded preference, exact message/preview, no unnecessary clinical/device-detail disclosure, interpreter path as approved, wrong/missing contact handling, bounce/failure visibility and opt-out policy.
8. **Conditional regression:** if Snipe-IT is considered, test maintenance permissions/attachments and allowed site/company history on the currently hosted version. If requestables are used, reproduce #18750's request→checkout→check-in sequence and verify request closure.

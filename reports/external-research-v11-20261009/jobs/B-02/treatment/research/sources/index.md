# I02 retained research evidence index

**As of:** 2026-10-09 UTC. Source IDs and immutable URLs are defined in the adjacent `../source-map.json`. This index preserves the relation, condition, limit, uncertainty, and release-chain graph derived from reviewed public primary sources. The notes are bounded research evidence, not downloaded product packages or trial results.

## Decision graph

- **Clinic obligations → minimum data model.** One reusable device has changing custody/readiness; each family's settings, caregiver contact preference, return date, and optional interpreter request belong to a distinct loan event. The reusable device record should carry only a device identifier and operational state. This separation is a design conclusion from the brief, not a capability verified in any vendor. Lend Engine and Blueprint advertise loan history, but neither source reviewed proves family fields are safely scoped to a loan rather than a reusable device ([SRC-02](../source-map.json), [SRC-04](../source-map.json)). Snipe-IT's reviewed custom fields attach to an asset model; that is not evidence of loan-scoped fields ([SRC-23](../source-map.json)).
- **Return → quarantine → release.** Record return as an event; it must not itself make the unit available. A controlled cleaning/inspection state should block reservations/checkouts until an authorized release. Cheqroom documentation distinguishes availability flags (which may remove an item from availability, if configured) from open work orders (which explicitly do not) ([SRC-09](../source-map.json), [SRC-11](../source-map.json)). Snipe-IT status categories include Undeployable/Pending, which can be used to block reassignment, but the exact clinic workflow and permissions remain to be tested ([SRC-22](../source-map.json)). Lend Engine advertises cleaning/repair routing, without sufficient public evidence about enforcement semantics ([SRC-02](../source-map.json)).
- **Two sites + intermittent outage → safe allocation rule.** AssetTiger says its mobile scanner can queue scans locally for later sync ([SRC-06](../source-map.json)); AppSheet supports offline launch/sync subject to initial-online and browser-loaded constraints ([SRC-14](../source-map.json)). AppSheet documents last-writer-wins when two users change the same row ([SRC-15](../source-map.json)). None of the reviewed sources establishes a collision-safe, authoritative offline handoff across two sites. The pilot therefore needs an online-first rule and a controlled fallback that pauses new assignment or uses a numbered paper queue followed by reconciliation; this is a proposed clinic control, not vendor behavior.
- **Child/family data → vendor and contract gate.** Lend Engine's live, undated privacy policy says its service does not address children under 13 and says it does not knowingly collect their PII, with a toy-library guardian-consent exception; it also says staff users can access member information and describes EU hosting ([SRC-03](../source-map.json)). This creates a material suitability question for a pediatric clinic; obtain written vendor clarification and review applicable contractual/privacy terms before entering child or caregiver information. The reviewed AppSheet security page describes data location, temporary caching, sign-in and customer-configured compliance steps but does not itself establish legal eligibility or a signed agreement ([SRC-16](../source-map.json)). Vendor claims should not be treated as independent certification or legal advice.
- **Shared workstation → account/session design.** Require individual staff identities and least privilege; test reauthentication, inactivity locking, clearing of session data, and the next-family boundary on the waiting-room machine. Lend Engine's price matrix describes unlimited staff/team accounts on Plus but that does not prove a secure configured session ([SRC-01](../source-map.json)). AssetTiger describes roles and audit logs in its FAQ, vendor claims that need a clinic trial ([SRC-06](../source-map.json)).
- **Reminders → preference and minimum disclosure.** Lend Engine states it can send a day-before email with item details and says the account holder can customize the message ([SRC-03](../source-map.json)). AppSheet's documented SMS path uses Twilio; the built-in SMS channel is described as discontinued after 2025-04-21 ([SRC-17](../source-map.json)). Any reminder should follow the caregiver's chosen channel and consent, use plain language, omit fitting/diagnosis/device-setting details, respect interpreter needs and opt-outs, and be tested for delivery and content. The tools' ability to send a message does not demonstrate that the clinic's consent or privacy requirements are met.
- **Screen-reader staff member → task-level accessibility gate.** W3C's color criterion requires another cue besides color; keyboard and name/role/value guidance address input and announcement behavior ([SRC-18](../source-map.json), [SRC-19](../source-map.json), [SRC-20](../source-map.json)). AssetTiger's Jan 2026 VPAT is vendor-authored and says it used WAVE; it reports support for several relevant criteria but is not a task-based evaluation ([SRC-07](../source-map.json)). Test the whole borrow/return/clean-release/reminder task with the staff member and screen reader, keyboard only, and status text independent of color.
- **No sysadmin + $7,500/year → hosted/list-price candidates.** Lend Engine Plus is publicly shown at $25/month ($300/year), Business $50/month ($600/year) with backups only in Business on the matrix ([SRC-01](../source-map.json)). AssetTiger Basic is $220/year at annual billing (500 assets; unlimited paid-plan users); hosted Snipe-IT Basic is $399.99/year with provider-described setup, maintenance, upgrades and backup ([SRC-05](../source-map.json), [SRC-21](../source-map.json)). Cheqroom Business is $275/admin/month, supports up to three locations, and prices maintenance/operations as additional fees ([SRC-08](../source-map.json)); one admin starts at $3,300/year before add-ons, while two would be $6,600/year before them. All are list prices, not total cost or quotes. Blueprint is a conditional option if already licensed; no public price was found in the reviewed page ([SRC-04](../source-map.json)).
- **Six-month pilot; no full migration; later appointment link out of budget → bounded scope.** Prefer a standalone pilot with only a roster/device list and minimum operational loan data. Do not import full clinical records or promise appointment-system integration in year one. A small low-code prototype is possible, but AppSheet Core's public list price is $10/user/month, Core DB is listed at 2,500 rows per database, and SMS needs a separate Twilio configuration ([SRC-13](../source-map.json), [SRC-17](../source-map.json)); ten paid users would be $1,200/year before any external data/SMS costs if not already licensed.

## Evidence boundaries

No product account, trial, configuration, data upload, message, runtime, screen-reader session, offline simulation, or concurrency test was performed. All such work below is a proposed validation. Public vendor price, feature, privacy, and security pages are mutable and may have changed after access; recheck them before purchase. `source-map.json` records access timestamps, locators, version/commit where available, observed operations, and limitations. No usage, billing, or deployment evidence was observed; those fields are null/not observed rather than inferred.

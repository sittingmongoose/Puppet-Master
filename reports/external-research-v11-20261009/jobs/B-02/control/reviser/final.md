# I02 — Complete revised planning recommendation

**Block/arm:** B-02/control · **Method:** M13 v1 · **Stage:** reviser  
**Purpose:** Planning recommendation for a six-month pediatric hearing-device loan pilot. This is not an implemented design, legal opinion, purchase quote, security assessment, or validation report.  
**Evidence boundary:** The research and critic evidence bundles are retained in [`sources/`](sources/index.md); exact source metadata and access records are in [`source-map.json`](source-map.json). No clinic product tenant, runtime, patient data, or staff workflow was available or tested.

## Recommendation

Run a short feasibility check of the clinic’s existing appointment/EHR product first. If it has a supported equipment-loan module covered by the clinic’s current service agreement, that may minimize administration, but only if it can represent a returned device as unavailable until its cleaning and functional check pass, support the minimum-data and access rules below, and pass the staff member’s screen-reader walkthrough. Its product, license, accessibility, privacy terms, and cost are presently unknown.

If no suitable existing module is available, evaluate Power Apps with Dataverse as a conditional low-code candidate for a narrowly scoped loan register. Ten Power Apps Premium users have a published annual-billing list-price subtotal of $2,400 at the rate through 2026-12-31 and $2,640 at the announced rate from 2027-01-01. Both are below the $7,500 annual software ceiling, but neither is a six-month-pilot quote or a total operating-cost estimate. A named clinic maker/owner or funded implementation partner is required; so are exact SKU/tenant and BAA checks, a role and retention plan, support and backup ownership, and successful synthetic workflow and accessibility checks. Microsoft lists Power Apps in the scope of its HIPAA BAA offering, but a service listing or BAA does not establish this clinic’s status, contract, configuration, or compliance ([ER11-S04](sources/research/power-platform.md#er11-s04), [ER11-S06](sources/research/governance-and-safety.md#er11-s06), [ER11-C06](sources/critic/independent-evidence.md#er11-c06)).

Use an online-only browser path for the first digital pilot. Power Apps canvas apps do not run offline in a browser; the documented offline-first canvas path uses Power Apps Mobile and a local SQLite-based cache. Each offline client reads its local copy and queues writes for later sync. The documentation does not establish an exclusive reservation across two disconnected clients. Microsoft’s sync guidance says offline updates are applied by last update to each column, and that the described conflict-resolution settings do not apply to canvas apps. Therefore, do not permit offline assignment from shared inventory based on a stale cached “Ready” value. During outages, either pause assignment or use a clinic-approved numbered paper procedure against physically separate, site-specific device pools; prohibit cross-site stock transfers until reconciliation. If electronic offline assignment is operationally required, make it a separate feasibility gate: preallocate non-overlapping physical device pools by site or implement and prove an appropriate live server-side reservation, then pass the two-client stale-availability test before loading any real data ([ER11-S01](sources/research/power-platform.md#er11-s01), [ER11-S19](sources/research/power-platform.md#er11-s19), [ER11-C09](sources/reviser-independent-evidence.md#er11-c09), [ER11-C11](sources/reviser-independent-evidence.md#er11-c11)).

Do not self-host Snipe-IT or commission a bespoke app for this six-month pilot: both create technical ownership, maintenance, backup, and recovery duties the clinic has said it cannot staff. Snipe-IT is an unfamiliar but useful option for a device-only catalog/status registry, not the family loan workflow. Consider its hosted version only if a separate device-catalog benefit justifies another system and the vendor’s contract, data scope, and privacy terms are acceptable. Do not place child/caregiver linkage, fitting settings, or reminders in it unless the exact service, data model, and any required BAA are verified. If neither an existing supported module nor an accountable low-code owner is available, start with a clinic-approved minimum paper/records-system process and defer purchasing or building until support is funded ([ER11-S13](sources/research/snipe-it.md#er11-s13), [ER11-S14](sources/research/snipe-it.md#er11-s14), [ER11-S15](sources/research/snipe-it.md#er11-s15)).

## Minimum data and workflow shape

Approve a data dictionary before configuration. This is a logical model, not a vendor schema:

- **Device:** clinic device ID, device family/model, current physical site, operational state, and the identifier/revision of the approved cleaning and check procedure. Keep serial number only if needed for safe identification and approved for the intended access scope.
- **Loan:** random internal loan ID, device ID, issue and promised return dates, issuing/receiving staff and site, and only the caregiver contact destination/preference and interpreter request required for handoff or an approved reminder. Include a minimal clinic-held chart reference only if necessary for reliable handoff. Do not import the full appointment record.
- **Fitting handoff snapshot:** the device manufacturer’s parameter names, field types, native units and precision, plus source device/model/software version where needed to interpret them. A clinician confirms the snapshot at issue and handoff. Do not infer, normalize, or convert values without clinical approval. No generic unit/range can be established without the actual device family and fitting software/manual.
- **Lifecycle event:** loan and device IDs, event type, actor, timestamp with a documented clinic time zone, prior/new state, handoff/return condition, cleaning outcome, functional-check outcome, and approved procedure revision. A failed or missing required outcome keeps the device unavailable.
- **Reminder event, if used:** due date, approved contact channel, delivery attempt/result, responsible staff or contracted service, and a minimal approved template. Keep message content only as long as the approved audit/retention policy requires.

Use a local calendar date for the promised return date and unambiguous timestamps for handoff/check events. The clinic’s clinical/device lead must specify each fitting value’s native type, range, units, precision, and display before live settings are entered. Define record- and field-level permissions only after the clinic decides which roles need each category. A random loan ID lowers direct exposure but does not anonymize a record if the clinic can link it to a child.

Recommended state path:

`Ready for issue` → `Prepared` → `Loaned` → `Returned: cleaning/check pending` → `Cleaning recorded` → `Functional check passed` → `Ready for issue`.

A failed check, damage, incomplete cleaning, or uncertain identity moves the unit to a nonassignable `Hold/repair/clinical review` state. The system must block assignment from every not-ready state rather than merely show a warning. Cleaning and functional check are separate recorded outcomes. An authorized staff member records who and when; required outcomes cannot be blank. If leadership chooses an override, require a named authority, reason, attributed event history, and separate validation. Do not add an override by default.

Keep the fitting snapshot that accompanied a handoff. A later change creates a separate attributable amendment and never silently replaces the earlier loan state. This is a clinical safeguard and proposed application requirement; it is not an assertion that Dataverse audit history is immutable. If a suitable retention and audit control cannot be shown for the exact selected configuration, keep the requirement open and reconsider the platform.

## Downtime, privacy, vendors, and retention

For a browser-first register, use a protected, numbered paper packet only if clinic operations approve it. It should record only the minimal active-loan change, loan ID, device ID, operator, site, and local time. Keep fitting details in the approved clinical source unless needed to deliver care. Store packets locked, reconcile each entry online with an authorized person, retain the paper action and reconciliation event under the approved schedule, and prohibit cross-site device movement during an outage. Each site’s paper process must use a non-overlapping device pool so the same device cannot be issued at both sites. Define who resolves duplicate events or conflicting fitting facts. Do not send a reminder while offline unless a separate approved process covers it.

The waiting-room workstation must be treated as visible to families. Require an individual staff identity, no generic shared login, least-necessary views, a tested short idle lock, sign-out at handoff, no patient exports/downloads or saved credentials, and a screen placement/boundary that prevents waiting-room viewing. Confirm this at the actual workstation. For native offline mode, the profile downloads a local SQLite-based cache; the page says it has no automatic expiry while the app and profile remain installed. Cache scope, device ownership, sync-before-revocation, lock, loss response, removal, and pending-edit reconciliation need separate controls. Microsoft documents offline profile restrictions, including the native app requirement, Dataverse table/profile constraints, and no offline cloud-flow execution; setting up the profile requires Environment Maker, System Administrator, or System Customizer privileges ([ER11-S02](sources/research/power-platform.md#er11-s02), [ER11-S03](sources/research/power-platform.md#er11-s03), [ER11-S19](sources/research/power-platform.md#er11-s19), [ER11-C11](sources/reviser-independent-evidence.md#er11-c11)).

The brief’s currency suggests, but does not establish, a U.S. location or HIPAA covered-entity status. Confirm jurisdiction and classification. If the clinic is subject to HIPAA and a cloud vendor maintains ePHI for it, verify the precise product/feature and agreement/BAA, data flow, subprocessors, retention/deletion terms and backups, and complete the clinic’s own risk analysis before live data. Microsoft’s BAA scope page lists Power Apps but says the BAA/service does not itself make the customer compliant. The Snipe-IT public pricing page is silent on BAA scope; that silence does not prove the vendor lacks one, so ask the vendor and keep patient-linked data out until written terms establish suitability ([ER11-S06](sources/research/governance-and-safety.md#er11-s06), [ER11-S07](sources/research/governance-and-safety.md#er11-s07), [ER11-S13](sources/research/snipe-it.md#er11-s13)).

Write a role matrix for eight clinicians and two reception staff, including view/edit/export/action rights, and review it periodically. Apply minimum-necessary guidance if applicable without blocking a clinician’s necessary treatment access. Plan backup, restore, termination, incident response, deletion, and vendor-backup disposition. HIPAA itself does not establish the clinic’s record-retention period; applicable state, professional, minor-record, contract, and legal-hold rules must be identified first. Apply safeguards for as long as data is held and through disposal. Do not select a number of years or a linkage-key destruction date here ([ER11-S08](sources/research/governance-and-safety.md#er11-s08), [ER11-S09](sources/research/governance-and-safety.md#er11-s09)).

## Clinic decisions before live data

1. Confirm jurisdiction, covered-entity status, record classification, and who approves legal/records/privacy requirements.
2. Check the current EHR/appointment product and module, current contract, accessibility, implementation/support cost, and whether it meets the lifecycle gate without integration or bulk import.
3. Name the clinical owner for fitting-note amendments; decide editor, approver, reason, emergency correction, and audit reviewer. Decide whether reception sees setting detail.
4. Approve minimum caregiver contact/interpreter fields, how preferences are obtained and updated, caregiver/child communication authority, reminder language/channel/content, and delivery-failure handling.
5. Approve the cleaning and functional-check procedure for every device family with the clinical/device and infection-control leads; define quarantine and failed-check actions.
6. Decide whether a protected paper outage process is acceptable. If native offline work is required, fund managed devices and define site-specific stock pools, local cache, assignment rules, sync/conflict handling, device loss, revocation, and reconciliation. Shared offline assignment stays blocked until the two-site test passes.
7. Decide retention for identifiable loan history, linkage keys, audit events, paper, and vendor backups only after the applicable rules and contracts are known.
8. Confirm Power Apps license/SKU/tenant, ten-user assumption, named maker/support owner, exact service/BAA terms, configuration and total six-month/annual cost under the $7,500 ceiling.
9. Keep appointment-system integration, automatic import, and bidirectional sync out of year one. Reconsider only through a later scoped funding and security/contract review.
10. Approve a written audit-history control that meets clinical governance needs, including who can delete histories, how long records are retained, what backup/restore preserves, and any residual administrative authority.

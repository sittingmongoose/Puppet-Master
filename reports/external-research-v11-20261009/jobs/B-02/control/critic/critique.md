# Independent critic report — I02, B-02/control

**Method/stage:** M13 v1, independent critic  
**Reviewed:** 2026-10-09 UTC  
**Scope:** exact I02 brief; full own-arm research draft and pre-reveal discovery; that arm’s source map, evidence index, and retained source notes; generated revealed plan; independent public primary-source checks recorded in source-map.json. No product tenant or runtime was available. No validation check was executed.

## Overall assessment

The draft is a coherent, appropriately conditional planning recommendation. Its strongest choices are to check the clinic’s existing supported module first; keep the first-year workflow separate from a full clinical-record migration; preserve an online-only browser path as one candidate while making downtime an explicit clinic decision; treat fitting fields and cleaning instructions as device-specific; and leave governance, retention, contracts, and purchase approval open. I found no factual error that reverses its recommendation.

Two material implementation conditions need sharper treatment before this could become a build or procurement specification: a disconnected local cache cannot establish one current availability state across two locations, and an immutable fitting-note history is a required control to verify rather than an established Power Apps feature. The proposed tests are thoughtful but should directly exercise those failure modes.

## Material findings

### M1 — Offline assignments can conflict across locations

Microsoft’s current mobile offline documentation says the profile is downloaded to a local SQLite cache, which becomes the app’s data source; offline writes queue for later sync. It also says iOS sync runs only in the foreground, while a sync started in the foreground on Android can continue in the background (ER11-C02; retained ER11-S01/S19). That supports the draft’s rejection of browser offline use and its cache-retention warning. It also narrows the draft’s general “foreground/background sync” test: test the chosen operating system and app version, and do not assume background sync behavior is uniform.

The more consequential issue is cross-site allocation. Two disconnected clients can each have a cached “Ready for issue” record for the same physical device. The documentation establishes local reads and queued writes, not a cross-device reservation or transaction lock while clients are disconnected. The draft mentions concurrent conflicting edits, but the risk should be explicit: offline assignment can issue one device twice or overwrite the only authoritative state after reconnection.

**Recommendation:** Keep the browser-plus-controlled-downtime path as the default candidate unless the clinic proves offline assignment is operationally required. If offline assignment is required, define an allocation rule before enabling it: for example, prohibit assigning shared stock while offline, or reserve physically distinct device pools by site and test that boundary. Add a two-site test in which two managed clients go offline with the same device cached as ready and each attempts assignment; demonstrate the approved policy prevents double issue and preserves attributable reconciliation. Test foreground/background and unsynced-device loss separately on every supported OS. This is a condition on offline deployment, not a reason to reject the overall Power Apps feasibility study.

### M2 — Append-only / immutable fitting history is a requirement, not verified platform behavior

The draft’s proposed handoff snapshot, dated amendment, reason, actor, and prior/new values are sound safeguards for the unresolved P5 governance choice. But “immutable audit trail” is a strong technical property. No evidence reviewed establishes that the proposed Power Apps configuration, subscription, privileges, audit settings, retention, and export path prevent an authorized maker or administrator from changing or removing relevant records or audit history. Ordinary form behavior alone cannot establish immutability.

**Recommendation:** Preserve the clinical decision in P5 and retain the append-only design as a requirement. Before live data, verify the exact product/license/configuration’s event and audit behavior, including ordinary users, administrators/makers, deletion, retention expiry, restore, and export. State a guarantee of immutability only if the selected architecture and contract demonstrably enforce it; otherwise specify attributable, access-restricted, retained audit history and document the residual administrative authority. Do not imply that this control already exists in a candidate product.

## Minor refinements and scope checks

- The independent W3C check used the Recommendation itself, which is the normative reference. The retained WAI “Understanding” pages are useful explanations, but not substitutes for the applicable criterion text. An acceptance test should listen for non-focus-changing messages when a loan state, validation result, or sync state changes, as relevant to the selected workflow.
- The annual Power Apps Premium subtotal in the draft is arithmetically correct for ten users at the cited U.S. annual-billing list price: $2,400 before 2027-01-01 and $2,640 at the announced $22 rate from 2027-01-01 (ER11-C06). It is not a complete six-month-pilot or total-cost quote. The draft correctly asks for tenant/SKU confirmation, implementation, training, support, storage, devices, reminders, and recurring operations before any purchase decision.
- The Snipe-IT security chain is accurately bounded by the reviewed primary records: GHSA-qffp-xpqv-gqr4 describes API checkout endpoints and says ordinary web equivalents were not vulnerable; PR #19330 records the undeleted-target validation fix and merge commit 81d0197; the release page lists v8.8.0 at 2c466fa as latest on the reviewed date (ER11-C03–C05). This is useful evidence of version-specific maintenance, not evidence of patient-data disclosure or a reason by itself to reject device-only use.
- The FDA page supports the general caution not to apply solvents, alcohol, or water to hearing aids and to clean as instructed. It does not establish a universal clinic disinfection method; the draft’s device-IFU and infection-control caveat is correct (ER11-C07).
- The draft appropriately treats BAA scope, HIPAA status, caregiver authority, identifiers, retention, reminder contract, and the existing EHR feature as unverified. U.S.-dollar currency alone does not resolve jurisdiction or covered-entity status.

## Validation disposition

All draft checks are explicitly proposed and marked not run. That is accurate. Before any live data, add the two-device/two-location stale-availability test and the platform-specific OS sync/cache-loss checks from M1; test whether amendment/audit history can be altered or removed under realistic user and administrative roles (M2); and retain the draft’s lifecycle, downtime reconciliation, staff screen-reader, workstation privacy, reminder, contract, and total-cost checks. No test result is claimed here.

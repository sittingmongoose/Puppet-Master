# C-01 / H01 — complete research draft

**Stage:** external discovery and plan comparison  
**Research status:** complete for public-source desk research; no product instance or customer data was used.  
**Recommendation:** run a short, quote-gated pilot of a funeral-home case-management product, beginning with a Passare demonstration and written six-staff quote. Keep private family/case details behind a tested staff role matrix; expose only the minimum confirmed logistics needed for coordination. Do not invite relatives into an editable planning area until the owner decides the policy and the vendor demonstrates the exact permissions. Treat outage behavior, export completeness, keyboard operation, translation workflow, and resource conflict handling as pilot acceptance gates. No purchase recommendation is possible yet: pricing and several consequential behaviors remain unverified.

## Case facts and scope

H01 is a six-person, family-owned funeral home serving nearby communities. The owner caps first-year spend at $10,000, wants a private planning aid rather than a public directory, and needs a short pilot for service scheduling and task handoffs. Today, a wall calendar and individual notebooks leave room, vehicle, and officiant confirmation uncertain. Services may change after relatives arrive from different time zones. Staff need role-limited access to family contacts, service preferences, faith/cultural requests, payment arrangements, and personal planning notes. One staff member primarily uses a keyboard; families may need translated information. Severe weather sometimes interrupts internet, most work happens in one office, and an export must be retainable independently. The owner has not decided whether relatives edit a shared schedule, how long notes remain accessible, or which service types use separate workflows. The desired experience is calm and discreet, not a high-volume event system.

This is a scoped pilot decision, not a claim that a product guarantees production-grade security, regulatory compliance, offline conflict resolution, or complete disaster recovery. The evidence below is public vendor documentation, public pricing, public issue/PR/release records, and immutable public code commits. Vendor statements are not independent tests.

## Findings that affect the pilot

### Scheduling and handoffs

Passare documents event-level visibility flags, event settings, participant availability and update notifications. Event types can supply default participant roles and service-specific configuration. Case tasks can have owners, due dates/times, reminders, completion state and comments. This aligns with the coordination problem but does not prove that a room, vehicle, or officiant is reserved exclusively, that simultaneous edits are locked, or that unresolved conflicts are surfaced. A changing service needs explicit states such as proposed, held, confirmed, changed, and cancelled, with a named staff owner and a visible update trail. These states are a proposed pilot configuration to test, not documented Passare guarantees. ([PA02](sources/evidence.md#pa02), [PA03](sources/evidence.md#pa03), [PA09](sources/evidence.md#pa09), [PA11](sources/evidence.md#pa11))

A common view should minimize exposure: case reference; service date/time and time zone; confirmation state; location/room; transport and officiant status; next staff task and owner; last-change timestamp or equivalent. Avoid putting payment arrangements, broad free-form notes, family contact lists, or sensitive faith/cultural details on a broadly shared calendar. Staff may need those details in the protected case record to deliver the service, but necessity and access should be decided field by field.

### Staff and family privacy

Passare describes area-level staff permissions and read-only controls, with examples of limiting financial access and case categories. The public evidence does not show a complete field-level matrix for family contact, preference, faith/cultural, payment and personal notes. The pilot must demonstrate access as each of the six staff roles, including search, notifications, mobile/web behavior, exports and any administrative account. ([PA04](sources/evidence.md#pa04))

Passare Planning Center can expose biographical and service-planning material, files, e-signatures, preferences, checklists and other family-facing features. Invitation is by text or email; the support page states internet is required. The roles are Viewer, Contributor and Editor; Editor is the default and has full Planning Center access, and each case requires at least one Editor. The documentation says scheduled events can appear for family viewing, but it does not establish that relatives edit the staff calendar. Do not accept the Editor default without an explicit role choice, owner approval, and a demonstration of the exact data and actions each role can access. ([PA05](sources/evidence.md#pa05), [PA06](sources/evidence.md#pa06), [PA07](sources/evidence.md#pa07), [PA08](sources/evidence.md#pa08))

For any chosen system, test least privilege with synthetic records for two unrelated families and representative staff roles. Include direct case access, list/search, notifications, exports, shared calendars and mobile views. A restricted view or read-only calendar is not proof that underlying records, related views or exports are private.

### Keyboard operation and translation

Nextcloud's Server 35 manual describes keyboard and assistive-technology support and an aim of WCAG 2.1 AA, including keyboard navigation patterns and themes. That is a vendor accessibility statement, not evidence that Calendar, task ownership, dialogs, search, error recovery or exports work for this staff member. No Passare workflow keyboard test or translation capability was established in reviewed material. A pilot should exercise the entire scheduling and handoff workflow using only a keyboard and actual staff-relevant devices; separately test family-facing translated instructions with a human reviewer for each selected language. Do not assume machine translation, automatic language detection or translated legal/financial terms. ([NC03](sources/evidence.md#nc03))

### Internet outage and independent export

Passare states that family Planning Center use requires internet. The reviewed public sources did not establish an offline staff workflow for Passare. Nextcloud documents differential CalDAV change tokens for offline clients such as Thunderbird and a default retention of 10,000 changes; it warns that too-short retention can create sync trouble. This is not evidence of offline browser use, safe multi-device conflict resolution, or a suitable outage workflow. It is also not a substitute for an independently retained export. ([PA06](sources/evidence.md#pa06), [NC02](sources/evidence.md#nc02))

Passare can export selected case information categories, including event information, payments, and goods/services, as separate CSV files. The reviewed page does not promise that this is a complete case backup or includes all notes, tasks, attachments, permissions, or audit history. Nextcloud offers .ics exports and administrators can disable export buttons, but .ics calendar data is not a complete case export. During the pilot, define the minimum independently retainable data, test actual exports, open them outside the service, and record which categories/attachments/history are absent. If useful, maintain a protected read-only outage snapshot plus a paper reconciliation log with one named coordinator; set access and disposal rules for the snapshot because it still contains personal information. ([PA10](sources/evidence.md#pa10), [NC01](sources/evidence.md#nc01), [NC02](sources/evidence.md#nc02))

### Issue, fix, release and code evolution

Nextcloud Calendar issue #2572 reported an import picker problem in an old Calendar 2.0.4 / Server 19.0.2 context when an imported file mixed event and task components. PR #7876 merged June 4, 2026 and changed the picker to disable unsupported destination calendars with an explanation; the fix clarifies eligibility and does not itself add unsupported component types. Immutable merge commit 02fb072fee39e83746b09582481f63c87505f14b contains the picker/store changes and tests. Calendar 6.6.0 notes dated July 28, 2026 list supported component types closing #2572 and import-picker work. Therefore the chain is an old import-path report, a picker affordance fix, and later release notes for component support; it should not be described as a current defect or evidence of funeral workflow readiness. ([NC04](sources/evidence.md#nc04), [NC05](sources/evidence.md#nc05), [NC06](sources/evidence.md#nc06), [NC07](sources/evidence.md#nc07))

A separate immutable Calendar code commit, ea4d703419d9a7b7fc467071c760d7541af7af14, reads separate create/modify/delete ACL capabilities for calendar sharing. This is useful evidence that a calendar's rights are not necessarily one boolean, but it governs calendar operations and does not establish privacy for case fields. ([NC08](sources/evidence.md#nc08))

## Exact plan-clause dispositions

### P1 — “Coordinate service times, rooms, transportation, and staff task handoffs in one planning view.”

**Disposition: already-covered; optional enhancement; uncertain.** The core coordination requirement is sound and directly addresses the wall-calendar/notebook failure. Passare's documented events and tasks are a promising fit, but the sources do not establish exclusive room/vehicle/officiant reservation, collision detection, confirmation semantics, or safe concurrent change handling. Add confirmation state, change owner, last-change visibility and time-zone clarity as pilot configuration questions, not as assumed product features. Nextcloud requires a resource backend for rooms/resources and separate workflow design; Baserow does not establish reservation behavior. **No correction to the intent.** Validate by creating overlapping room, vehicle and officiant events and simultaneous changes; check whether users see collisions and whether a confirmed change produces a reliable handoff.

### P2 — “Limit family contact, preference, and payment details by staff role.”

**Disposition: already-covered; correction; uncertain.** Keep the requirement. Correct the implementation interpretation: area-level roles, calendar read-only rights, or filtered views must not be treated as proof of field- and record-level least privilege. Passare documents staff area permissions but the full field matrix was not established; Baserow warns that broader table/database access can bypass restricted-view expectations; Nextcloud calendar ACLs do not govern private case details. Require a role-by-field and role-by-action matrix covering contacts, preferences, faith/cultural requests, payments, notes, search, notifications, mobile/web screens and exports. A separate private case area and minimal shared logistics view are optional design safeguards if native controls fail. Validate with synthetic unrelated cases and all six staff roles; verify denied fields cannot leak through lists, links, notifications or exports.

### P3 — “Provide keyboard-operable controls and a way to prepare translated family information.”

**Disposition: already-covered; optional enhancement; uncertain.** Both needs must remain in scope. The optional enhancement is to test a complete staff workflow and real translated family materials rather than accepting a vendor-level accessibility statement or a generic “translation” label. Nextcloud's manual claims keyboard/assistive support and aims at WCAG 2.1 AA, but does not validate this particular workflow. No reviewed source established the complete Passare keyboard or translation behavior. Test scheduling, task handoff, search, dialog/error recovery and export without a pointer; test translated instructions in the languages the family actually needs with a human reviewer. Do not assume automated translation is accurate enough for service, faith/cultural, payment or consent details.

### P4 — “Preserve a usable office workflow during temporary internet outages and support independent export.”

**Disposition: already-covered; correction; optional enhancement; uncertain.** Keep both the outage and export obligations as pilot gates. Correct any assumption that a cloud family portal or CalDAV sync automatically supplies offline continuity or a full backup. Passare says family Planning Center requires internet and no offline staff mode was established. Nextcloud's retained CalDAV change tokens support some offline clients but do not establish offline browser editing or conflict safety. Selected Passare CSV categories and Nextcloud .ics files do not establish a complete, independently restorable case archive. Optionally use a secured read-only snapshot and one named paper reconciliation coordinator during outages. Test an actual internet interruption, capture/resolve changes made during the outage, export defined categories, open the exported files independently, and document missing data and retention handling.

### P5 — “Relative editing access and the treatment of family-approved schedule changes are policy choices.”

**Disposition: user decision; uncertain; already-covered as an open decision.** Research cannot decide family authority. Before enabling family access, the owner should choose among: (a) read-only schedule with all change requests routed to staff; (b) family can submit a correction/change request that a named staff member approves and publishes; or (c) limited direct editing after a product demonstration proves scope, auditability and recovery. Option (b) is a prudent starting workflow if the selected product supports it, but it is a proposal, not a fact about Passare. In Passare, Editor is full-access default and at least one Editor is required; documented family display of events does not prove staff-calendar edit permission. The owner should also decide how a family “approved” change is distinguished from an unverified request, who confirms it across time zones, and how the prior schedule remains recoverable. Until decided, keep relatives out of editable schedule roles and do not interpret an Editor requirement as approval to grant broad access.

### P6 — “Retention of personal planning notes and separate workflows for different service types remain open.”

**Disposition: user decision; uncertain; optional enhancement; already-covered as open questions.** The owner must decide the retention period and which service types require separate workflows. Do not invent a duration or assume that deleting a visible note also removes exported copies, attachments, backups or audit records. Ask the vendor to demonstrate retention/deletion behavior and exports before making the policy. For workflow separation, compare a common case template with separately configured service types; Passare event types have defaults and funeral-specific flags, but that does not establish separate case workflows. Keep only the minimum coordination fields in any shared view. An optional pilot enhancement is to use separate templates/permissions where the owner identifies materially different service steps, while preserving a common logistics view where that reduces duplicate scheduling. Record who owns the taxonomy, exceptions, and any migration of an existing case when its service type changes.

## Explicitly rejected assumptions and unselected options

- Reject making the aid a public directory or presenting the workflow as a high-volume event system; both conflict with the brief's privacy and calm/discreet requirements.
- Reject defaulting family members to broad editing solely because an Editor account is required by one vendor's Planning Center documentation.
- Reject treating a calendar ACL or restricted view as proof of private family case fields.
- Reject treating a CSV or .ics export as a complete restore/retention package without inspecting actual coverage.
- Do not select Nextcloud as a complete funeral case system or Baserow as the default store for private case details on the current evidence. Both remain conditional alternatives if their missing workflow, permission, outage and operating-cost requirements are separately solved.
- No research-supported core requirement is rejected. Several convenience capabilities remain optional; decisions about relative authority, note retention and service taxonomy belong to the owner.

## Discriminating validation plan (proposed, not executed)

1. **Written quote and scope:** obtain a first-year quote for six staff and a short pilot, including setup, training, support, modules, export fees, storage and renewal pricing; confirm the total stays at or below $10,000.
2. **Scheduling conflicts and changing service:** with synthetic cases, book overlapping room, vehicle and officiant slots; change date/time after a family arrives from another time zone; create a simultaneous edit. Observe conflict signal, confirmation status, time-zone display, update notice, owner and recoverable prior state.
3. **Least privilege:** define six staff roles and a field/action matrix. Use two unrelated synthetic families. Attempt direct, search, list, linked, mobile/web, notification and export access to private contacts, preferences, faith/cultural requests, payment arrangements and notes. Record both authorized access and denied leakage.
4. **Family roles:** demonstrate Viewer/Contributor/Editor or equivalent in a test case. Verify default role, full data scope, schedule viewing versus editing, invitations, authentication, revocation, notifications and audit trail. Keep family editing disabled until the owner records a choice.
5. **Keyboard and translation:** ask the keyboard-primary staff member to complete a whole scheduling/task workflow without a pointer. Record blocked controls, focus order, labels and error recovery. Have a human reviewer check sample family instructions in each selected language, including schedule-change wording and sensitive terminology.
6. **Outage and reconciliation:** disconnect internet during a controlled pilot window. Record what each staff role can see/change, whether edits queue, how conflicts appear, and what the coordinator must reconcile. Do not assume queued edits are safe. Check protected local snapshot access and disposal.
7. **Independent export:** export a representative synthetic case, schedule and task handoff; open the files with independent software and confirm required fields, attachments, relationships, history, encoding and time zone. Write down what is omitted, then decide whether the output is adequate for independent retention and recovery.
8. **Notes and workflow policy:** before real data, the owner sets the note retention rule and service-type taxonomy. Demonstrate deletion, export and any retained copies; test a service-type exception and migration. Record who approves changes and who audits exceptions.

**Executed checks:** public source pages and public repository records were opened; the two GitHub commit metadata API responses were queried to confirm full immutable SHAs; the required plan-reveal script was invoked after discovery and source map had been saved; source-map JSON syntax was validated as part of that step.

**Not executed:** no product trial/account, runtime workflow, outage drill, accessibility audit, translation review, role-leak test, pricing quote, export/restore trial, or issue reproduction was performed. The validation list above is proposed work, not claimed results.

## Method and evidence limitations

The assignment's M16 method calls for three fresh contexts: investigator, independent critic and reviser. The assignment also prohibits nested agents. The available native Goal interface exposed Goal lifecycle calls but no separate fresh-context critic/reviser call, so the research and final draft were completed in this context. No independent critic pass is claimed. This is a method deviation and remains an uncertainty in review quality; do not represent it as satisfying an independent review.

All sources and bounded paraphrased evidence are indexed in [sources/index.md](sources/index.md), with exact URLs, source versions/commits, locators, UTC access times and observed operations in [source-map.json](source-map.json). Mutable live vendor documentation, pricing and the main-branch changelog may drift. The full-SHA code commits are immutable. Usage/billing was not observed; the required usage/billing field is null. No source root or predecessor was declared. No external capture or runtime logs were created.

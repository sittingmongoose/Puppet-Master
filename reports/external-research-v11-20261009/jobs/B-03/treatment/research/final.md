## Planning recommendation

For the two-family pilot, preserve existing bench-tag IDs and the shared calendar while adding a phone-friendly way to record tray movements, count observations, readiness forecasts, allocations, and fulfillment outcomes. Keep propagation batch, physical tray/container, location, forecast, verified count, holds, reservations, and fulfillment as distinct linked concepts. An expected readiness window is an estimate. A customer allocation may be shown as confirmed only against an accepted quantity under a nursery-approved rule.

Disconnected phones can capture observations and requests, but cannot know what another disconnected phone has reserved. Mark offline records as saved/pending until reconciliation. Confirming allocations needs one online authority, physically partitioned quotas, or another conflict-safe process the nursery chooses. This is an inference from the wireless constraint and documented sync behavior, not a measured property of a nursery deployment.

No platform is selected. ODK Collect plus Central could be evaluated for offline event capture if devices and hosting fit, with important Entity privacy and type limits. ERPNext v15 is a useful comparator when recorded-stock reservations and fulfillment integration matter, but its reservation voucher is a Sales Order and its stock is actual stock, not expected germination. farmOS offers agricultural data concepts, with current client/server compatibility unverified. A small purpose-built PWA could express the workflow but requires engineering, support, hosting, and cost estimates. Improved paper-first reconciliation remains a viable current-season path and fallback. No quotes, device inventory, deployment estimate, or hosting price were collected; budget fit under $5,500 is unknown.

## Research findings and alternatives

### Data and workflow

Separate plant family/variety, propagation batch, physical tray/tag, location, readiness forecast, count observation, order/allocation, and fulfillment result. Use stable batch and event identifiers. Keep the tag ID on the physical item as a lookup key; if trays are split or combined, preserve lineage rather than reusing an ambiguous identity. A movement event can record from/to, actor, effective time, and event ID. Counts should state their unit and distinguish saleable, held, damaged, or otherwise unavailable plants.

Treat an offline count or movement as an observation until accepted. A retry with the same stable event ID should be idempotent. A later correction should create a correction/superseding record rather than mutate a finalized event. Two conflicting observations should both remain visible with actor/time and a resolution reason; “latest timestamp wins” cannot decide which physical observation is accurate.

Keep expected germination separate from verified saleable stock, safety holds, reservations, and picked quantity. Confirmed allocation should reduce the allocatable balance once. Fulfillment posts actual picked/short/damaged results once, and cancellation restores a reservation only through an auditable event. These are candidate semantics: the nursery still needs to choose bundle sizes, count units, thresholds, count authority, hold policy, reservation rules, and partial-fulfillment behavior.

## Discriminating validations proposed; none executed

1. **Disconnected walkthrough:** With actual target phones in both weak-coverage structures, identify a tagged tray, record a movement and count, close/reopen the app, and reconnect. Verify records survive restart; saved, pending, synced, and failed states are distinct; retries do not duplicate stock effects; and no offline value is presented as confirmed global stock.
2. **Two-device contention:** Begin with ten verified units. While disconnected, request six from device A and six from device B, then reconnect in both orders. The system must not silently confirm twelve; it should preserve both requests with actor/time and show a review/resolution state.
3. **ODK data-boundary/type test, if shortlisted:** Inspect Entity data downloaded to each intended role/device; verify fulfillment PII is absent regardless of form/choice filters. Save representative counts into Entities and exercise validation/conversion and arithmetic explicitly. Check Central/Collect versions and conflict order.
4. **Forecast/cohort test:** Use uneven germination with an early-ready subset. Ensure a split forecast does not create allocatable stock, and only an accepted count affects available quantity. Ask coordinators and seasonal workers whether the state text is understood.
5. **Tag/order/fulfillment reconciliation:** Move a tagged tray between propagation, hardening, and pickup; if normal practice allows, split or combine a cohort; partially fulfill an order. Reconcile tag identity, location, order alias, reserved amount, picked amount, short/damaged count, and remaining stock without double decrement.
6. **Missed-window safety:** Move an order beyond its forecast range. Confirm the workflow requests a decision and does not auto-notify, cancel, substitute, or reallocate. After the nursery chooses policy, test each allowed action and its audit record.
7. **Accessibility and language:** Test status comprehension with the color-blind coordinator and seasonal workers on phone and print views, in color and grayscale. For web content, evaluate WCAG 2.2 SC 1.4.1; record native-app and printed-tag checks as usability results rather than WCAG conformance tests. Identify actual worker languages and validate plain labels.
8. **ERPNext comparator, if shortlisted:** In a qualified sandbox, configure two plant batches and the chosen UoM, then test a household Sales Order and municipal allocation, actual quantity less reservations, partial/excess behavior, batch movement/split, permissions, and negative-stock routes including the documented exception. No source inspection substitutes for transaction behavior.
9. **Field Kit compatibility, if shortlisted:** Record an exact current app release/build and proposed farmOS server version, then verify offline sync, conflict behavior, hosting, SSL, and restart persistence. The docs reviewed do not establish that pairing.
10. **Privacy role test:** Attempt access to customer contacts/addresses through forms, Entities, linked records, search, notifications, shared calendar, reports/exports/print, and offline cache using representative seasonal and fulfillment accounts. Include role changes and device behavior for the chosen platform.
11. **Budget/operations gate:** Inventory devices and support capacity; estimate setup, hosting/services, import, tag work, training, backups, and first-season maintenance. Confirm total cost and coordinator workload against $5,500 before purchase or deployment.

All eleven checks are proposed. No nursery visit, current-device test, installation, product instance, runtime code test, ERP transaction, user study, purchase, or configuration was performed in this research.

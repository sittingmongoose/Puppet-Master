# ER11 external-research campaign — in progress

At the 2026-10-09T20:08:30 UTC snapshot, 48 original logical arms had started and 33 declared finals were delivered. Seventeen paired assessments are terminal: 30 complete outputs received scientific review (nine PASS_WITH_LIMITATIONS, 21 FAIL); four stopped/missing arms have no complete final and null scientific quality. **No repeatable quality-preserving speed or affordability improvement is established.**

Retaining the original investigator has one both-PASS pair: 27.47 versus 38.29 minutes to candidate delivery, a descriptive 28.3% saving, with 37.42 versus 32.08 occupied agent-minutes, a 16.7% increase. Critic science was saved before final science, but the v1 author had no observed frozen-at-read receipt. Both arms took about 58.2 minutes to independently qualified results because the shared paired assessment waited for both candidates. This is a scoped latency lead, not a proven production recipe or subscription saving. A terminal/hash handoff successor is locked and running on a fresh case.

The [brief](BRIEF.md) is the exact assignment with its private account email redacted; original and published hashes are in [PUBLICATION_MANIFEST.json](PUBLICATION_MANIFEST.json). Product canon and main are outside scope. The production target remains 60–75 minutes per topic with two to four agents. The experimental swarm is not a subscriber production recipe.

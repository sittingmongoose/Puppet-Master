# ER11 three-context full-discovery reference

**Status:** a reusable reference recipe, not a qualified universal winner. The recipe preserves the full user brief and exact plan, uses three fresh stage contexts, and targets the practical two-to-four-agent scale. ER11's broader experimental swarm is not this production recipe.

## Recipe

For each topic, the root T3 thread launches three direct child tasks in sequence. Each child creates one real native Goal under 4,000 characters referring to its frozen `assignment.md`. Each stage uses the configured `codex_gmail` / `gpt-6-luna` route with requested `reasoningEffort=max` and `serviceTier=priority`. A child does not launch another worker and receives no parent conversation history; the stage packet and runtime paths carry only the material it needs.

1. **Investigator — 30 minutes maximum.** Read the actual user brief and public primary sources. Discover useful unfamiliar approaches and products, compare evidence, and trace governing behavior, conditions, defaults, exceptions, versions, issue/fix/release history, and applicability. Save substantive `discovery.md` and `source-map.json` before the one-time exact plan reveal. Read the released plan and write a complete `draft.md` with every clause dispositioned. Keep the discovery bytes unchanged after reveal.
2. **Independent critic — 12 minutes maximum.** In a fresh Goal/context, inspect the complete brief, released plan, discovery, draft, and navigable source handoff. Independently check consequential claims against primary evidence. Record material errors/omissions separately from minor locator or wording issues, plus honest unresolved external inputs. Save `critique.md` and its source map; do not repair or author the final.
3. **Fresh complete reviser — 18 minutes maximum.** In a third fresh Goal/context, read the complete frozen investigator package and critic package. Recheck disputed claims, then explicitly accept, amend, reject, or retain uncertainty for each criticism. Write one self-contained `final.md` preserving the original scope, supported findings and conditions, exact plan comparisons, alternatives, optional capabilities, user decisions, uncertainty, and the distinction between proposed and executed validation. Save the final and source map before native Goal completion.

The 30/12/18 budgets sit inside one absolute 60-minute envelope. Each timestamp is frozen before its accepted dispatch, so startup, queueing, source work, handoffs, retries, writing, and delivery consume the declared time. A retry reuses the original request and deadline; it does not reset a clock. The existing ER11 selection rules protect a writing/delivery reserve and require stopping substantive work at expiry. Delivery time, additive occupied agent work, and time to independently assessed result remain distinct measurements.

## Exact prompt source and input boundary

`prepare.py` is the prompt source of truth. It freezes the stage `assignment.md`, `input-map.json`, `freeze.json`, and T3 `request.json` before dispatch. The short `bootstrap.js` task points the child to that assignment; the complete stage wording is stored there. The prompts require exact source identity (URL, released version or commit, locator, access UTC, observed operation, conditions/applicability), source navigation, immutable source IDs, and a final that carries the evidence rather than relying on IDs. Source caches and outputs stay under the caller's dedicated `runtime_root`.

The investigator's initial packet contains the brief but no plan bytes. The one-use `reveal.py` helper refuses to act until `discovery.md` is at least 500 bytes and `source-map.json` exists. It copies the exact configured plan bytes into `revealed-plan.md` and records SHA-256 hashes of both discovery and plan. Later stages verify those hashes. The investigator may then use the exact plan for comparison, but may not rewrite the frozen discovery.

After each task reaches T3 `completed` with `hasPendingChildRuns=false`, the root rechecks the complete required files. Before dispatching the next context, it freezes the predecessor file paths, byte counts, and SHA-256 values in `freeze.json`; a changed predecessor blocks dispatch. Critic completion and the frozen critic/source-map bytes are prerequisites for the reviser. The child must save all required science before its native Goal reaches terminal state. T3 task termination alone does not prove native Goal completion; keep direct native Goal activation/terminal evidence separate, and report missing native provenance or timestamps as `UNKNOWN`.

## Full-source assessment versus operational qualification

A full-scope source `PASS` or `PASS_WITH_LIMITATIONS` means an independent assessor reviewed the complete delivered candidate against the original brief and exact plan across six axes: original obligations; consequential facts/defaults/conditions/applicability; useful discovery and alternatives; false corrections/rejections and plan comparisons; draft/critique/final preservation; proposed versus executed validation. The judgment is based on primary-source applicability and preserved substance, not citations, output length, agreement, hashes, or a successful script check. A pass with limitations permits minor issues but no remaining material error, material omission, or unsupported consequential decision within the declared scope. An honestly unresolved external input is acceptable only after the required research was exhausted within the original scope.

Track separate qualifications for: native Goal lifecycle/provenance; requested versus effective provider/model/effort/service tier; frozen predecessor delivery; candidate final delivery time; occupied work; time to independently assessed result; and billing/usage. A source pass does not fill those fields. Supported host observations show actual active and complete native Goal states for all five M05-B product contexts. Native exact terminal timestamps remain unknown. The overall v1 method qualification remains unknown because the retained author has no frozen-at-read receipt; individual observed native fields are not erased by that uncertainty. Billing/subscription and campaign token totals were unavailable. Supported provider context snapshots expose input/cache/output/reasoning fields where present; their accumulation semantics are unqualified. Neither those snapshots nor native cumulative counters are summed across overlapping fields/sessions. Do not infer dollars from tokens, affordability from latency alone, or universal reliability from a small number of passes. The reference has not been shown to always pass.

## Observed reference cohort

The [exact cohort and measurements](OBSERVED_REFERENCE.json) retain all ten completed Luna controls with this three-context topology across screening and B-01/B-02/B-03: eight full-source PASS_WITH_LIMITATIONS and two FAIL. M01 controls used four contexts and are excluded by topology. All ten delivered, but only 5 of the eight source passes also met every whole-arm/stage/occupied time ceiling. This sample supports a scoped pilot; it does not establish general reliability, subscription affordability, or a qualified optimization. Provider/case differences prevent attributing this cohort comparison to model alone. The two matching failures are [A-M02-B/control](../evaluations/A-M02-B/assessment.md), which falsely characterized documented Volgistics opening recurrence as unestablished, and [A-M11-B/control](../evaluations/A-M11-B/assessment.md), which confused the pgvector vector type dimensional limit with its ANN-index limit. Both failures remain in the ten-attempt denominator.

## Scope and limits

This three-context workflow is the usable reference candidate for a topic-scale pilot. ER11's experiment itself spans many paired cases and provider pools; its swarm, setup, queue, and evaluation costs cannot be presented as the production recipe or hidden as free preparation. The root product target remains roughly 60–75 minutes per topic with about two to four agents. The three stage budgets fit that target on paper; actual end-to-end time, occupied work, quality, effective settings, native lifecycle, and billing must be measured on fresh cases before claiming that it fits in practice.

The 12-minute critic allowance was not reliably sufficient in observed ER11 runs, especially retained-author/evidence-first variants. A source pass does not make an overrun time-eligible. The later 75-minute Muse/Luna bundle used 30/20/25 stage ceilings prospectively; both development treatments failed source assessment, so it is retired. The final M15 development contrast adds a separate independent factual verifier within the same 75-minute target, at most two simultaneous and four total contexts; it is still under test and cannot yet replace this reference. The packaged 60-minute reference should be used as a measured pilot with explicit failure/overrun recording, not a guaranteed service time. The launcher enforces the current T3 minimum of 6 GB available before each new worker.

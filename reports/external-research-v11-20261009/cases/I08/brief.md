# I08

A school district's band program lends instruments and coordinates repairs for students at nine schools. Two music staff and a part-time repair coordinator manage the pool, while local teachers record urgent issues during class. The program can spend $8,000 this year and must keep the workflow usable during summer when the district help desk is closed. Records need an instrument identifier, school, loan status, repair notes, expected return date, and a way to contact a caregiver when an instrument is delayed. Student details must be limited because many teachers can see the schedule, and the district wants to avoid storing grades or disciplinary data. Some teachers use large-print screens; a family liaison needs messages available in two languages. The present binders make it hard to tell whether a repair is waiting for parts or ready for pickup. Staff want to begin with a single inventory room and a few schools, then expand only if the handoffs are clear. They have not decided who can approve a replacement instrument or whether caregivers should be able to report a problem directly. Existing district sign-in requirements may apply, but a formal integration request has not been funded.

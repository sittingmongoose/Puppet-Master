# H01

A family-owned funeral home coordinates services, transportation, room setup, and memorial materials for several nearby communities. Six staff share duties, and a service can change after relatives arrive from different time zones. The owner has a $10,000 ceiling for the first year and wants a private planning aid rather than a public directory. Family contact details, service preferences, faith or cultural requests, and payment arrangements should not be visible to every staff member by default. Staff currently use a wall calendar and individual notebooks, creating uncertainty about which room, vehicle, or officiant has been confirmed. Families may need translated information, and the home has a staff member who navigates primarily by keyboard. It sometimes loses internet during severe weather, though most work is done from one office. The owner wants a short pilot covering service scheduling and task handoffs, with an export that can be retained independently. The family has not decided whether relatives may edit a shared schedule, how long personal planning notes remain accessible, or which services should be handled as separate workflows. They want the process to feel calm and discreet, not like a high-volume event system.

O1 Independently discover useful unfamiliar tools, products and materially different approaches beyond the thin plan.
O2 Investigate consequential primary source/code behavior and governing defaults, units/types, limits and applicability for selected mechanisms.
O3 Investigate at least one relevant issue/fix/regression/release or evolution chain; say when evidence is absent/inapplicable.
O4 Compare every exact P clause after plan reveal; distinguish correction, optional enhancement, user decision, already-covered, rejected and uncertain findings.
O5 Retain useful alternatives, conditions, original constraints, disagreement and uncertainty in one self-contained coherent final; do not replace text with IDs.
O6 Propose meaningful discriminating validations and separate executed checks from proposed work. No runtime available is honest; do not pretend proposals ran. Scope is this small product brief, not unlimited production guarantees.

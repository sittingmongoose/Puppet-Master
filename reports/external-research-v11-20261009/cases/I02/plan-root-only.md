# Plan root

P1: Track each loan through preparation, assignment, return, cleaning, and availability for reassignment.
P2: Associate loan records with the minimum patient and caregiver details needed for contact and handoff.
P3: Provide accessible status controls, including screen-reader labels and cues that do not depend on color.
P4: Support plain-language reminders and a staff view that limits exposure of other families' records.
P5: The role allowed to amend fitting notes after reassignment is a clinical governance decision.
P6: Identifiable loan-history retention and any appointment-system connection remain open scope choices.

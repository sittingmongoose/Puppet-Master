**N2-F02: The open-checkout index has no specified release path in an immutable event journal**

Candidate: `/home/sittingmongoose/PM-Experiments/er11-20261009-116bb1e4/jobs/A-M10-B/control/reviser/final.md`, lines 100, 114–121 and 176–184.

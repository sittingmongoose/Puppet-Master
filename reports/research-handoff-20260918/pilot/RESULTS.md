# Discovery-to-Plan pilot — final bounded results

**Status: bounded pilot complete.** All four attempts are closed and independently evaluated. Terminal/premium stopped after the repeated-report/assignment problem was demonstrated; both Usage arms exhausted the same prospective comparison budget. Two historical terminal finding groups and three later workflow amendments landed in live PM Plans; one accounting-boundary amendment landed in a separate synthetic Usage derivative. These are distinct categories, not six new discoveries. Original research cohorts remain partly unreviewed, and new product choices remain unapproved.

The practical result is a reusable, operator-reviewed prototype that has carried external research through actual specification amendments. The experiment also exposed lost child evidence, repeated shared reports, incomplete comparisons and an overstated delivery claim. The stable entrypoint fixes the observed transport defects in offline scheduler checks and real-artifact replay; it has not received a new native model run. Both Usage arms reach 7.5/8 partial controls, but substantive comparison covers only 21/38 premium and 7/14 hybrid original records. A high control score is not complete research coverage or approval to build.

## Verdict

**Fix first.** The four corrections are, in substance, right: each repairs the adjudicated defect, cites the right
passage, and — with one exception — adds nothing beyond it. The schema work is careful (the pre-init fence is a
field-for-field copy, the `else` branch genuinely closes the widening, the narrow scoping of the continuation rule is
deliberate), the fixtures fail for the intended reasons, nothing pre-existing changed meaning, the derived files
regenerate byte-identically, and the currentness claim holds under independent hashing. Two things must change before
this lands.

Required fixes:

1. **B1** — Resolve the 600-reference `parent_refs` bound as an owner decision rather than a derivation. Either remove
   `"maxItems": 600` from `Plans/source_control_contracts.schema.json:2837` and carry the finite-parent-policy
   obligation as an owner question, or keep the bound and say in `Plans/Source_Control_System.md:1080-1083`, in the
   bundle README, and in the ledger's open items that 600 is a provisional bound pending owner specification —
   dropping the claim that it follows from the edge cap. Either way, add it to the questions for Jared; the authorized
   follow-up does not cover it.
2. **B2** — Correct
   `reports/jujutsu-research-2026-09-11/continuation3-landing/evidence-receipts.json:13-14`: `premium-J0021.json` and
   `premium-J0019.json` do not support F108. Cite the real chain (`independent-review.json#/candidates/2` plus the
   out-of-repository J0026-compare captures it records), and extend the `out_of_repository_evidence` note at `:18` —
   and its mirror at `source_shards/findings.md:97` — to say F108's direct job is out-of-repository too.

Recommended with the same commit (cheap, and they close the gaps a later reader would trip on): **S1** a negative
fixture for the parent bound if it survives; **S2** the partial-enforcement clause in `cor-002`/`dec-002`; **S3** the
open question as a ledger question record. **N1** (real timestamps) if the author is touching the ledger anyway.

table is overstated.

# Matched pilot protocol — fixed before v3 participant jobs

Date: 2026-09-08 UTC. This is a bounded process pilot, not a model ranking or exhaustive audit.

## Question and conditions

Can broad external discovery, followed by independent implementation/history research, technical reconciliation and Plan comparison, preserve useful findings and produce concrete Plan repairs or reviewable product proposals? Compare Astra XHIGH at every stage with DeepSeek V4 Flash MAX discovery/studies followed by Astra XHIGH reconciliation and comparison. Supporting evaluator and implementation work primarily uses Astra Medium; it is outside participant-arm cost totals.

Use the same frozen input case and protocol in each matched pair, one observation per arm per case. Cases: current terminal Plans (309 readable files; five current fixtures explicitly unavailable) and a separate synthetic early Usage plan (185 words). The corpus already includes the two manually landed September correction groups; credit detecting their existing coverage, not claiming them as new gaps. Participants receive a product brief during external research and the Plan corpus only during comparison. Conclusions from other arms and evaluator answers are not deliberately supplied. Workspaces are cooperative path isolation, not an adversarial filesystem security boundary.

## Resource policy

V3 per-job native runtime allowance: 360 seconds. Deep-study admission budget per campaign: 180 seconds, beginning after discovery. Each admitted independent implementation/history pair may finish; two study workers maximum. Discovery and subsequent review have their own per-job allowance and are outside the study-admission budget. Review batches use text volume, not a project, mechanism or finding-count ceiling. Retain pending leads and missing outputs explicitly. Follow-up discoveries may require another pass. One expensive campaign at a time. There is no claim that a campaign is wall-clock bounded by 180 seconds.

Save lead notes and reports progressively. A native Goal's completion, a nonempty file or a lead-ID marker does not establish semantic correctness. Failed and partial attempts remain part of the outcome and cost record. V1 warmup used a different protocol and 900-second jobs; it remains separate. V2 was initialized but not launched and is not counted as a failed model observation.

## Evaluation

Predeclared thin-plan omission key: eight semantic controls in `evaluator/usage-known-omissions.json`, with interpretation in `evaluator/usage-review-rubric.md`. Primary report: full/half/missed counts and semantic points out of eight. Secondary severity score: critical weight 2, major weight 1, denominator 12. This is partial ground truth; validate distinct useful discoveries outside it separately without changing the denominator. The mature terminal case has no invented complete-recall denominator; apply `evaluator/terminal-review-rubric.md` and cite actual owner/consumer passages.

Evaluate discovery breadth, factual correctness, useful findings reaching comparison, genuine gaps versus covered behavior, concrete approval handling and actual amendment evidence. Count duplicate expressions once for semantic findings while preserving their lead IDs. Unsupported consequential claims, materially false gap claims and lost findings are distinct failures. Compare corresponding delivered stages before interpreting cost or quality differences. A zero or partial result under this resource policy is an observed outcome, not a rerun silently replaced by a favorable result.

New features and consequential product choices remain pending an explicit user decision after a concrete proposal is available. Existing-contract repairs may use the current bounded authorization. A finding is landed only when actual owner/consumer amendments and verification exist. Synthetic fixture amendments are separate from live canonical changes. The two historical manual repairs are evidence of useful prior findings, not evidence that the new workflow succeeds.

## Measurement and limitations

Use `collect.py` and preserve native valuations, request receipts, hashes, failed attempts and pending IDs. Report observed execution wall span separately from summed job durations. Keep Astra conditional API equivalence, Codex credits and DeepSeek Go allowance value separate; actual billed cash and unobserved search/controller/setup costs are unknown. Rates and conditions are in `PRICING_BASIS.md`.

The protocol can reveal practical errors and useful findings. One run per condition, partial controls, asymmetric prior source-cache warming, shared public acquisition pinning and incomplete current fixtures limit generalization. No broad matrix, retired Qwen run, July backlog revival or governance seal is included.

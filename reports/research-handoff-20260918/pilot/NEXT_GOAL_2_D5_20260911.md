Reconcile each completed request's usage promptly. Usage that cannot be reconciled stays visible as unresolved; it is neither dropped nor silently charged against the cap. - **Fallback where no average exists.** Neither arm completed reconciliation or comparison, so those stages have no observed per-job cost. Until two jobs of a stage have reconciled usage in that arm, the in-flight allowance for a job of that stage is a fixed $12 on the preserved basis, which is above the highest per-job average seen in any completed stage of either arm. A job cancelled that way is recorded as budget-truncated, not failed.

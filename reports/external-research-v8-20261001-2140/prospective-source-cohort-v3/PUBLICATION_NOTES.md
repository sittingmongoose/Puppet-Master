This is a positive-list source and metadata projection, with 100 original files, their root handoff, and these publication notes. It includes frozen planning inputs, independent source-review tests/results, durable continuation source and immutable/adopted root metadata, and the original plus repaired standalone public checker. No generated candidate proposals, candidate critic/correction bodies, semantic evaluator keys, auth, private profiles, proprietary SDK source, native events/stdout/stderr, or raw web/stream/trace capture bodies are published.

Original and public SHA-256 values are distinct. Host paths are replaced by LAB_ROOT, WORKTREE_ROOT or USER_HOME references; native sess_ handles are consistently pseudonymized and internal execution/thread identifiers omitted from JSON metadata. Embedded original hashes remain original-runtime provenance, not hashes of sanitized public files. Unselected dependency paths and hashes describe omitted original-runtime dependencies and do not expand this positive list.

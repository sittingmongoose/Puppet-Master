**C2 — "cell_type must match at every predicate level" is false at the `by_ids` level. [CORR, V-SRC + counterexample]**
Q2 Stage B states pairing "must match on `cell_type` (nbdime requires this at every predicate level [SRC])". At v4.0.4, `compare_cell_approximate/moderate/strict` each check `cell_type`, but `compare_cell_by_ids` does not: its entire body is `'id' in x and 'id' in y and x['id'] == y['id']` (`nbdime/diffing/notebooks.py`). Counterexample: `{id:"abc", cell_type:"code"}` and `{id:"abc", cell_type:"markdown"}` compare **equal** under the first alignment pass. Consequence for the prototype: Stage A same-id pairing is likewise cell_type-blind, and the proposal's reported category list (source/outputs/metadata/execution_count/attachments/id) has **no `cell_type` category** — a same-id code→markdown flip would go unreported. Fix: add a `cell_type` change category (or declare its exclusion explicitly in N2 and fixture F3).

**C3 — The §6 witness tally is internally inconsistent; it also contradicts F2. [CORR, INFER on verified mechanics]**
Witness interpretation (I) computes LCS of `[a,b,c,d]` vs `[c,d,a,b]` = 2 and then asserts "**2 structural ops, 0 source patches, 4 position changes**". These cannot coexist: with only 2 cells retained by the LCS, exactly 2 cells are matched and both change index ⇒ **2 position changes**, not 4. "4 position changes" is obtainable only under full id-pairing of all 4 equal-id cells — under which structural ops are **0** (nothing needs removerange/addrange). The LCS=2 derivation itself is correct (any 3-element common subsequence would need equal relative order in both lists; A-order a<b<c<d and B-order c<d<a<b are opposite, so 2 is maximal) — and it does match the verified nbdime mechanics (`snakes.py`: `compute_snakes_multilevel` starts at `level = len(compares)-1`; `compute_diff_from_snakes` emits `removerange`/`addrange` for gaps) [V-SRC]. But the stated total mixes the two models, and the falsification criterion — "any observed count outside {0 edits + 2 structural ops} under (I) falsifies the id-first implementation" — would brand F2's own expected outcome ("id-paired; 0 source edits; positions changed", i.e. 0 structural ops) a falsification. Fix: state one representation consistently — (a) full id-pairing: 0 structural ops, 4 position changes, 0 source edits; or (b) ordered snakes alignment: snake length 2, 1 removerange + 1 addrange, **2** position changes, 0 source patches. Either way the witness still discriminates: identity-aware counting yields **0 source edits** where positional counting (interpretation II, correctly derived) yields 4 changed cells, so the example does discriminate its stated assumption. Arithmetic remains UNEXECUTED here and in the proposal; no execution receipt exists on either side.

## Coverage and method

Both stock generator runs passed all currentness checks with `event_authority_closed=false`. The recount separately scanned **all 96 direct Plans Markdown files**, taking every dotted token even outside cue windows and backticks, and structurally parsed **425 JSON files**: every direct `Plans/*.json` (including all schema documents) and every JSON under `Plans/event_payloads/`. Semantic keys are discovered by the case-insensitive pattern `event|emit|receipt.*effect`, covering `event_name`, `event_family_ids`, `receipt_or_event_effect`, `receipt_or_event_refs`, positive/negative/compatibility event lists, event tests and `x-event-type`, as well as all original keys. String, array and const/enum bindings are scanned for every dotted token, including tokens within effect descriptions; event/payload schema titles are also scanned. This covers production Wiring Matrix emit/receipt lists. Every generator occurrence was also included, regardless of its mechanical class. L9's 158 rows were re-located and rechecked, not substituted for the new scan.

Keys with dotted bindings in this snapshot: `admitted_event_types`, `canonical_event_type`, `compatibility_event_type`, `event_family_ids`, `event_id`, `event_name`, `event_policy`, `event_record`, `event_refs`, `event_schema_id`, `event_test_requirements`, `event_type`, `event_types`, `excluded_event_types`, `expected_event_id_preimage`, `expected_event_types`, `expected_events`, `home_workspace_layout_event_command_id`, `receipt_or_event_effect`, `receipt_or_event_refs`, `superseded_events`, `typed_events`, `x-event-type`.

The prose scan has no sampling, cue-window cutoff or skipped ambiguous tail. Generated shards/indexes, migration runs, ledgers, audits and validation outputs do not originate product authority. Generator-inherited machine inputs remain discoverable; the five readiness event_type bindings are retained as **undetermined unresolved evidence**, outside NEW-LIST and all candidate buckets. They are not excluded, registered or merged. Canonical required-owner candidates remain candidates even while an admission fence prevents emission. By contrast, PKT-04 inventories explicitly marked canonical=false and deferred_non_emitting_event_candidate are classified not_persisted_by_owner_text. Invalid-fixture-only names are negative oracles. Full structural parsing is not a claim of full-document semantic reading.

All 21 likely, 5 contested and 219 ambiguous generator names outside August and registry are accounted for (245 total). CLASSIFIED.jsonl contains one sorted row per examined exact token, its current `path:start-end` citations, discovery sources, occurrence count, and any unchanged historical membership. SKIPPED.jsonl is the explicit subset handled by mechanical classes; every skipped name and its rule are listed. Command/control IDs are classified individually by namespace or exact current binding, rather than silently discarded.
